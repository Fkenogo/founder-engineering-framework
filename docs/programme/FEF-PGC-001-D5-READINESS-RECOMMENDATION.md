## 3. Conditions for D5 Entry

No conditions are required beyond the existing Master Programme requirement that D5 commence only through a separately governed entry sequence.

Specifically, D5 shall not commence until:

1. the Master Programme authorises D5 mobilisation;
2. a D5 mobilisation record is prepared and validated;
3. D5 Review Questions are admitted through DG-2;
4. D5 Evidence Pack is frozen through DG-3.

## 4. What D5 Shall Not Do

D5 shall not:

- redesign the FEF lifecycle;
- create new registers;
- amend D1–D4 artefacts;
- perform Framework Evolution;
- amend constitutional instruments;
- commence D6.

## 5. Success Criteria for D5

D5 will be successful if it:

- follows the established DG-1 → DG-6 lifecycle;
- produces one-to-one traceability chains;
- preserves evidence boundaries and frozen baselines;
- records Founder wording verbatim;
- maintains non-independent validation disclosure;
- closes only after complete reconciliation.

## 6. Non-Effects

This recommendation:

- does not amend D1–D4 artefacts;
- does not create new registers, governance domains, or lifecycle stages;
- does not perform Framework Evolution;
- does not amend constitutional instruments;
- does not commence D5 or D6;
- does not issue Founder Decisions;
- does not restructure the repository.
