## 1. Purpose

Define the complete operational approach for executing the new Founder Governance Review in accordance with the approved Charter and Agenda.

This Plan converts the approved strategic architecture into:

- preparation stages;
- dependency-aware domain operations;
- indicative session structures;
- evidence-pack controls;
- entry and exit gates;
- validation checkpoints;
- risk and quality controls;
- completion and final-approval preparation.

This Plan does not commence the review or create a review identifier, session, Review Question, Governance Finding, Founder Decision, Constitutional Candidate, evidence pack, or Open Question disposition.

## 2. Authority and Plan Boundary

### 2.1 Authority

FEF-WPK-001G is authorised to prepare this operational Plan only. The Charter and Agenda were approved by FD-2026-07-24-001 and FD-2026-07-24-002.

### 2.2 Plan Approval Boundary

This Plan becomes operational only after:

1. Founder disposition of FEF-FGRP-001;
2. resolution or acceptance of all Plan conditions;
3. Master Programme synchronisation;
4. a separate Founder commencement decision;
5. assignment of the review identifier;
6. satisfaction of the review entry gate.

Plan approval and review commencement are separate decisions.

### 2.3 Prohibited Plan Effects

The Plan shall not:

- assign the review identifier;
- schedule actual sessions or dates;
- select participants;
- instantiate Review Questions;
- instantiate Governance Issues;
- assemble evidence packs;
- create GFs, FDs, or Constitutional Candidates;
- close Open Questions;
- modify the Charter or Agenda;
- amend the Constitution or FEF-RGS-000;
- authorise downstream work.

## 3. Operational Review Objectives

The review operation shall:

1. execute all eight approved Agenda domains within Charter controls;
2. admit and preserve evidence before substantive analysis;
3. prepare bounded Review Questions without predetermining answers;
4. conduct traceable review sessions only after session entry validation;
5. separate discussion, analytical issues, GFs, recommendations, and FDs;
6. preserve exact Founder decisions contemporaneously;
7. map all 23 existing Open Questions without automatic closure;
8. identify but not enact Constitutional Candidates;
9. validate each output at the required lifecycle checkpoint;
10. assemble a complete review record for final Founder disposition.

## 4. Operational Scope

### 4.1 Included Operations

- review initiation preparation;
- review identifier allocation procedure;
- role assignment;
- Review Question admission;
- evidence registration and evidence-pack preparation;
- domain examination;
- session entry, execution, recording, and validation;
- GF drafting and lifecycle validation during future execution;
- FD question preparation, exact recording, and validation during future execution;
- cross-domain integration;
- Open Question mapping;
- Constitutional Candidate identification under Charter tests;
- deferred-matter management;
- final review assembly and validation;
- Founder completion-disposition preparation.

### 4.2 Excluded Operations

- reconstructing the historical review;
- constitutional drafting or amendment;
- RGS amendment or approval;
- engineering design or discovery;
- programme implementation beyond the review;
- creating another governance standard;
- exercising Founder decision authority;
- commencing any activity before its gate.

## 7. Operational Sequencing

### 7.1 Preparation Stage

Before review execution:

1. obtain Founder approval of this Plan;
2. update the Master Programme;
3. prepare the separate commencement package;
4. assign the review identifier through Founder decision;
5. assign roles;
6. establish empty controlled registers;
7. fingerprint the 23-question Open Questions baseline;
8. identify candidate evidence sources without admitting them;
9. prepare and validate the initial Review Question set;
10. assemble and freeze the first evidence pack;
11. pass the review and first-session entry gates.

None of these steps is executed by the Plan document itself.

### 7.2 Domain Execution Stage

The operational order shall follow:

```text
D1 Authority
   ↓
D2 Evidence
   ↓
D3 Assurance
   ↓
D4 Records
   ↓
D5 Lifecycle
   ↓
D6 Administration
   ↓
D7 Constitutional Boundary
   ↓
D8 Final Open Question Synthesis
```

D8 mapping operates across every domain. Controlled iteration may return an unresolved dependency to an earlier domain under the Execution Rules.

### 7.3 Integration Stage

After each domain:

- validate domain coverage;
- reconcile cross-domain terminology;
- update traceability without changing source records;
- register deferrals and unresolved dependencies;
- confirm whether later-domain entry criteria are satisfied;
- preserve any reopening trigger.

### 7.4 Completion Stage

After D8:

1. assemble the final review record;
2. reconcile evidence, RQs, sessions, GFs, FDs, Open Questions, candidates, and deferrals;
3. validate counts, identifiers, links, authority, and status;
4. produce the final validation report;
5. prepare the Founder final-review disposition package;
6. stop pending Founder decision.

## 8. Indicative Session Structure

This section defines a generic future session structure, not actual sessions.

### 8.1 Session Preparation

- confirm the domain and bounded objectives;
- admit Review Questions through their lifecycle gate;
- assemble the evidence pack;
- freeze and fingerprint the pack;
- identify roles and independence constraints;
- disclose dependencies, risks, conflicts, and unavailable evidence;
- complete the session entry checklist.

### 8.2 Session Opening

- confirm Charter, Agenda, Plan, review, and session metadata;
- confirm Founder authority and participant roles;
- confirm evidence-pack version;
- confirm in-scope RQs and exclusions;
- record conflicts, conditions, or stop issues.

### 8.3 Evidence Examination

- examine evidence by RQ;
- identify corroboration and contradiction;
- preserve uncertainty and access limitations;
- distinguish evidence from advisory analysis.

### 8.4 Governance Analysis

- discuss governance implications;
- record alternative interpretations;
- identify potential Governance Issues only if a later approved control defines them;
- draft candidate GFs only during authorised execution;
- identify decision needs without selecting decisions.

### 8.5 Founder Decision Gate

- present validated evidence and GF inputs;
- present decision question and options;
- record the Founder’s exact disposition, or record that no decision was issued;
- preserve conditions, rationale, deferral, or risk acceptance.

### 8.6 Session Close

- review outputs and non-outputs;
- identify deferred matters and actions;
- confirm Open Question mapping effects;
- record entry dependencies for later work;
- complete recorder attestation;
- stop substantive reliance pending post-session validation.

## 9. Evidence-Pack Strategy

### 9.1 Pack Layers

The future review should use:

- **Review Baseline Pack:** approved instruments, authority record, current programme context, protected historical boundary, and Open Questions baseline;
- **Domain Pack:** evidence common to a domain;
- **Session Pack:** bounded evidence for admitted RQs in one future session;
- **Supplemental Pack:** controlled evidence admitted after a freeze;
- **Decision Extract:** exact evidence and GFs used at an FD gate.

### 9.2 Pack Controls

Every pack shall comply with the [Evidence Pack Specification](FEF-EPS-001-EVIDENCE-PACK-SPECIFICATION.md) and shall:

- have a unique future identifier;
- contain a manifest;
- cite Evidence Records rather than unregistered sources;
- record admissibility and limitations;
- be versioned;
- be frozen before use;
- carry an integrity manifest;
- preserve updates without overwriting prior versions;
- map every item to RQs and sessions.

No evidence pack is assembled by this Plan.

## 12. Session and Domain Exit Gates

### 12.1 Session Exit Gate

A future session exits substantive work only when:

- the Session Record is complete;
- exact decisions, non-decisions, and deferrals are recorded;
- evidence and RQ references resolve;
- candidate outputs have correct lifecycle status;
- post-session validation is complete;
- unresolved defects are named.

### 12.2 Domain Exit Gate

A domain exits only when:

- planned domain coverage is complete or attributable deferral exists;
- all domain RQs are dispositioned;
- evidence and output traceability is validated;
- terminology conflicts are reconciled or deferred;
- Open Question mappings are current;
- downstream entry dependencies are explicit.

### 12.3 Review Exit Gate

The review reaches the final Founder gate only when all Charter completion criteria are met and the final validation package is ready.

## 15. Quality Assurance Approach

### 15.1 Quality Principles

- evidence completeness is assessed, not presumed;
- authoritative wording is preserved exactly;
- templates support consistency without deciding content;
- every artefact has a defined lifecycle;
- every gate produces a recorded outcome;
- validation is separate from Founder authority;
- defects remain visible until corrected or accepted;
- no output advances merely because a session ended.

### 15.2 Quality Metrics

The future Review Plan execution should report:

- percentage of admitted sources with complete Evidence Records;
- percentage of RQs with evidence mapping;
- GF validation status distribution;
- FD recording and validation completeness;
- Open Question mapping coverage;
- unresolved link and identifier defects;
- deferred matters with attributable treatment;
- session and domain gate outcomes;
- final Charter completion-criteria coverage.

Metrics measure record quality and completeness. They do not score Founder decisions or pressure the Founder to issue decisions.

### 15.3 Quality Defect Classes

| Severity | Meaning | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Unsupported authority, missing attributable decision, evidence integrity failure, historical reconstruction, or premature constitutional effect | Stop affected review activity |
| Major | Missing traceability, unresolved contradiction, incomplete mandatory field, or invalid lifecycle transition | Return artefact; block reliance |
| Minor | Formatting, navigation, or non-substantive consistency defect | Correct before final validation |
| Observation | Improvement opportunity without current control failure | Record for later consideration |

## 16. Reopening and Iteration Strategy

Reopening a future RQ, session conclusion, GF, domain outcome, or evidence pack shall require:

- a named trigger;
- authority to reopen;
- affected artefact identifiers;
- new or corrected evidence;
- impact assessment;
- preserved prior state;
- revalidation scope;
- downstream notification.

Iteration may move backward in the domain sequence, but it may not erase prior records or silently change an FD. Founder Decisions change only through Founder authority.

## 17. Escalation and Stop Conditions

Immediate escalation is required for:

- ambiguous Founder authority;
- evidence authenticity or integrity concerns;
- confidentiality or legal constraints;
- conflict between approved instruments;
- inability to record exact Founder wording;
- a proposed Open Question closure without FD support;
- attempted historical reconstruction;
- proposed Constitution or RGS amendment inside the review;
- uncontrolled scope expansion;
- critical validation failure.

Until resolved, affected activity shall stop or be bounded away from the defect.

## 18. Review Completion Strategy

### 18.1 Progressive Completion

Completion evidence shall be accumulated per session and domain rather than reconstructed at the end.

### 18.2 Final Assembly

The final package shall include every artefact required by Charter Section 22, complete cross-record traceability, validation outcomes, deferred matters, and Founder-only disposition fields.

### 18.3 Final Readiness

The review is ready for Founder completion disposition only when:

- all Charter completion criteria are satisfied or conditions are explicit;
- the final record is integrity-fingerprinted;
- all 23 existing Open Questions have traceable statuses;
- no unresolved Critical quality defect remains;
- constitutional candidates remain candidates;
- downstream recommendations are separated from authority.

### 18.4 Post-Review Boundary

After final review disposition, constitutional consolidation, RGS amendment, and Engineering Discovery remain separately authorised downstream work.
