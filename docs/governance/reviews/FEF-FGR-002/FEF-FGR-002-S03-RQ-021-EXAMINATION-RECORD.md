### 2.2 Supported Observations

1. The mapped evidence demonstrates a complete before-and-after assurance
   cycle for one specific Founder gate (D3-G1): a neutral, non-recommending
   package (EV-060) assembled from separately validated inputs, followed
   by a verbatim disposition-capture record (EV-061), followed by an
   exact-source comparison and admission record (EV-062) that explicitly
   distinguishes the Founder's own authorising input from any operational
   capacity's assertion. All three stages are evidenced for the same
   decision instance.
2. For the six D2 Founder Decisions (FD-011 through FD-016), the mapped
   set evidences only the **after** side directly: the issued records
   themselves (EV-066–071) and a separate post-decision validation pass
   (EV-056) confirming exact wording and condition preservation. The
   neutral pre-disposition package used to prepare those six dispositions
   is not among RQ-021's mapped items (it is mapped to other RQs), so the
   **before** side for this specific decision instance is not directly
   evidenced within RQ-021's own mapped set.
3. EV-018's §2 statement — that a Founder's substantive decision is "not
   validated for correctness," only the record's "exactness, authority
   source, conditions, boundaries, and traceability" — is the mapped
   set's clearest statement of the boundary RQ-021 itself asks about:
   assurance over the record, categorically apart from evaluation of the
   judgement it records.
4. FD-010's own text supplies a rare example of a decision record stating
   a downstream-reliance precondition (§2.1: a dependent domain "shall
   not commence until" specified steps pass) and, within the same
   document, later reporting that the precondition was met (§3: "passed
   on 2026-07-25"). This is the mapped set's only instance where a
   decision record's own downstream-reliance gate is both stated and
   self-reported as satisfied.
5. EV-021's Role Assignment Register and EV-017/EV-018's structural rules
   supply the general operating scaffold (capacity labelling, mandatory
   separation, disclosed combinations) within which both the D3-G1 cycle
   and the D2 FD cycle operate; neither structural document records an
   operated instance itself.

### 2.3 Unsupported Assertions (explicitly excluded)

- That the Founder's substantive judgement in either cycle (D3-G1 Accept
  dispositions, or D2 Accept-with-Conditions dispositions) was correct,
  well-founded, or evaluated for quality. RQ-021's own declared exclusion
  bars judgement assessment, and no mapped item is read as such an
  assessment.
- That EV-060's and EV-061's own claims of neutrality and non-influence
  ("did not recommend, suggest, or otherwise influence any disposition")
  are independently verified. Both records were prepared by the same
  non-independent operational capacity that prepared the underlying
  candidates; the claim is stated, not corroborated by a separate party
  within the mapped set.
- That downstream implementation was actually carried out as a result of
  either cycle. FD-010 §3 states what "may" commence once its conditions
  pass and reports that they passed; the mapped set does not include a
  separate, independent record confirming that the authorised downstream
  activity (e.g., D2 mobilisation) in fact occurred as described — those
  records are not mapped to RQ-021.
- That a single worked example (D3-G1) generalises into a complete,
  FEF-wide model for pre- and post-Founder-disposition assurance. RQ-021's
  own declared exclusions bar creating implementation authority or D5/D6
  architecture from this examination.
- That the recurring Non-Effects and Conditions sections across FD-010
  through FD-016 prove those conditions were subsequently honoured beyond
  what each record's own later text states about itself.

### 2.4 Uncertainty

Whether the single complete before-and-after cycle evidenced for D3-G1
reflects a deliberately designed, repeatable assurance sequence or a
one-off practice specific to that gate cannot be determined from the
mapped evidence; no mapped item states whether the same three-stage
structure (neutral package, verbatim capture, exact-comparison admission)
is intended to recur for future Founder gates, including any future D3
Founder Decision.
