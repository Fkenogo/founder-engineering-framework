## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What minimum, proportionate administrative obligations, practices, or
> reporting, if any, should apply uniformly across Founder projects that
> adopt the FEF to preserve trust and governance integrity, and what
> should instead remain project-specific to preserve project autonomy
> and governance efficiency, consistent with Minimum Viable
> Administration?

This finding uses only EV-006 (restricted to Strategic Objective SO-6
and the "Over-governance" row of §11) and EV-086 (restricted to its
"Role of This Document" framing and Executive Summary structure, at its
frozen post-D6-DG-2 state), as frozen for RQ-042 in EP-006 v1.0/MAN-001.
No unmapped evidence or later live administrative version was used. No
FEF review machinery is made mandatory project governance by this
finding, and no specific reporting format is adopted.

## 2. Finding

The mapped evidence establishes that proportionality and simplicity were
named as review-design intent from the outset: FEF-FGRA-001's Strategic
Objective SO-6 commits the review to determining "the minimum
identifiers, classifications, taxonomies, registers, and administrative
controls needed for consistent operation," and its Strategic Risks and
Controls table names "Over-governance" as a risk controlled by a named
Simplicity principle. Separately, the Founder Dashboard (EV-086)
currently and actually operates as a minimal, consumer-only reporting
artefact — it states of itself that it "does not independently maintain,
assert, or duplicate programme state" and is "deliberately short." This
is more than documented intent — it is a real, currently-operating
example of the kind of minimum, proportionate reporting practice RQ-042
asks about.

The evidence does not establish more than this. Applying the
documented-intent / administrative-capability / demonstrated-operation /
verified-operation distinction: what exists is a stated design intent
(EV-006) and one demonstrated instance of minimal reporting in actual
operation (EV-086); what is **not** evidenced is any minimum
administrative obligation, practice, or reporting requirement determined
to apply uniformly across Founder projects, or any criterion for what
should instead remain project-specific — no such standard exists in
either mapped source, and no second adopting Founder project currently
exists against which one could be tested. Whether the Simplicity
principle named in EV-006 is an operative Framework administrative
standard or an unoperationalised review-design aspiration, and whether
EV-086's minimal-reporting pattern is intended as a cross-project
template or is specific to this Framework's own internal governance
documentation, are both recorded as genuinely uncertain, not resolved by
inference in either direction.

## 3. Evidence Gaps and Unsupported Matters

| Matter | Finding treatment |
|---|---|
| Minimum administrative obligation, practice, or reporting standard for uniform cross-project application | Not evidenced |
| Criterion distinguishing common versus project-specific administration | Not evidenced |
| Formal adoption or operationalisation of the Simplicity principle as an enforceable standard | Not evidenced |
| Testing of any proportionality standard against a real second adopting Founder project | Not possible; no second adopting project exists |

These gaps are part of the answer at finding level; they are not
silently filled by inference.

## 4. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source directly contradicts another. Two qualifications
prevent overstatement:

- EV-006 is a review-process document governing how FEF-FGR-002 itself
  is conducted; its Simplicity principle and SO-6 objective could inform
  Framework-wide administrative design or could apply only to this
  review's own conduct — the mapped evidence does not resolve which
  reading is correct; and
- EV-086's minimal-reporting demonstration is one document's design
  choice within one project's internal governance and must not be read
  as proof of a repeatable, cross-project reporting standard without
  further evidence.

## 5. Limitations, Uncertainty, and Open Questions

- Confidence is high that proportionality and simplicity were named as
  review-design intent (EV-006) and that at least one controlled
  document currently operates as a minimal, non-duplicating reporting
  artefact (EV-086).
- No positive or negative confidence is assigned to whether the
  Simplicity principle is an operative standard or an unoperationalised
  aspiration — this is recorded as genuinely uncertain.
- No positive or negative confidence is assigned to whether EV-086's
  pattern is intended as a cross-project template — this is recorded as
  genuinely uncertain.
- No positive confidence is assigned to any minimum cross-project
  administrative standard or common/project-specific boundary, because
  neither is evidenced to exist at any level.
- No Open Question maps directly to RQ-042. This finding does not create
  one.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and
  validation; validation is non-independent.

## 6. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The evidence does not decide what minimum administrative obligations,
practices, or reporting should apply uniformly across Founder projects,
nor what should remain project-specific. Those remain matters for
separately governed Founder judgement or later authorised work,
properly informed by experience once a second adopting Founder project
exists. This finding makes no recommendation.

## 7. Scope and Confidence

**Confidence: High for the bounded observation that proportionality and
simplicity were named as review-design intent and that one document
demonstrates minimal reporting in actual current operation; not
applicable to any cross-project minimum-administration standard, because
none is evidenced to assess and no second adopting project exists;
genuinely inconclusive for whether the Simplicity principle is operative
or aspirational, and whether the demonstrated pattern is a cross-project
template.** The directly quoted text in EV-006 and EV-086 strongly
supports the bounded conclusion. The absence of any adopted standard or
second adopting project is the direct and sufficient basis for the
negative findings in §3.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-042 and the two mapped frozen records.
It does not decide RQ-038 through RQ-041 (already answered in GF-037,
GF-038, GF-039, and GF-040), or RQ-043.

## 8. Non-Effects

This finding does not adopt or recommend any cross-project
administrative standard, reporting format, or proportionality rule. It
does not modify frozen evidence or evidence treatment, make any FEF
review machinery mandatory project governance, judge any project's
existing practice, examine RQ-043, issue a Founder disposition or
Founder Decision, close S06, or commence D7. It remains Presented and
pending Founder disposition.
