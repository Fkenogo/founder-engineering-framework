## 2. Phase 2 — Evidence Examination

### 2.2 Supported Observations

1. The mapped evidence shows at least four **distinct outcome vocabularies**
   operating at different levels: item-level evidentiary admissibility
   (EV-022: Conditionally Admitted), artefact-level check verdicts (bare
   Pass / Pass with Conditions, recurring across EV-025, EV-028, EV-050,
   EV-053, EV-054), gate-specific outcomes (EV-062: Admit), and
   domain-lifecycle outcomes (EV-057: Closed). RQ-019's question — what
   distinct outcomes are needed — is answered, within this mapped
   evidence, by these four observed levels, not by a single uniform
   vocabulary.
2. The **consequence control** that recurs across every level is the same:
   an explicit, per-record "Non-Effects" or "Gate Non-Effects" clause
   stating what the outcome does *not* do (does not approve, does not
   activate, does not exercise Founder authority, does not commence the
   next stage). This is evidenced as the operating mechanism by which
   validation outcomes are kept distinct from approval/activation/Founder
   authority, rather than a single named "consequence control" concept.
3. EV-059 supplies the mapped set's only operated example of
   **correction**, and its mechanism has three identifiable components:
   (a) the original claim is preserved and remains visible, not deleted;
   (b) the correction cites the specific controlling authority the
   original claim contradicted; (c) the correction does not invent a
   Founder decision to justify itself. EV-058 supplies a second,
   different consequence path for a defective outcome — quarantine and
   restoration rather than in-place correction — suggesting more than one
   consequence mechanism is evidenced for handling an incorrect prior
   outcome, and the mapped evidence does not state when one applies
   rather than the other.
4. EV-062's five numbered conditions attached to "Admit" show that
   **conditional reliance** is operated by listing forward-looking
   restrictions on what the outcome permits next (e.g., "no RQ may be
   assigned to a session until evidence mobilisation, pack freeze, and
   session-entry controls pass"), rather than by a separate named
   "conditional" outcome distinct from "Admit."

### 2.3 Unsupported Assertions (explicitly excluded)

- That **blocking**, being **returned**, being **failed**, being
  **reopened**, or being **superseded** have been operated as distinct
  validation outcomes anywhere in this RQ's mapped evidence. RQ-019's own
  declared evidence need lists these as items to look for; none is
  present in the mapped set (see §4, Gap Assessment).
- That the two different consequence paths observed for a defective prior
  outcome (EV-059's in-place correction versus EV-058's quarantine) are
  governed by a stated rule choosing between them. No such rule is
  present in the mapped evidence; this finding does not infer one.
- That a "final vocabulary" or "universal lifecycle map" of validation
  outcomes can be stated from this mapped evidence. This is RQ-019's own
  declared exclusion (D3-RQS-002 §5).
- That any mapped outcome grants approval, activation, or Founder
  authority. Every mapped record that reaches an outcome explicitly
  denies this in its own text (§2.1); no mapped record is read against
  that explicit denial.

### 2.4 Uncertainty

Whether "conditional reliance" and "correction" are treated in this
review as the same mechanism (a conditioned outcome that may later be
corrected) or as two separate mechanisms cannot be determined from the
mapped evidence; EV-059's correction did not itself operate through the
numbered-conditions pattern EV-062 uses, and no mapped record states
whether that difference is intentional.
