## 1. Finding Reference

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-036 — Specific Legacy Labels and Recovery-Preservation Practices Are Evidenced, but No General Classification, Reliance, or Migration Rule Is Established**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

**Founder Observations**

Legacy material should be governed when it affects current authority or
delivery—not catalogued simply because it exists.

**Discussion**

The Founder considered GF-036 and its authorised lean conditions. No additional
discussion is attributed.

**Disposition**

Accept with Conditions

**Conditions**

Common conditions applying to GF-031 through GF-036:

1. Acceptance confirms the finding, not a proposed solution.
2. Any future FEF control must remain a minimum cross-project foundation.
3. Project-specific lifecycle detail remains under project authority unless a
   universal requirement is demonstrated.
4. Founder-reserved authority cannot be delegated by implication.
5. Existing valid work and historical records must not be retrospectively
   altered without explicit authority.
6. No repository-wide reconciliation, renumbering, reclassification or
   migration programme is authorised.
7. Implementation design requires separately governed follow-up after Founder
   Decision issuance.
8. All eight applicable Open Questions remain open unless separately resolved.

Finding-specific Conditions:

1. Classification is required only where legacy material may affect current
   authority, reliance, traceability, active decisions, ongoing delivery, audit,
   legal obligations or evidence preservation.
2. Classification must not retrospectively approve or invalidate material.
3. Ordinary obsolete drafts do not require controlled legacy classification.
4. No universal legacy taxonomy is adopted through this disposition.
5. No repository-wide inventory, reclassification or migration programme is
   authorised.

**Rationale**

A risk-based approach protects current governance without turning historical
housekeeping into a programme of its own.

**Follow-up Actions**

Develop proportionate legacy-treatment criteria only after Founder Decision
issuance.

## 3. Exact Finding Scope

**Confidence: High for the item-specific labels, non-effects, and recovery
facts; insufficient for a general classification, reliance, or migration
rule.** Exact acquisition objects support the bounded observations. Their
different purposes, the Context Only limitation, and the absence of a general
retention or authority rule prevent generalisation.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-037 and the six mapped frozen records. It
does not classify or change a legacy artefact, reopen GF-001, decide authority
or validity, prepare the Founder Review Package, close S05/D5, or commence
D6/D7.

## 4. Exact Finding Non-Effects

This finding does not adopt, recommend, or apply a legacy taxonomy,
classification, inventory, migration, relabelling, numbering, retention,
archival, validity, authority, effectiveness, or reliance model; validate or
