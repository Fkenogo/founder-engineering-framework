## 1. Decision Question

What attributable disposition and enduring treatment applies to
FEF-FGR-002-GF-012?

## 2. Exact Validated Finding

> A frozen Evidence Pack is an immutable, versioned examination baseline rather
> than a truth determination. Material additions, removals, replacements,
> admissibility changes, or integrity changes require preservation of the prior
> pack, an attributable successor or Supplemental Pack, impact assessment,
> revalidation, re-freeze, and explicit session linkage before reliance.

## 3. Exact Founder Disposition and Decision

**Disposition: Accept with Conditions**

Accept that a frozen Evidence Pack is an immutable and versioned record of the
evidence baseline examined.

A frozen pack:

- shall not be mutated in place;
- is not a truth determination;
- shall retain its original identity, version, manifest, integrity evidence,
  limitations, and session linkage.

A material addition, removal, replacement, admissibility change, or integrity
change requires:

- preservation of the prior pack;
- an attributable successor or Supplemental Pack mechanism;
- impact assessment;
- renewed validation;
- renewed freeze;
- explicit linkage to affected sessions and outputs before reliance.

The immutable-freeze principle is accepted now.

Detailed Supplemental Pack, successor-version, reopening, supersession, and
framework-wide lifecycle mechanics remain provisional until operated and later
examined under the applicable D5 and related controls.

This disposition does not generally approve FEF-EPS-001 as a Framework
standard.

## 4. Decision Treatment

The immutable-freeze principle and minimum attributable change controls are
accepted. Detailed mechanisms remain provisional and must not be represented
as operated or as a settled framework-wide lifecycle.

## 5. Rationale, Conditions, and Limitations

No separate Founder rationale was supplied. The disposition itself supplies
the controlling conditions and provisional boundary. No Supplemental Pack or
successor pack has been operated, and FEF-EPS-001 retains its draft-status
header.
