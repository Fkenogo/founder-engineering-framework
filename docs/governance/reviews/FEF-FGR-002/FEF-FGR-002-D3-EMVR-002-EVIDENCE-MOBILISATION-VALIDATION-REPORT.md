## Revalidation Note (v1.0 → v1.1)

Version 1.0 of this report identified the RQ-020/RQ-021 Founder-Decision
standalone-record limitation as an open gap and used the phrase
"independently checks (within the disclosed non-independence limitation)"
in Section 1, which risked being read as a claim of independent validation.
Version 1.1 corrects both: FD-010 through FD-016 are now individually
registered as EV-065–071 (Section 3), and Section 1's language is corrected
to state plainly that this is a separate pass by the same non-independent
capacity, not an independent one. The RQ-018 gap and the EV-058/EV-059
contradictions are unchanged and remain open by design.

## 1. Scope of This Validation

A separate validation pass was performed by the same combined acting
capacity that prepared FEF-FGR-002-D3-ERC-002 and FEF-FGR-002-D3-EQR-002,
with non-independence explicitly disclosed (see the control-field table
above). This report checks the completeness, traceability, admissibility,
limitations, and identifier integrity of the D3 evidence mobilisation
baseline produced in FEF-FGR-002-D3-ERC-002 (requirements and catalogue) and
FEF-FGR-002-D3-EQR-002 (qualification), and the resulting Evidence Register
v1.5 update — including the FD-010 through FD-016 direct-evidence
registration (EV-065–071) performed to close the RQ-020/RQ-021 gap
identified in v1.0 of this report. It assesses readiness for the next gate.
It does not examine evidence, answer a Review Question, or freeze an
Evidence Pack.

## 8. Verdict

**Pass with Conditions.**

Conditions:

1. The RQ-018 dissent/challenge evidentiary gap must remain visible to
   whoever next assesses Evidence Pack readiness; it may not be silently
   closed, reconstructed, or inferred away.
2. EV-058 and EV-059's preserved contradictions must not be resolved by
   inference; any resolution requires substantive examination, which this
   report does not perform.
3. The newly registered FD-010–016 evidence (EV-065–071) must continue to
   be read as evidence of the Founder's decisions, not as evidence that the
   validation process surrounding those decisions was adequate; that
   remains separate, unexamined territory.
4. This validation is a separate pass performed by the same combined
   acting capacity that prepared the requirements and qualification; it is
   not independent. A genuinely independent revalidation remains a future
   objective, consistent with FEF-GER-D3-001 CE1/CE2 (both still undecided
   by the Founder).
5. No Evidence Pack freeze (DG-3), session entry (DG-4), examination, or
   Governance Finding may rely on this baseline until a separate,
   explicitly authorised gate performs that step.
