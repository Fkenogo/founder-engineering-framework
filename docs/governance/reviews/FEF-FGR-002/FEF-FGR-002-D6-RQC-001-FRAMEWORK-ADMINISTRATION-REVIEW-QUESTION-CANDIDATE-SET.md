## 1. Purpose and Authority

This record prepares the smallest complete and separately examinable set of
neutral D6 Review Question candidates supported by:

- the bounded scope in
  [FEF-FGR-002-D6-MPP-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-MOBILISATION-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md)
  (v1.1, Mobilised — Effective);
- the Founder's Approve with Conditions mobilisation disposition in
  [FEF-FGR-002-D6-FMAR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-FOUNDER-MOBILISATION-AUTHORISATION-RECORD.md);
- the validated engineering blueprint in
  [FEF-FGR-002-D6-EA-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-EXAMINATION-ARCHITECTURE.md); and
- the Founder's Approve with Minor Administrative Observation disposition of
  the D6 Founder Review Package in
  [FEF-FGR-002-D6-FDR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-FOUNDER-DISPOSITION-RECORD.md),
  which Confirmed all five examination themes and explicitly authorised this
  preparation activity (Decision Agenda Item 5).

Preparation is governed by the Founder-approved
[Charter](../FEF-FGRC-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-CHARTER.md) (D6
definition), [Agenda](../FEF-FGRA-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-AGENDA.md)
§6.6, and [Plan](../FEF-FGRP-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-PLAN.md) §6/§7,
the controlled [Execution Rules](../FEF-FGRER-001-REVIEW-EXECUTION-RULES.md),
and [FEF-RQS-001](../FEF-RQS-001-REVIEW-QUESTION-SPECIFICATION.md).

The temporary references `D6-RQC-01` through `D6-RQC-06` are drafting aids
only. They are not canonical RQ identifiers, do not instantiate canonical RQ
lifecycle entries, and are not entered in the Review Question Register.

This preparation stops at the **Founder Candidate Review boundary**: it does
not allocate canonical Review Question identifiers, admit Review Questions,
identify or qualify evidence, prepare an Evidence Pack, commence
examination, create Governance Findings, create Founder Decisions, amend
constitutional content, perform Framework Evolution, or resolve any Open
Question — consistent with all eight Founder mobilisation conditions
recorded in FEF-FGR-002-D6-FMAR-001 §2 and reaffirmed unchanged in
FEF-FGR-002-D6-FDR-001.

The objective of this preparation is **to identify the correct questions
that should later be examined within Domain D6**, not to improve the
Framework, recommend administrative solutions, or determine that any
observed pattern already is Framework Administration practice.

## 2. Preparation Method

The Founder-approved examination themes in
[FEF-FGR-002-D6-MPP-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-MOBILISATION-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md) §4
and confirmed in
[FEF-FGR-002-D6-FRP-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-FOUNDER-REVIEW-PACKAGE.md) §3
identify five examination themes: (1) administrative ownership and
stewardship; (2) maintenance and current-state integrity; (3) roadmap,
release, and dependency administration; (4) identifier, register, and
document relationships; (5) simplicity, reporting, and tailoring. The
Founder's most recent guidance additionally organises D6's subject matter
into five illustrative areas — Programme Administration, Repository
Administration, Register Administration, Framework Administration, and
Documentation Architecture — and separately raises one specific
administrative subject for consideration: whether "every controlled
document should have one clearly defined administrative responsibility"
(the principle demonstrated by the Single Source of Truth architecture)
deserves its own Review Question.

The Review Analyst:

1. treated the five approved examination themes as the primary coverage
   structure, consistent with the Founder-confirmed FEF-FGR-002-D6-FRP-001
   §3, rather than as a target question count;
2. mapped the Founder's five illustrative subject areas onto the five
   approved themes rather than treating them as a sixth, separate
   examination structure — Programme Administration and Register
   Administration map primarily to themes 1–2 and 4; Repository
   Administration maps primarily to themes 1 and 4; Framework
   Administration maps across all five themes as the domain's overall
   subject; Documentation Architecture is addressed separately below;
3. prepared one candidate per approved examination theme (D6-RQC-01 through
   D6-RQC-05), each drawing its primary themes, governance object, and
   preliminary evidence classes from the Founder's illustrative sub-topics
   for the corresponding subject area; and
4. separately considered, and elected to prepare as a distinct candidate,
   the Founder's specific administrative subject — Documentation
   Architecture and the "one clearly defined administrative responsibility"
   principle (D6-RQC-06) — because it is not already covered by any of the
   five approved themes' existing wording, it is a genuinely separable
   question (whether a demonstrated pattern should become permanent
   practice, distinct from what that practice's content would be), and the
   Founder explicitly invited this consideration without pre-deciding it.

No candidate states a preferred administrative model, ownership assignment,
standard, or implementation design. No candidate assumes that the Single
Source of Truth principle, or any other observed pattern, already is
permanent Framework Administration practice.

## 5. Proportionality and Overlap Assessment

Six candidates are proportionate because:

- five candidates map one-to-one to the five examination themes the Founder
  has already confirmed as appropriate (FEF-FGR-002-D6-FRP-001 Decision
  Agenda Item 2), so no theme is left uncovered and none is duplicated;
- the sixth candidate is not a subdivision of any approved theme but a
  distinct, Founder-invited question about whether a demonstrated pattern
  should become permanent practice — a genuinely separable question from
  what any such practice's content would be;
- no candidate is included merely to enumerate every illustrative sub-topic
  in the Founder's guidance; sub-topics are treated as coverage inputs to
  the relevant theme-level candidate, consistent with the D5 precedent's
  treatment of Agenda strategic-examination items; and
- no candidate proposes, recommends, or embeds a preferred administrative
  standard, ownership model, or documentation-architecture design.

No merge or removal is proposed. The separately labelled Validator pass may
still return any candidate for revision before canonical identifier
allocation or DG-2 admission.
