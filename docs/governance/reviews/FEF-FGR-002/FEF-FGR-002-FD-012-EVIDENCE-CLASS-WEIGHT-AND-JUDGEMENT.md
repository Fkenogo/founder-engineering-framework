## 1. Decision Question

What attributable disposition and enduring treatment applies to
FEF-FGR-002-GF-010?

## 2. Exact Validated Finding

> Evidence class constrains the kind of proposition a source may support but does
> not automatically determine decisive weight. Contested weight requires an
> explicit rationale addressing exact authority, scope, provenance, relevance,
> temporal state, corroboration, contradiction, and uncertainty; repetition or
> automation cannot substitute for human or Founder judgement.

## 3. Exact Founder Disposition and Decision

**Disposition: Accept with Conditions**

Accept that evidence class constrains the kind of proposition a source may
support but shall not automatically determine decisive weight.

Contested evidential weight shall require an explicit attributable rationale
considering:

- exact authority;
- scope;
- provenance;
- relevance;
- temporal state;
- corroboration;
- contradiction;
- uncertainty.

Repetition, preference, or automation shall not substitute for human or Founder
judgement.

This disposition does not establish:

- an ordinal evidence hierarchy;
- an automatic scoring model;
- quantitative weighting;
- a universal conflict-priority rule;
- a complete D3 assurance model.

Those matters require later evidence and governed review.

## 4. Decision Treatment

The no-automatic-weight and attributable-rationale requirements are accepted.
No hierarchy, score, threshold, or conflict-priority rule may be inferred from
this decision.

## 5. Rationale, Conditions, and Limitations

No separate Founder rationale was supplied. The disposition itself supplies
the controlling conditions and exclusions. EP-002 contains no live conflict
between equally authoritative sources and no E3 source. Those evidence limits
remain controlling.
