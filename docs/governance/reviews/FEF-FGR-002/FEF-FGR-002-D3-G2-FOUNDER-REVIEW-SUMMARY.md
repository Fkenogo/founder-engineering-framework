## 1. What D3 Examined

D3 examined nine admitted Review Questions (RQ-016 through RQ-024) using
the frozen FEF-FGR-002-EP-003 v1.0 Evidence Pack only, across nine bounded
Execution Loops (001 through 009), each following an identical seven-phase
methodology: RQ load, four-way evidence separation (established evidence /
supported observations / unsupported assertions / uncertainty), contrary
evidence review, gap assessment, finding draft, independence and
compensating controls, and non-effects.

The session considered assurance coverage and proportionality;
independence and combined operational capacities; challenge, rebuttal,
dissent, and escalation; validation outcomes and reliance consequences;
residual governance risk; assurance of Founder decision inputs and
records; assurance continuity and revalidation triggers; tool-assisted
and AI-assisted assurance boundaries; and assurance traceability,
closure, and downstream reliance.

FEF-FGR-002-S03 subsequently passed the Session Exit Gate (FEF-FGRP-001
§12.1) and closed — Examination Complete; Governance Findings Presented —
producing nine findings and no Founder Decision, Constitutional
Candidate, Deferred Matter, or Open Question disposition.

## 2. What the Findings Collectively Establish

Each finding is independently scoped to its own Review Question and
explicitly draws no conclusion about the other eight. Without performing
new cross-finding synthesis, the nine findings' own text, read together,
establishes the following about the mapped evidence:

1. assurance across FEF-FGR-002 is applied through layered, separately-
   timed passes, with evidence-class tiering operated but no distinct
   heavier control tier evidenced for transitions approaching Founder-
   reserved authority (GF-015);
2. combined operational roles are Founder-approved and compensated at
   assignment level, but a stricter artefact-level closing condition
   (independent revalidation or express Founder risk-acceptance) remains
   unsatisfied for any specific artefact examined (GF-016);
3. the evidence mapped to the challenge/dissent/escalation question is
   insufficient to answer it; the RQ-018 gap disclosed at pack freeze
   remains open (GF-017 — negative/gap finding);
4. four distinct validation-outcome levels are operated, each denying
   approval or Founder authority in its own text, while several declared
   outcome types (Blocked, Returned, Failed, Reopened, Superseded) remain
   unevidenced (GF-018);
5. Founder Decisions repeatedly disclose that no risk is accepted beyond
   their exact conditions, and one process-level non-automation boundary
   is the clearest control identified; no operated escalation or
   reassessment example is evidenced (GF-019);
6. a complete before-and-after assurance cycle is evidenced for exactly
   one Founder gate (D3-G1); the D2 Founder Decision track evidences only
   the after side within the mapped set, and downstream implementation
   reliance is stated as a precondition, not independently confirmed
   (GF-020);
7. routine fingerprint reverification is evidenced at every observed gate
   transition, and two operated defect/challenge-correction episodes
   exist, both handled by the same non-independent capacity; whether
   independent revalidation should become a standing requirement is left
   explicitly undecided by the mapped evidence's own most direct statement
   on the question (GF-021);
8. a recurring deterministic-check-plus-disclosure-plus-non-effects
   pattern is the review's de facto practice, not a Founder-approved
   standard; the same two operated defect episodes (EV-058, EV-059) show
   the pattern did not, by itself, prevent an authority overreach or a
   false closure claim (GF-022); and
9. the FEF-RQS-001 §12 traceability template is operated end-to-end for
   D1 and D2, but for D3 the chain currently stops at the GF node, with
   "Founder Disposition: Pending" recorded as the specification's own
   explicit reason for the missing link (GF-023).

The EV-058 (unauthorised admission, later quarantined) and EV-059
(inaccurate closure claim, later corrected) episodes recur as disclosed,
unresolved qualifications across GF-015, GF-018, GF-019 (by explicit
non-mapping), GF-021, GF-022, and GF-023. Neither episode is adjudicated,
cured, or resolved by any finding or by this package.

## 3. What Remains Unresolved

- whether the RQ-018 gap reflects no disagreement having arisen, an
  absence of preserving controls, or another cause (GF-017);
- whether the absence of a distinct transition-authority proportionality
  tier is a deliberate design choice or an unaddressed gap (GF-015);
- a risk-differentiated independence standard, and whether independent
  revalidation should become a standing requirement (GF-016, GF-021);
- a rule for choosing between in-place correction and quarantine-and-
  restoration as consequence paths for a defective outcome (GF-018);
- whether the recurring "no risk accepted beyond exact conditions" field
  is a deliberate control or an administrative artefact (GF-019);
- whether the D3-G1 before/after assurance cycle generalises to a
  repeatable model for future Founder gates, and whether downstream
  implementation reliance is independently confirmed rather than merely
  authorised and self-reported (GF-020);
- whether the recurring deterministic-check/disclosure/non-effects
  pattern was deliberately designed as an assurance-boundary model
  (GF-022);
- whether D3's eventual Founder-Decision and Candidate/Deferral
  traceability will take the same single-register form used for D1 and
  D2 (GF-023);
- D4–D8 subject matter, which every finding excludes by its own declared
  scope boundary.
