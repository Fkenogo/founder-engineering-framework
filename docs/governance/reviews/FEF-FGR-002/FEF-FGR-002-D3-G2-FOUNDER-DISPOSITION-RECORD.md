## 1. Purpose and Authority

This record captures the Founder's disposition of each of the nine D3
Governance Findings (GF-015 through GF-023), presented from
FEF-FGR-002-D3-G2-FOUNDER-REVIEW-PACKAGE during the D3-G2 Founder Review
session held on 2026-07-27.

The coding agent presented each finding's exact conclusion, evidence,
limitations, confidence statement, and scope without reinterpretation,
and did not recommend, suggest, or otherwise influence any disposition.
The Founder alone determined the disposition, conditions, and follow-up
for every finding. The text recorded in §2 below is reproduced verbatim
from the Founder's own supplied record and has not been paraphrased,
expanded, shortened, or reasoned about further by the recording capacity.

This record does not itself create a Founder Decision Record (FD), issue
DG-5, perform DG-6 Domain Exit, close FEF-FGR-002-S03 or D3, create a
Constitutional Candidate, or extract a constitutional principle. Those
remain separately governed actions at later gates.

## 2. Finding-by-Finding Dispositions

### FEF-FGR-002-GF-015

**Founder Observation**

The finding accurately reflects the evidence and demonstrates a sound layered assurance approach.

**Founder Rationale**

I accept the finding because it is supported by the examined evidence. However, the proportionality principle has only been demonstrated within the reviewed scope and shall not yet be interpreted as a framework-wide governance rule.

**Disposition**

Accept with Conditions

**Condition**

The finding shall remain bounded to the evidence examined and shall not establish a general proportionality doctrine.

**Follow-up**

None.
