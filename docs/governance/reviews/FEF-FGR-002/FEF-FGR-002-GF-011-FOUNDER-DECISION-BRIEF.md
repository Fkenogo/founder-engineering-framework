## 1. Exact Finding

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-011 — Sufficiency Requires Explicit Gap, Conflict, and Stop Treatment**

> Evidence sufficiency is specific to the Review Question and proposition. Before
> an examination path proceeds, the record must disclose coverage, contrary or
> superseding evidence, material limitations, uncertainty, unavailable evidence,
> and gaps, and must state whether the path may proceed, proceed conditionally,
> or must stop. Missing evidence may never be replaced by assumption.

Lifecycle state: `Presented — Founder disposition pending`.

## 2. Decision Question

Should the Founder accept an enduring qualitative requirement that each RQ
explicitly record coverage, gaps, conflict, limitations, and a proceed,
conditional-proceed, or stop treatment, without creating universal
quantitative sufficiency thresholds?

## 3. Evidence Basis

EV-005–EV-008; EV-012; EV-014; EV-022–EV-027; and EV-031. Exact citations
remain in the [S02 Governance Finding Set](FEF-FGR-002-S02-GOVERNANCE-FINDINGS.md).
No evidence outside EP-002 is introduced.

## 4. What Is Established

- Sufficiency is specific to the RQ and proposition, not a generic pack label.
- Coverage, conflict, limitations, uncertainty, unavailable evidence, and gaps
  must remain visible.
- The path requires an explicit proceed, conditional-proceed, or stop treatment.
- Missing evidence cannot be filled by assumption or reconstruction.
- A bounded conclusion may proceed when the remaining evidence supports a
  narrower proposition and the limitation remains explicit.

## 5. What Is Not Established

- Universal quantitative sufficiency or corroboration thresholds.
- External completeness duties for legal, privacy, security, professional, or
  project evidence.
- Treatment of a live conflict between equally authoritative external sources.
- A general D3 residual-risk or assurance model.
- Independent validation.

OQ-006 and OQ-018 remain open.

## 6. Decision Need

Current FEF-FGR-002 controls already use qualitative proceed/condition/block
treatment. Founder authority is required to accept an enduring minimum or
conditions for later FEF governance reviews.

A bounded disposition can accept qualitative disclosure and stop-treatment
requirements while expressly declining to create universal numeric thresholds
or external completeness rules.
