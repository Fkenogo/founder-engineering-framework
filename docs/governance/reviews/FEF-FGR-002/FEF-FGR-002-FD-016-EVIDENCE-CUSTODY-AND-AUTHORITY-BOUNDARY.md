## 1. Decision Question

What attributable disposition and enduring treatment applies to
FEF-FGR-002-GF-014?

## 2. Exact Validated Finding

> Evidence custody is an operational responsibility for preserving evidence
> identity, provenance, access state, integrity, versions, pack custody, and
> traceability. It does not determine evidential meaning or exercise Founder
> authority. Combined custody, analysis, recording, and validation must remain
> capacity-labelled, disclosed as non-independent, supported by deterministic
> checks and escalation, and subject to later independent revalidation where
> required.

## 3. Exact Founder Disposition and Decision

**Disposition: Accept with Conditions**

Accept that Evidence Custody is an operational responsibility for preserving:

- evidence identity;
- provenance;
- access state;
- integrity;
- versions;
- Evidence Pack custody;
- traceability.

Custody, repository access, tool access, authorship, recording, or validation
shall not confer:

- authority to determine evidential meaning;
- approval authority;
- Founder authority.

Where operational capacities are combined, each action shall remain:

- capacity-labelled;
- sequentially performed;
- disclosed as non-independent;
- supported by deterministic controls;
- subject to ambiguity escalation;
- separated from Founder judgement;
- subject to independent revalidation where later assurance controls require
  it.

This disposition does not establish:

- independent assurance thresholds;
- role-handover and succession requirements;
- restricted-evidence policy;
- retention schedules;
- archival architecture;
- deletion or legal-hold rules;
- broader information-lifecycle governance.

Those subjects remain for D3 and D4.

## 4. Decision Treatment

The operational custody boundary, non-conferment of decision authority, and
combined-capacity controls are accepted. Independent assurance, handover,
restricted evidence, retention, archival, deletion, legal hold, and broader
information lifecycle remain outside D2.

## 5. Rationale, Conditions, and Limitations

No separate Founder rationale was supplied. The disposition itself supplies
the controlling conditions and exclusions. Current validation remains
non-independent. No role handover, custody succession, restricted evidence, or
external E3 source has been operated.
