## 1. Authorisation Question

Should the validated D6 Mobilisation-Planning Package
(FEF-FGR-002-D6-MPP-001) — which, under Post-D5 Founder Condition 2,
substitutes for a standard domain mobilisation record — become the basis on
which D6 mobilisation is made effective?

## 2. Exact Founder Record

The following block reproduces the Founder-supplied decision, rationale,
conditions, and conditions-rationale exactly, without interpretation,
amendment, or supplementation. It is reproduced from the handover instrument
"FEF-FGR-002 — Record Founder Disposition — Approve D6 Mobilisation with
Conditions."

<!-- BEGIN EXACT FOUNDER RECORD -->
**Founder decision:**

Approve D6 Mobilisation with Conditions

This decision supersedes the planning stage only.

It does not authorise:

- D7
- D8
- Framework Evolution
- Constitutional implementation
- Final Assembly

Only D6 mobilisation.

**Founder rationale**

The Founder is satisfied that:

- the Post-D5 prerequisite activity was completed correctly;
- authorised control corrections were completed and validated;
- D6 has been appropriately narrowed;
- D6 is now limited to residual cross-project Framework Administration;
- no premature Review Questions or implementation authority have been created;
- the Framework has matured sufficiently to transition from framework construction toward governed execution.

The Founder therefore authorises D6 mobilisation subject to the recorded conditions.

**Founder conditions (must be preserved exactly)**

Condition 1

D6 shall remain strictly limited to residual cross-project Framework Administration as defined within the approved mobilisation-planning package.

No expansion of scope is authorised.

Condition 2

D6 shall not perform constitutional redesign.

Any constitutional implications identified during D6 shall be recorded only and handled through the appropriate later governance lifecycle.

Condition 3

No Framework Evolution activity is authorised.

Framework improvements identified during D6 may be recorded where appropriate but shall not be activated, dispositioned or implemented.

Condition 4

Mobilisation authorises examination only.

No recommendation, finding or Founder Decision produced during D6 creates implementation authority.

Condition 5

The distinction between:

- programme administration;
- governance methodology;
- constitutional governance;
- Framework Evolution

shall remain explicit throughout D6.

No category shall be merged or treated as interchangeable.

Condition 6

The established Founder Governance Review lifecycle shall continue to be followed without omission or compression.

No Review Question shall bypass the approved governance sequence.

Condition 7

D7 and D8 remain Uncommenced.

This mobilisation creates no authority for D7, D8 or Final Assembly.

Condition 8

The Founder Engineering Framework is now considered operational.

Future changes to the Framework itself should be exceptional and justified by governance necessity rather than preference or convenience.

Execution of the approved methodology is now the primary objective.

**Founder rationale for the conditions**

These conditions preserve the governance boundaries established during D1–D5 while recognising that the Framework has reached sufficient maturity to transition from framework construction toward framework execution.

The Founder intends D6 to complete the remaining administrative governance necessary before the Framework proceeds to Constitutional Candidate Assessment (D7) and Open Question Resolution (D8).

## 3. Permitted Outcomes

[FEF-FGR-002-D6-MPP-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-MOBILISATION-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md) §12
presented four unselected Founder options:

| Option | Effect if Selected |
|---|---|
| Authorise D6 mobilisation as proposed | Permit mobilisation within the package's narrow boundary and the six proposed conditions in §10 |
| **Authorise D6 mobilisation with amendments** | Permit mobilisation only after Founder-specified scope or conditions are recorded and validated |
| Require further narrowing or correction | Return the package for bounded revision; D6 remains Uncommenced |
| Defer D6 | Preserve D6 as Uncommenced pending later attributable reconsideration |

The Founder's exact record in Section 2 is controlling. Mapped transparently
against this table, the Founder's disposition corresponds to **"Authorise D6
mobilisation with amendments"**: the Founder supplied eight of the Founder's
own conditions (Section 2), which supersede — rather than simply select — the
six conditions proposed without preselection in FEF-FGR-002-D6-MPP-001 §10.
This mapping is disclosed as a cross-reference only; it does not alter,
interpret, or supplement the exact Founder record above.

## 4. Authorisation Effect

The exact Founder record in Section 2 is controlling.

This authorisation changes the D6 lifecycle state only:

> **Uncommenced**

to:

> **Mobilised — Effective, subject to the eight recorded conditions**

FEF-FGR-002 remains Active. D1 through D5 remain Closed. D7 and D8 remain
Uncommenced, consistent with Condition 7. No Review Question, evidence item,
Evidence Pack, session, examination, Governance Finding, or review-scoped
Founder Decision is created by this authorisation. D6 examination preparation
— under the exact boundaries stated in Conditions 1 through 8 — is the next
separately governed activity.

This authorisation is validated in
[FEF-FGR-002-D6-FMAVR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-FOUNDER-MOBILISATION-AUTHORISATION-VALIDATION-REPORT.md).

## 5. Non-Effects

This authorisation does not prepare, allocate, validate, admit, or answer a
Review Question; identify, register, qualify, admit, or examine evidence;
create or modify an Evidence Record or Evidence Pack; create or open a
session; commence substantive D6 examination; create or modify a Governance
Finding, review-scoped Founder Decision, Constitutional Candidate, Deferred
Matter, or Open Question; amend the review methodology; change review
checkpoint cadence; perform Framework Evolution or disposition
FEF-FEV-001-FEC-001; commence, prepare, or authorise D7, D8, or Final
Assembly; perform constitutional redesign or create constitutional content
(Condition 2); create implementation authority from any D6 recommendation,
finding, or Founder Decision (Condition 4); merge or treat as interchangeable
programme administration, governance methodology, constitutional governance,
and Framework Evolution (Condition 5); bypass, omit, or compress the
established Founder Governance Review lifecycle or DG sequence (Condition 6);
or reopen D1–D5.
