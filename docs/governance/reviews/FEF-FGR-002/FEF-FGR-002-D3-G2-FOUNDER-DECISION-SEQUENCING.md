## 3. Basis for This Order

This order matches the sequence in which the nine Review Questions were
examined (Execution Loops 001 through 009) and the order in which the
findings themselves were produced and numbered (GF-015 through GF-023).
It is not a new analytical ordering invented for this package; it
reproduces the existing, already-established examination sequence, and
the "sequencing rationale" column above restates relationships each
finding's own text already discloses about itself (principally through
its own citation of EV-058, EV-059, or an adjacent RQ) rather than
introducing new cross-finding analysis.

## 4. Dependency Chains

```text
GF-015 → GF-016
GF-016 → GF-020 → GF-021
GF-018 → GF-021 → GF-022
GF-015, GF-018, GF-021 → GF-022 (shared EV-058/EV-059 citation)
GF-015 through GF-022 → GF-023 (traceability chain-stop observation)
```

- GF-015 is a natural starting point because it establishes the general
  layered-assurance-pass pattern.
- GF-016, GF-018, and GF-021 should be considered in the same review
  window because each cites EV-058 and/or EV-059 as a disclosed,
  unresolved qualification, but each remains separately dispositioned
  because its Review Question and evidence differ.
- GF-020 depends on understanding GF-016 (independence) and GF-019
  (Founder-decision conditions) first.
- GF-023 is best considered last because it examines the traceability
  chain that every earlier finding's "Founder Disposition: Pending"
  status instantiates.

## 5. Coordinated Consideration Without Combination

The Founder may consider these groups together for context:

- GF-015 and GF-016 — layered assurance versus independence at the point
  of assurance;
- GF-018, GF-021, and GF-022 — outcome consequences, continuity triggers,
  and the recurring deterministic-check pattern, all touching EV-058 and
  EV-059;
- GF-019 and GF-020 — residual risk and Founder-decision-input assurance;
- GF-023 with all eight preceding findings — the traceability chain each
  instantiates through its own "Founder Disposition: Pending" status.

Each finding must retain a separate attributable disposition because the
evidence, risks, conditions, and later-domain effects differ.
