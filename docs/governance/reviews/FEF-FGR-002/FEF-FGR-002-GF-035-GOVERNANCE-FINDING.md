## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What exception, deviation, or expiry mechanism, if any, should apply when a
> governance instrument is temporarily or conditionally departed from, and
> what distinguishes a bounded exception from controlled evolution of the
> framework itself?

This finding uses only EV-005, EV-012, EV-017, EV-066, EV-074, and EV-083 as
frozen for RQ-036 in EP-005 v2.0. All six remain bounded to their exact
qualification and permitted uses. No unmapped evidence or later substituted
file was used.

## 2. Finding

The mapped evidence establishes bounded controls and examples:

1. EV-005 establishes a Charter-specific exception rule requiring an affected
   clause, reason, scope, duration, risks, compensating controls, approval
   authority, and expiry or review trigger. Such an exception cannot delegate
   Founder decision authority or permit evidence reconstruction.
2. EV-012 records OQ-012 as an unresolved question about exception, deviation,
   expiry, and escalation for research standards; it supplies no answer.
3. EV-017 reserves Founder Decision Authority to the Founder and separates it
   from operational roles. It does not establish that every exception is an FD
   or identify delegated exception authority.
4. EV-066 and EV-074 evidence Accept with Conditions and Approve with
   Conditions dispositions. Neither invokes the Charter exception rule's
   clause, duration, or expiry-trigger structure, so neither is established as
   an exception, deviation, waiver, or temporary departure.
5. EV-083 establishes a Framework Evolution intake mechanism with no amendment,
   candidate-decision, implementation, or constitutional effect. Intake
   availability is not candidate evaluation and does not itself define the
   boundary between exception and controlled evolution.

Together these sources establish a scoped exception rule, conditioned decision
examples, an authority boundary, and a prospective evolution-intake route.
They do not establish a general FEF-wide exception/deviation/expiry model,
delegated exception authority, complete lifecycle or record mechanics, or an
approved boundary test between bounded exception and controlled evolution.

## 4. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source directly imposes an exception rule incompatible with another.
The decisive limitations are scope and missing reconciliation. EV-005 controls
exceptions to the Charter, EV-012 remains an open research-standard question,
EV-017 controls Founder Decision Authority rather than every exception, EV-066
and EV-074 are conditioned dispositions without exception metadata, and EV-083
establishes intake structure and non-effects without defining controlled
evolution. These qualifications prevent the examples from being combined into
a complete model.

## 5. Limitations, Uncertainty, and OQ-012

- Confidence is high that Charter §21.3 states the scoped exception fields and
  protected authority boundary described above.
- Confidence is high that EV-066 and EV-074 are conditioned dispositions, but
  insufficient to classify them as exceptions.
- Confidence is high that FEF-FEV-001 provides intake only, with no amendment,
  evaluation, adoption, or implementation effect.
- No positive confidence is assigned to a general mechanism, delegation,
  expiry lifecycle, ownership, register design, or exception/evolution
  boundary.
- OQ-012 remains open, unchanged, and undispositioned. Its linkage is
  clarified by this finding, but the question is not resolved.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.

## 6. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The evidence does not decide whether a FEF-wide exception mechanism should
exist; which authority may approve non-FD exceptions; how deviation, waiver,
duration, expiry, renewal, revocation, monitoring, escalation, ownership,
closure, or registration should work; or where the authoritative boundary
between bounded exception and permanent framework evolution should lie. These
remain matters for later separately governed Founder judgement. This finding
proposes no model and makes no recommendation.

## 7. Scope and Confidence

**Confidence: High for the Charter-specific rule and bounded operated facts;
insufficient for a general exception/evolution model.** The exact acquisition
objects support the stated exception fields, authority boundary, conditioned
decisions, Open Question, and intake non-effects. Their scope limits and the
absence of an operated Charter exception or approved boundary test prevent
generalisation.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-036 and the six mapped frozen records. It does
not decide RQ-037, amend FEF-RGS-000, grant an exception, evaluate a Framework
Evolution candidate, or commence D6/D7.

## 8. Non-Effects

evidence treatment; alter GF-031 through GF-034; issue a Founder disposition or
Founder Decision; examine RQ-037; close S05 or D5; or commence D6 or D7. It
remains Presented and pending Founder disposition.
