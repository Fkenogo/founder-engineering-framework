## 9. Lessons Learned (Administrative Only)

The following are historical administrative observations arising from
the conduct of D6. They carry no constitutional authority, create no
Framework requirement, and are not Framework Evolution. They are not
recommendations and are not framed as policy.

- Across all six D6 findings, the recurring evidentiary limitation was
  the absence of a second adopting Founder project: several findings
  could establish only that a pattern operates within FEF-FGR-002's own
  internal documents, without evidence of cross-project applicability.
  This was recorded as a bounded observation in each affected finding,
  not treated as a gap requiring immediate closure.
- Version-pinning of live, actively-edited evidence sources (the Master
  Programme, Founder Dashboard, Review Identity, and Document Manifest)
  to their exact frozen acquisition state was applied consistently
  across all six examination loops and the Founder Decision issuance
  step, and required explicit citation discipline in each examination
  record.
- The one-examination-loop-one-commit engineering discipline, and the
  subsequent one-gate-one-commit discipline through Session Exit,
  Founder Review Package preparation, and Founder Decision issuance,
  produced a linear, independently verifiable commit history for the
  domain.
- The Founder's chosen decision format for D6 (a single Disposition and
  a single Founder Reflection per finding, without itemised Conditions)
  differed from the D3–D5 pattern (common and finding-specific
  Conditions). Both formats were preserved exactly as supplied, without
  being reshaped to match either precedent.
