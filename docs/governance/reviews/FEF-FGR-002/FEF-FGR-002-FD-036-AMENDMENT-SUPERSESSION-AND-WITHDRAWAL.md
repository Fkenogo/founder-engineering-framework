## 1. Finding Reference

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-034 — Bounded Founder-Authority and Recovery-Preservation Practices Are Evidenced, but No General Transition, Delegation, Ownership, or Preservation Model Is Established**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

**Founder Observations**

A controlled transition record is required when a change affects authoritative
reliance. Routine editing should not trigger excessive transition machinery.

**Discussion**

The Founder considered GF-034 and its authorised lean conditions. No additional
discussion is attributed.

**Disposition**

Accept with Conditions

**Conditions**

Common conditions applying to GF-031 through GF-036:

1. Acceptance confirms the finding, not a proposed solution.
2. Any future FEF control must remain a minimum cross-project foundation.
3. Project-specific lifecycle detail remains under project authority unless a
   universal requirement is demonstrated.
4. Founder-reserved authority cannot be delegated by implication.
5. Existing valid work and historical records must not be retrospectively
   altered without explicit authority.
6. No repository-wide reconciliation, renumbering, reclassification or
   migration programme is authorised.
7. Implementation design requires separately governed follow-up after Founder
   Decision issuance.
8. All eight applicable Open Questions remain open unless separately resolved.

Finding-specific Conditions:

1. Transition control is required when an authoritative instrument is amended,
   superseded or withdrawn in a way that affects reliance.
2. The transition must identify the affected instrument, old and new states,
   approving authority and effective date or event.
3. It must explain the effect on ongoing work and preserve the previous
   authoritative version.
4. Detailed workflows and ownership may remain project-specific unless a
   universal need is demonstrated.
5. No retrospective transition-record reconstruction programme is authorised.

**Rationale**

Authority changes must remain traceable without treating every document edit as
a constitutional transition.

**Follow-up Actions**

Define the minimum authoritative-transition record through separate design
work.

## 3. Exact Finding Scope

**Confidence: High for the bounded operated controls and examples;
insufficient for a general transition model.** The exact historical records
support the stated Founder-authority, identifier, quarantine, correction, and
candidate-state observations. Their scope limits, the absence of an ordinary
amendment example, and open OQ-021 prevent generalisation.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-035 and the six mapped frozen records. It does
not decide RQ-036 or RQ-037, D6 administration, D7 constitutional effect, D4
retention policy, or Framework Evolution.
