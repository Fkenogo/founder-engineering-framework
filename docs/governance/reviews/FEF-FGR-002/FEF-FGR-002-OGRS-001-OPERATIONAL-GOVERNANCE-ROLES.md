## 1. Purpose

Define the operational roles required to administer, record, support, and validate FEF-FGR-002 without transferring or expanding Founder authority.

This is a review-specific operational standard. It does not create framework-wide governance, constitutional authority, or substantive review outcomes.

## 2. Role Model and Charter Mapping

| Operational Role | Approved Charter Role Mapping | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Founder | Founder | Reserved authority role |
| Review Administrator | Review Coordinator | Operational administration and gate coordination |
| Review Analyst | Review Analyst | Evidence-based analysis and candidate-output preparation |
| Review Recorder | Session Recorder | Attributable and exact record preparation |
| Evidence Custodian | Evidence Custodian | Evidence identity, provenance, preservation, and pack custody |
| Validator | Validator | Independent record, evidence, boundary, and gate validation |

“Review Administrator” and “Review Recorder” are operational labels for the approved Charter functions. They do not change the Charter’s authority model.

## 3. Universal Role Rules

Every role:

- acts only within an attributable assignment recorded under FEF-FGR-002-RAP-001;
- preserves exact role boundaries when roles are combined;
- records conflicts, limitations, vacancies, and temporary arrangements;
- maintains traceability to the governing instruments;
- stops and escalates when required authority or evidence is absent;
- may not infer a Founder Decision, close an Open Question, amend the Constitution, amend FEF-RGS-000, or authorise Engineering Discovery.

An operational role is not authority merely because it performs work.

## 4. Founder

### 4.1 Purpose

Exercise the reserved authority that only the Founder may exercise within FEF-FGR-002.

### 4.2 Responsibilities

- issue, condition, defer, supersede, withdraw, or decline Founder Decisions;
- approve exact FD wording;
- accept risks reserved to the Founder;
- disposition matters escalated for reserved authority;
- approve final review treatment under the Charter;
- approve initial role assignments and controlled role combinations;
- preserve the boundary between Founder decisions and operational recommendations.

### 4.3 Authority

The Founder is:

- accountable for reserved decision authority;
- the only role that may issue or amend a review-scoped FD;
- the authority for initial operational role assignments;
- the final escalation point for unresolved authority, scope, exception, and reserved-risk matters.

### 4.4 Authority Limitations

Founder authority does not:

- convert unvalidated evidence into admissible evidence;
- make an incomplete record complete;
- remove the need to record exact decisions and non-effects;
- merge operational roles by implication;
- create constitutional effect unless a separately authorised constitutional process does so;
- approve or amend FEF-RGS-000 through review commencement or operational activity.

### 4.5 Required Competencies

- understanding of the approved Charter, Agenda, Plan, and execution gates;
- ability to distinguish decision, recommendation, validation, and implementation;
- ability to state exact scope, conditions, non-effects, and risk acceptance;
- commitment to attributable decisions and preserved uncertainty.

### 4.6 Mandatory Independence

The Founder may perform operational roles in a Founder-operated review, but must state the capacity in which each action is taken. Founder decision authority must remain distinguishable from preparation, custody, recording, and validation activity.

### 4.7 Interactions

- receives decision-ready matters from the Review Administrator;
- relies on validated inputs without delegating the decision;
- provides exact wording to the Review Recorder;
- receives escalations from the Validator and Evidence Custodian;
- may consult the Review Analyst without treating analysis as a decision.

## 5. Review Administrator

### 5.1 Purpose

Coordinate authorised review operations, sequence, scope, registers, dependencies, and gates.

### 5.2 Responsibilities

- administer the review identity and operational control records;
- maintain sequence, domain scope, dependencies, risks, and gate status;
- coordinate RQ drafting and admit validated RQs under DG-2;
- prepare sessions only after prerequisites pass;
- coordinate candidate GF and constitutional-candidate workflows;
- prepare completion packages;
- escalate reserved or unresolved matters;
- ensure no activity proceeds through an unsatisfied gate.

### 5.3 Authority

- administer registers and workflow;
- return incomplete operational artefacts;
- admit, return, defer, or reject an RQ within the Plan after validation;
- block operational progression when a dependency or gate fails;
- coordinate domain and review completion preparation.

### 5.4 Authority Limitations

The Review Administrator cannot:

- issue a Founder Decision;
- determine evidential meaning unilaterally;
- validate their own preparation where independence is mandatory;
- make a GF authoritative;
- approve constitutional content;
- waive a Charter or Founder-controlled gate.

### 5.5 Required Competencies

- controlled-document and register administration;
- review planning, dependency management, and gate control;
- neutral RQ framing;
- traceability and lifecycle management;
- escalation and scope control.

### 5.6 Mandatory Independence

The Review Administrator must not act as sole Validator for an artefact they prepared or admitted. If temporary combination is unavoidable, the exception and compensating validation must be recorded before reliance.

### 5.7 Interactions

- coordinates all operational roles;
- receives RQ and GF analysis from the Review Analyst;
- receives evidence-state information from the Evidence Custodian;
- submits artefacts and gates to the Validator;
- submits reserved matters to the Founder;
- instructs the Review Recorder on required record scope without dictating Founder wording.

## 6. Review Analyst

### 6.1 Purpose

Perform bounded, evidence-based governance analysis and prepare candidate review outputs.

### 6.2 Responsibilities

- draft candidate RQs when authorised;
- analyse admitted evidence against admitted RQs;
- identify conflicts, uncertainty, gaps, and options;
- prepare candidate GFs and constitutional candidates;
- distinguish fact, interpretation, recommendation, and unresolved matter;
- preserve contrary evidence and minority interpretations.

### 6.3 Authority

- prepare analysis and candidate artefacts;
- recommend treatment;
- request clarification or additional evidence;
- identify a potential finding or deferred matter.

### 6.4 Authority Limitations

The Review Analyst cannot:

- admit an RQ;
- admit or freeze evidence;
- validate their own analysis;
- issue a GF as dispositioned;
- select a Founder option;
- issue an FD;
- create constitutional effect.

### 6.5 Required Competencies

- structured governance analysis;
- evidence evaluation within recorded admissibility limits;
- neutral drafting;
- uncertainty and conflict treatment;
- cross-domain traceability.

### 6.6 Mandatory Independence

Analysis must remain distinguishable from evidence custody and validation. The analyst may not validate the sufficiency of their own candidate GF.

### 6.7 Interactions

- works from RQs admitted by the Review Administrator;
- uses evidence registered by the Evidence Custodian;
- submits candidate work to the Validator;
- provides analysis to the Founder only as analysis;
- supports the Review Recorder with references, not authoritative decision wording.

## 7. Review Recorder

### 7.1 Purpose

Create complete, attributable, and exact records of review activity and Founder wording.

### 7.2 Responsibilities

- prepare controlled session and decision records;
- capture participants, capacities, evidence versions, RQs, discussion, non-decisions, decisions, conditions, and deferrals;
- preserve exact Founder wording;
- maintain immutable history and change records;
- correct recording errors transparently;
- link records to their source artefacts.

### 7.3 Authority

- request clarification when wording or attribution is ambiguous;
- return a record to draft when mandatory recording information is absent;
- identify discrepancies between source wording and the record.

### 7.4 Authority Limitations

The Review Recorder cannot:

- paraphrase a Founder Decision as authoritative wording;
- infer a decision from discussion or silence;
- change lifecycle state without supporting authority;
- validate the substance of their own record;
- decide a governance question.

### 7.5 Required Competencies

- exact and neutral recording;
- source attribution;
- structured metadata and traceability;
- version control and correction history;
- distinction between discussion, recommendation, decision, and non-decision.

### 7.6 Mandatory Independence

The Recorder may prepare records for work in which they participated, but a separate Validator should verify exactness and completeness before downstream reliance.

### 7.7 Interactions

- receives scope and metadata requirements from the Review Administrator;
- confirms exact Founder wording directly from the attributable source;
- obtains evidence references from the Evidence Custodian;
- submits completed records to the Validator.

## 8. Evidence Custodian

### 8.1 Purpose

Control evidence identity, provenance, integrity, admissibility records, preservation, access, and Evidence Packs.

### 8.2 Responsibilities

- register candidate evidence without implying admission;
- preserve provenance, version, integrity, authority, limitations, and access treatment;
- perform admissibility checks jointly with the Validator;
- maintain the Evidence Register and Evidence Pack Register;
- assemble and freeze Evidence Packs under FEF-EPS-001;
- preserve superseded and supplemental versions;
- report integrity failures, conflicts, and evidence gaps.

### 8.3 Authority

- control evidence registration and custody;
- reject unsafe or untraceable material from a pack pending disposition;
- propose rejected, conditional, context-only, or admissible treatment;
- block pack freeze when custody or integrity controls fail.

### 8.4 Authority Limitations

The Evidence Custodian cannot:

- decide the substantive meaning of evidence;
- determine a governance outcome;
- suppress conflicting evidence;
- treat registration as admission;
- validate a pack independently when also its preparer;
- issue an FD.

### 8.5 Required Competencies

- provenance and records control;
- integrity verification;
- evidence admissibility procedures;
- versioning, freeze, and supplement control;
- confidentiality and access handling.

### 8.6 Mandatory Independence

Evidence Pack preparation and final pack validation should be separated. Joint admissibility confirmation requires Validator participation. Any unavoidable combination must be disclosed and independently checked before session use.

### 8.7 Interactions

- receives evidence needs from admitted RQs and the Review Analyst;
- coordinates pack scope with the Review Administrator;
- submits admissibility and freeze controls to the Validator;
- supplies exact evidence references to the Recorder.

## 9. Validator

### 9.1 Purpose

Validate structure, evidence links, completeness, consistency, lifecycle, authority boundaries, independence disclosures, and gate readiness.

### 9.2 Responsibilities

- validate RQs before admission and session use;
- validate evidence admissibility jointly with the Evidence Custodian;
- validate Evidence Pack freeze, version, scope, and integrity;
- validate session entry and records;
- validate candidate GFs, FD records, domain exits, and final review completeness;
- record defects and required corrections;
- stop reliance when a critical validation control fails.

### 9.3 Authority

- issue permitted validation outcomes;
- return an artefact for correction;
- block a gate when required validation fails;
- escalate authority, integrity, independence, and scope defects.

### 9.4 Authority Limitations

The Validator cannot:

- create missing evidence;
- answer an RQ;
- choose a recommendation or Founder option;
- issue, amend, or withdraw an FD;
- validate their own preparation without a disclosed exception and compensating control;
- cure a substantive defect through inference;
- approve constitutional or RGS content.

### 9.5 Required Competencies

- evidence and traceability validation;
- authority-boundary analysis;
- controlled lifecycle and identifier checks;
- internal-consistency and completeness review;
- independence and conflict assessment;
- defect classification and escalation.

### 9.6 Mandatory Independence

The Validator should be independent of the preparation being validated. At minimum, the validator must not be the sole preparer and sole validator of the same artefact without a recorded exception, stated limitation, and later independent revalidation or Founder-accepted procedural risk.

### 9.7 Interactions

- receives operational artefacts from all preparing roles;
- works jointly with the Evidence Custodian on admissibility;
- reports gate results to the Review Administrator;
- escalates reserved matters to the Founder;
- verifies Recorder outputs against attributable sources.

## 10. Supporting Participants

Technical Advisers and coding agents may provide bounded advice, drafting, cross-referencing, or validation assistance only under an assigned operational role or explicit task.

They do not acquire authority from expertise, tool access, document authorship, or automation.

## 11. Role Interaction Sequence

```text
Founder authority
        ↓
Review Administrator coordinates scope and gates
        ↓
Review Analyst prepares candidate analysis
        ↔
Evidence Custodian controls evidence
        ↓
Review Recorder preserves attributable records
        ↓
Validator independently tests readiness and integrity
        ↓
Founder receives decision-ready matters
```

The sequence does not imply that operational roles may exercise Founder authority.
