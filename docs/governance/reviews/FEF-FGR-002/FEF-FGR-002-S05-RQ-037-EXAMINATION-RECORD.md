## 3. Examination Results by Analytical Classification

### 3.1 Directly Established by Evidence

1. EV-012 establishes that OQ-013 and OQ-016 remain open questions concerning
   the RGS/constitutional hierarchy relationship and the disposition of
   planned FEF-P0-004. It supplies no answer to either question.
2. EV-014 records its own status as `Incomplete — Missing Source Evidence`,
   preserves supplied review metadata, identifies evidence gaps, and expressly
   prevents reconstruction of unsupported review content.
3. EV-078 records one recovery-specific quarantine that preserves six named
   artefacts for possible future authorised reuse without judging quality,
   admitting evidence, registering evidence, or creating constitutional
   effect.
4. EV-080 records FEF-P1-001 as a legacy plan requiring sequence
   reconciliation and FEF-P1-002–004 as legacy planning items not authorised
   to commence at the governed RQ-037 acquisition.
5. EV-084 identifies itself as an `Exploratory Draft — Non-Authoritative` and
   says governed review and approval are required before constitutional use.
6. EV-085 records GF-001's already-dispositioned conclusion that current
   authority is attributable and scope-bounded and that no complete
   constitutional hierarchy is evidenced.

### 3.2 Reasonably Supported

1. The evidence reasonably supports preserving an item's existing label and
   explicit non-effects without treating that label as approval, validity,
   effectiveness, or permission to rely. This is supported as a bounded
   observed practice, not an approved general rule.
2. The evidence reasonably supports distinguishing recovery quarantine from
   ordinary lifecycle classification: EV-078 records a named defect-recovery
   action with specific non-effects and a future-authority condition.
3. The examples reasonably support maintaining traceability to unresolved
   authority and disposition questions rather than filling those gaps through
   retrospective inference. They do not allocate who should own or implement
   a future classification model.

### 3.3 Unsupported

The mapped evidence does not support:

- a universal taxonomy for pre-FEF-FGR-002 or legacy governance material;
- criteria assigning `Exploratory Draft`, `Incomplete`, `Legacy plan`,
  quarantine, superseded, withdrawn, archived, valid, or invalid states;
- a general rule separating classification from retrospective validation or
  invalidation;
- any determination of a legacy instrument's authority, validity,
  effectiveness, approval, or permitted reliance;
- a repository-wide legacy inventory, migration, relabelling, or numbering
  exercise;
- a general retention, archival, deletion, or disposition rule;
- a classification owner, approval authority, register, transition process,
  or exception mechanism;
- reopening, re-examination, or extension of GF-001 or FD-002; or
- commencement of D6 or D7.

### 3.4 Contradictory or Qualifying Matters

No mapped source directly imposes an incompatible classification rule. The
material qualification is that the examples are not a common governed model:

- EV-014 is Context Only and describes an evidence-gap record;
- EV-078 is a specific correction/recovery quarantine, not an ordinary
  transition or retention rule;
- EV-080 classifies four scheduled-work identifiers at one historical
  acquisition point;
- EV-084 is one document's self-description;
- EV-085 supplies only GF-001's fixed, already-dispositioned text; and
- EV-012 preserves two open questions rather than a classification answer.

No source reconciles those distinct cases into a general taxonomy or defines
the authority and reliance consequences of applying one label rather than
another.

### 3.5 Uncertain

It remains uncertain whether a general legacy-material classification rule
should exist; which labels, criteria, authority, custodian, register, or
transition process it would use; whether or how classification affects
reliance; how missing evidence should be handled beyond the EV-014 example;
how legacy scheduled work should be reconciled; and what retention or archival
control should bind any future treatment. OQ-013 and OQ-016 remain unresolved.

### 3.6 Outside Scope

This loop does not classify, approve, validate, invalidate, migrate, renumber,
archive, withdraw, supersede, delete, or change any legacy instrument or work
