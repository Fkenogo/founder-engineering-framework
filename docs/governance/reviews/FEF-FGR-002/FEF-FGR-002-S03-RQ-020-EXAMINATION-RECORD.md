## 2. Phase 2 — Evidence Examination

### 2.1 Established Evidence

- EV-056 (`FEF-FGR-002-D2-DISPOSITION-VALIDATION-REPORT.md`) records an
  overall **Pass with Conditions** verdict and, in its own §6, states that
  "the same Codex agent recorded and validated the disposition package.
  Validation is non-independent and shall not be represented as
  independent assurance." Its §8 Non-Effects clause states the validation
  does not exercise Founder authority, close an Open Question, commence
  D3, create constitutional effect, approve FEF-P1-002, amend FEF-RGS-000,
  or authorise Engineering Discovery.
- EV-065 (`FEF-FGR-002-FD-010`) §2.2, "Founder authority remains
  non-automated," states that governed execution loops "shall not
  exercise, simulate, infer, or automate: Founder judgement; Founder
  dispositions; Founder approval; constitutional authority; interpretation
  of materially ambiguous evidence." §2.4, "Artefact-necessity gate,"
  requires any proposed new governance artefact to demonstrate "what
  governance risk does this reduce that is not already controlled
  elsewhere," and states "no additional artefact shall be created where no
  distinct governance risk is reduced." §4 states the decision creates no
  constitutional effect and no Constitutional Candidate.
- EV-066 through EV-071 (`FD-011` through `FD-016`) each carry an
  identical control-field row, **"Accepted risks | None inferred beyond
  the exact conditions and limitations,"** and each records disposition
  **Accept with Conditions** together with an exact, itemised list of
  subjects that "remain outside this disposition and require later
  governed treatment." Each carries its own Non-Effects clause (e.g., does
  not create constitutional effect, does not commence D3, does not
  authorise Engineering Discovery).
- EV-068 (`FD-013`) §3 explicitly states the disposition "does not
  create ... a general D3 residual-risk model," and that "those subjects
  remain for later governed treatment."
- EV-069 (`FD-014`) §3 requires that a material addition, removal,
  replacement, admissibility change, or integrity change to a frozen
  Evidence Pack undergo "preservation of the prior pack," "impact
  assessment," "renewed validation," and "renewed freeze" before reliance.
- EV-071 (`FD-016`) §3 states that, where operational capacities are
  combined, each action shall remain "subject to ambiguity escalation"
  and "subject to independent revalidation where later assurance controls
  require it."

### 2.2 Supported Observations

1. Across six of the eight mapped items (FD-011 through FD-016), the
   identical control-field row "Accepted risks | None inferred beyond the
   exact conditions and limitations" recurs unchanged. This functions as
   the mapped set's operating mechanism for keeping a Founder's acceptance
   of a *finding* distinct from acceptance of a *risk* — directly
   responsive to RQ-020's "without treating validation as risk acceptance"
   clause — evidenced by exact repetition across six separately numbered
   decision records rather than a single instance.
2. FD-010's non-automation boundary (§2.2) and artefact-necessity gate
   (§2.4) are the mapped set's clearest *process-level* controls: they
   govern what an execution loop may not do and what must be justified
   before a new artefact is created, rather than addressing a specific
   named residual-risk item.
3. FD-013 names "a general D3 residual-risk model" as something it does
   not create, and defers it to "later governed treatment." This is the
   mapped evidence's own acknowledgement, recorded on 2026-07-25, that no
   residual-risk assurance model existed prior to D3. On this record,
   RQ-020's own examination is the first governed attempt at that subject.
4. FD-016 supplies the mapped set's only forward-looking
   escalation/reassessment language ("ambiguity escalation";
   "independent revalidation where later assurance controls require it"),
   and FD-014 supplies a distinct, evidence-baseline-specific conditioning
   mechanism (impact assessment, revalidation, and re-freeze before
   reliance on a materially changed pack). Both describe control
   obligations attached to future operation, not an outcome that has
   already occurred.
5. EV-056's own §6 and §8 repeat the same per-record disclosure pattern
   observed in RQ-019's mapped items (FEF-FGR-002-S03-RQ-019-EXAMINATION-RECORD.md
   §2.2.2): an explicit statement of what a validation record does not do,
   operating here as the identification/disclosure control for the D2
   disposition-validation step itself.

### 2.3 Unsupported Assertions (explicitly excluded)

- That any mapped item records an **operated** example of escalation,
  reassessment, or conditional-reliance being actually triggered. FD-014
  and FD-016 state prospective obligations; no mapped item describes an
  instance of either obligation having been exercised.
- That RQ-020's own declared evidence need for "Accepted D1 risk/exception
  decisions" is satisfied by the mapped set. FD-010 sits at the D1/ORC-001
  boundary, but FD-011 through FD-016 are D2 decisions responding to D2
  Governance Findings (GF-009 through GF-014), not D1 risk or exception
  decisions. This is reported as a mismatch between the RQ's own declared
  evidence need and its mapped set (see §4), not resolved by substitution.
- That a completed residual-governance-risk assurance model, an automatic
  risk score, a risk-acceptance decision, or a FEF-wide exception model can
  be stated from this mapped evidence. These are RQ-020's own declared
  exclusions (D3-RQS-002 §6).
- That the recurring "Accepted risks: None inferred ..." field is itself a
  Founder-authored explanation of what risk acceptance would look like if
  it occurred. The field states an absence; it does not define the
  concept it denies.

### 2.4 Uncertainty

Whether the "Accepted risks: None inferred beyond the exact conditions and
limitations" field was a deliberately designed assurance control aimed at
preventing risk-acceptance conflation, or an administrative artefact of a
repeated decision-recording template, cannot be determined from the
mapped evidence; no mapped record states the field's design rationale.
