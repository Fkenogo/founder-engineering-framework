## 2. FEF-FGR-002-GF-009 — Evidence Qualification Controls Permitted Reliance

### 2.1 Finding Statement

Evidence may support an FEF governance conclusion only when an attributable
qualification record establishes its identity, provenance, acquisition route,
issuer or source authority, version or temporal state, integrity, relevance,
access state, limitations, and permitted use. Registration or pack inclusion
does not itself establish admissibility, truth, or unrestricted reliance.

### 2.2 Factual Basis

- EV-005 Sections 9–10 define evidence classes, ten mandatory admissibility
  tests, prohibited treatments, and Evidence Record fields.
- Conditionally admitted EV-023 defines explicit admission dispositions and
  requires the condition and permitted reliance to be recorded.
- EV-024 through EV-028 demonstrate operated registration, classification,
  hashing, conditional/context treatment, freeze, and entry verification.
- EV-014 and EV-031 demonstrate that missing provenance cannot be replaced by
  reconstruction.

### 2.3 Interpretation

Qualification is a proposition-specific gate. It controls what a source may be
used to establish and prevents a source from gaining authority merely through
repetition, registration, packaging, or later citation.

### 2.4 Governance Risk

Absent qualification or permitted-use controls, stale, substituted,
out-of-scope, context-only, restricted, or conditionally admitted material may
be treated as authoritative.

### 2.5 Current Control State

The current review controls define and operate a qualification model. The
permanent mandatory/conditional field catalogue and external/protected-source
policy are not established.

### 2.6 Gap or Deficiency

- OQ-005, OQ-010, and OQ-018 remain open.
- No E3, restricted, or project-specific evidence was operated.
- FEF-RQS-001 and FEF-EPS-001 remain conditionally admitted preparation
  controls.

### 2.7 Possible Treatment

Retain the operated qualification and explicit-disposition controls for
FEF-FGR-002, and present the enduring minimum-field and external/protected-use
requirements for later Founder decision.

### 2.8 Uncertainty and Limitations

Validation is non-independent. The evidence does not prove that the current
fields are sufficient for every external, mutable, confidential, or
project-controlled source.

### 2.9 Cross-Domain Interfaces

D3 assurance; D4 access and records lifecycle; D6 register administration; D8
OQ-005/OQ-010/OQ-018 treatment.

### 2.10 Candidate and Deferral Assessment

No Constitutional Candidate: no D2 Founder disposition exists and D7
extraction has not occurred. No Deferred Matter: the neighbouring-domain
subjects and open questions are mapped interfaces, not attributable deferrals.

### 2.11 Non-Effects

This finding does not approve an evidence standard, decide external policy,
close an OQ, or expand a source’s authority.

## 3. FEF-FGR-002-GF-010 — Evidence Class Does Not Automatically Determine Weight

| Field | Recorded Value |

| Version | 1.0 |

| Evidence basis | EV-005–EV-008; EV-012; EV-014; EV-022–EV-026 |
| Confidence | High for the no-automation boundary; Medium for a future hierarchy |
| Lifecycle state | Presented — Founder disposition pending |
| Founder decision need | Required |

### 3.1 Finding Statement

Evidence class constrains the kind of proposition a source may support but does
not automatically determine decisive weight. Contested weight requires an
explicit rationale addressing exact authority, scope, provenance, relevance,
temporal state, corroboration, contradiction, and uncertainty; repetition or
automation cannot substitute for human or Founder judgement.

### 3.2 Factual Basis

- EV-005 Section 9 defines classes through permitted use rather than ordinal
  rank and prohibits repetition from being treated as corroboration.
- EV-005 and EV-023 require contrary evidence and authority limitations to
  remain visible.
- EV-024 through EV-026 demonstrate differential reliance for Admitted,
  Conditionally Admitted, and Context Only sources.

### 3.3 Interpretation

Class is a reliance boundary, not a scoring algorithm. A source may be
authoritative for one proposition and irrelevant or limited for another.

### 3.4 Governance Risk

Automatic or preference-based weighting may elevate stale or out-of-scope
material, suppress challenge, or automate reserved judgement.

### 3.5 Current Control State

Evidence classes and permitted uses exist. No approved conflict-priority rule,
ordinal hierarchy, or corroboration threshold exists.

### 3.6 Gap or Deficiency

OQ-006 remains open. EP-002 contains no E3 source or live conflict between
equally authoritative sources.

### 3.7 Possible Treatment

Require an attributable weighting rationale and escalation of materially
ambiguous conflict; reserve an enduring hierarchy for Founder decision.

### 3.8 Uncertainty and Limitations

The finding cannot select the future hierarchy or quantify corroboration. It
does not create a D3 assurance model.

### 3.9 Cross-Domain Interfaces

### 3.10 Candidate and Deferral Assessment

No Constitutional Candidate before Founder disposition and D7 assessment. No
Deferred Matter; the open decision need remains active rather than deferred.

### 3.11 Non-Effects

## 4. FEF-FGR-002-GF-011 — Sufficiency Requires Explicit Gap, Conflict, and Stop Treatment

| Field | Recorded Value |
|---|---|
| Version | 1.0 |
| Related RQ | FEF-FGR-002-RQ-012 |
| Evidence basis | EV-005–EV-008; EV-012; EV-014; EV-022–EV-027; EV-031 |
| Confidence | High for qualitative controls; Low for universal thresholds |
| Lifecycle state | Presented — Founder disposition pending |
| Founder decision need | Required for enduring sufficiency policy |

### 4.1 Finding Statement

Evidence sufficiency is specific to the Review Question and proposition. Before
an examination path proceeds, the record must disclose coverage, contrary or
superseding evidence, material limitations, uncertainty, unavailable evidence,
and gaps, and must state whether the path may proceed, proceed conditionally,
or must stop. Missing evidence may never be replaced by assumption.

### 4.2 Factual Basis

- EV-005 requires relevance, conflict, uncertainty, temporal, access, and
  permitted-use treatment.
- EV-008 prohibits creation of missing evidence and preference-based conflict
  resolution and supplies return, block, escalation, and reopening controls.
- EV-022 and EV-023 require explicit gaps and limitations before RQ or pack use.
- EV-031 demonstrates a bounded non-recovery conclusion without reconstructing
  the missing subject matter.

### 4.3 Interpretation

A gap can be non-blocking only when remaining evidence supports a narrower
proposition and the missing source could not silently be treated as support.
Material ambiguity or potentially outcome-changing absence requires pause,
return, or controlled supplementation.

### 4.4 Governance Risk

Generic completeness claims or assumption-filled gaps can contaminate findings
and later Founder decisions while hiding uncertainty.

### 4.5 Current Control State

Qualitative proceed/condition/block controls exist and were operated for D1
and evidence recovery. Universal thresholds and external completeness duties do
not.

### 4.6 Gap or Deficiency

OQ-006 and OQ-018 remain open. No E3 or project-specific completeness evidence
is present, and no conflict between authoritative external sources was tested.

### 4.7 Possible Treatment

Require RQ-specific coverage and limitation statements and an attributable
proceed/conditional/block treatment before examination and finding reliance.

### 4.8 Uncertainty and Limitations

No universal quantitative sufficiency threshold is evidenced. Validation
remains non-independent.

### 4.9 Cross-Domain Interfaces

D3 assurance and residual risk; D4 unavailable/access-controlled records; D8
OQ-006/OQ-018.

### 4.10 Candidate and Deferral Assessment

No Constitutional Candidate before Founder disposition and D7 review. No
Deferred Matter; evidence limitations remain active finding conditions and
interfaces.

### 4.11 Non-Effects

compliance requirements.

## 5. FEF-FGR-002-GF-012 — Frozen Evidence Baselines Require Versioned Change Control

| Field | Recorded Value |
|---|---|
| Version | 1.0 |
| Related RQ | FEF-FGR-002-RQ-013 |
| Evidence basis | EV-005–EV-008; EV-022–EV-023; EV-026–EV-029 |
| Confidence | High for freeze; Medium for unoperated successor/supplement paths |
| Lifecycle state | Presented — Founder disposition pending |
| Founder decision need | Potential |

### 5.1 Finding Statement

A frozen Evidence Pack is an immutable, versioned examination baseline rather
than a truth determination. Material additions, removals, replacements,
admissibility changes, or integrity changes require preservation of the prior
pack, an attributable successor or Supplemental Pack, impact assessment,
revalidation, re-freeze, and explicit session linkage before reliance.

### 5.2 Factual Basis

- EV-007 defines pack layers and requires version, freeze, manifest, integrity,
  limitations, and mappings.
- EV-008 prohibits informal evidence change during examination.
- Conditionally admitted EV-023 defines frozen version `1.0`, no in-place
  mutation, major versions for material change, supplements, SHA-256
  verification, and impact assessment.
- EV-026 through EV-029 demonstrate frozen-pack integrity, entry verification,
  and session use without post-freeze evidence.

### 5.3 Interpretation

Freeze makes later analysis reproducible. Change remains possible only through
a new attributable baseline that preserves earlier reliance.

### 5.4 Governance Risk

In-place mutation or uncited post-freeze evidence would make it impossible to
determine what a session actually examined.

### 5.5 Current Control State

Initial freeze and entry-use controls are operated. Successor and supplement
paths are specified but untested.

### 5.6 Gap or Deficiency

FEF-EPS-001 retains a draft-status header; no Supplemental Pack or successor
major version has been operated.

### 5.7 Possible Treatment

Retain the current review freeze control and test the first successor or
supplement path before generalising it as a framework-wide lifecycle rule.

### 5.8 Uncertainty and Limitations

Non-independent custody and validation remain disclosed. Framework-wide
lifecycle design belongs to D5.

### 5.9 Cross-Domain Interfaces

D3 pack assurance; D4 preservation; D5 lifecycle and supersession; D6
identifier/register administration.

### 5.10 Candidate and Deferral Assessment

No Constitutional Candidate before Founder disposition and D7 review. No

### 5.11 Non-Effects

### 6.1 Finding Statement

Every substantive governance output must trace to precise Evidence Records and
their permitted use through the RQ, session, finding, and any later Founder
Decision. Cross-domain reuse must preserve source identity, version, class,
authority, admissibility, limitations, and purpose mapping and must not elevate
authority, substitute a summary for the controlling source, or conflate
evidence with assertion or conclusion.

### 6.2 Factual Basis

- EV-005 Section 10 defines the evidence-to-downstream traceability chain.
- Conditionally admitted EV-023 requires item-to-record, pack, RQ, session,
  finding/non-finding, FD extract, and applicable OQ/candidate mapping and
  prohibits citing a pack alone for a GF.
- EV-024 through EV-030 demonstrate evidence-to-RQ, pack, session, finding,
  decision, OQ, and operational-checkpoint links.

### 6.3 Interpretation

Reuse is a new purpose mapping to the same controlled source, not a new source
or a transfer of authority from one domain to another.

### 6.4 Governance Risk

Broken or authority-expanding reuse can make an unsupported conclusion appear
evidenced and hide conditional or context-only treatment.

### 6.5 Current Control State

reuse and final-review reconciliation are not yet operated.

### 6.6 Gap or Deficiency

### 6.7 Possible Treatment

### 6.8 Uncertainty and Limitations

## 7. FEF-FGR-002-GF-014 — Evidence Custody Is Operational and Does Not Decide Meaning

| Field | Recorded Value |
|---|---|
| Version | 1.0 |
| Related RQ | FEF-FGR-002-RQ-015 |
| Evidence basis | EV-005–EV-008; EV-012; EV-014; EV-016–EV-019; EV-021–EV-031 |
| Confidence | High for current role boundary; Medium for durable continuity |
| Lifecycle state | Presented — Founder disposition pending |
| Founder decision need | Potential |

### 7.1 Finding Statement

Evidence custody is an operational responsibility for preserving evidence
identity, provenance, access state, integrity, versions, pack custody, and
traceability. It does not determine evidential meaning or exercise Founder
authority. Combined custody, analysis, recording, and validation must remain
capacity-labelled, disclosed as non-independent, supported by deterministic
checks and escalation, and subject to later independent revalidation where
required.

### 7.2 Factual Basis

- EV-005 separates Custodian, Analyst, Recorder, Validator, and Founder roles.
- EV-016 through EV-019 define current duties, prohibitions, RACI, conflict,
  escalation, and independence controls.
- EV-021 records the same-agent RA-002–RA-006 combination and compensating
  controls.
- EV-024 through EV-030 demonstrate custody, hashing, pack control, session use,
  and traceability; EV-031 demonstrates the risk of an unrecoverable chain.

### 7.3 Interpretation

One individual may hold multiple operational capacities only where each action
retains its separate authority boundary. Tool access, custody, authorship, or
validation does not confer interpretive or Founder authority.

### 7.4 Governance Risk

Combined roles increase error and self-validation risk. Missing handover or
continuity evidence could recreate the historical preservation failure.

### 7.5 Current Control State

The current review has explicit role boundaries, assignments, disclosures,
hashing, registers, and escalation. Independent validation remains a future
objective accepted under the D1 decision state referenced by EV-030.

### 7.6 Gap or Deficiency

- OQ-010 and OQ-011 remain open.
- Restricted-evidence handling, role handover, and successor custody are
  untested.
- No E3 source establishes external preservation duties.
- D4 retention, archival, deletion, and information lifecycle remain outside
  D2.

### 7.7 Possible Treatment

Retain the current operational boundary and compensating controls, preserve the
future independent-validation objective, and pass records-lifecycle design to
D4 without deciding it here.

### 7.8 Uncertainty and Limitations

The present same-agent arrangement cannot demonstrate independent assurance or
durable organisational continuity.

### 7.9 Cross-Domain Interfaces

D3 independence and assurance; D4 access, preservation, retention, archival,
and handover; D8 OQ-010/OQ-011.

### 7.10 Candidate and Deferral Assessment

No Constitutional Candidate before Founder disposition and D7 review. No
Deferred Matter: D4/D3 interfaces and existing OQs preserve the unresolved
subjects without inventing a deferral.

### 7.11 Non-Effects

This finding does not approve a retention schedule, archival architecture,
deletion rule, confidentiality policy, or independent-assurance claim.
