## 1. Purpose

This record provides a stable administrative order for presenting all seven
temporary candidates. It preserves the order of the validated candidate set
and prevents omission or duplication during Founder review.

The sequence does not rank candidates, create dependency, constrain Founder
judgement, or imply a disposition. The Founder may consider the candidates in
any order.

## 3. Decision Handling

For each candidate:

1. present the exact validated wording;
2. permit Founder observations and discussion;
3. leave the disposition unselected until attributable Founder wording is
   supplied;
4. record rationale and follow-up only when supplied by the Founder; and
5. preserve any relationship to other candidates exactly as directed without
   allocating canonical identifiers.

A merge, split, or amendment disposition would require the later governed
treatment applicable to that disposition. This preparation record does not
perform that treatment.

## 4. Completion Check

The Founder review is not recorded as complete merely because the candidates
have been presented. Completion requires attributable treatment for each of
the seven candidates through a separate recording action.

update the Review Question Register, identify or register evidence, create an
Evidence Register or Requirement Matrix, assemble an Evidence Pack, create a
session, commence examination, produce a Governance Finding, prepare a
review-scoped Founder Decision, amend the methodology, perform Framework
Evolution, or advance D4 beyond Founder review preparation.
