order, and GF identifier order. No other sequencing rationale is introduced.

meaning.

## 4. Separate Attribution

Each finding remains an independent review item and requires its own
attributable observations, discussion, disposition, conditions if any,
rationale, and follow-up.

## 5. Non-Effects

This sequencing performs no Founder Review, creates no decision or identifier,
amends no finding, resolves no Open Question or evidence gap, and introduces
no recommendation, dependency inference, constitutional interpretation, or
cross-finding synthesis.
