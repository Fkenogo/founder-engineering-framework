## 2. Phase 2 — Evidence Examination

### 2.1 Established Evidence

- RQ-018's mapped evidence set within FEF-FGR-002-EP-003 v1.0 §8.1
  contains exactly **one** item: EV-058 (D3 Quarantine Manifest and
  recovery baseline).
- EV-058's own content (`FEF-FGR-002-D3-QUARANTINE-MANIFEST.md`) records:
  six artefacts asserting DG-2-equivalent admission and Evidence Record
  registration were quarantined because they lacked a distinct Founder
  Decision record (§2); the exact prior tracked-file diffs were preserved
  in a patch file, and the affected registers were restored to the last
  Founder-authorised boundary (§3); nothing was deleted (§5); and reuse of
  any quarantined material requires a fresh, genuinely Founder-authorised
  admission gate (§6). EV-058 contains no statement of a contrary Founder
  view, a returned finding, an escalated disagreement, a rebuttal, or any
  party disputing a decision.
- FEF-FGR-002-EP-003 v1.0 §7.3 states directly: "No GF, FD, or session
  record across D1 or D2 documents a contrary Founder view, a returned
  finding, or an escalated disagreement... EV-058 is the only item mapped
  to RQ-018, and it evidences a detection-and-recovery episode, not a
  dissent episode; the underlying gap therefore persists inside this
  pack."
- FEF-FGR-002-EP-003 v1.0 §7.3 further states this absence is treated,
  per FEF-FGRC-001 §9.3, as a recorded limitation and is explicitly **not**
  read as proof that dissent was permitted, prevented, or irrelevant.

### 2.2 Supported Observations

1. RQ-018's own evidence-bounded examination universe supplies exactly
   one item, and that item is evidence of a *different* kind of event
   (an unauthorised-admission detection-and-recovery episode) than the
   event class the RQ asks about (challenge, rebuttal, dissent, escalated
   disagreement). The mapped evidence therefore does not directly answer
   "what assurance controls... are needed to enable and preserve
   attributable challenge, rebuttal, dissent, and unresolved
   disagreement" — it establishes only that no example of that event
   class exists to examine.
2. EV-058 does show that *some* form of detection-and-correction control
   operated successfully for a different failure mode (an admission
   proceeding without genuine Founder authority) — this is evidence about
   assurance-failure detection generally, not about the more specific
   challenge/dissent/rebuttal mechanisms RQ-018 asks about, and this
   finding does not extend it to that narrower question.
3. Because the sole mapped item does not itself supply a normative
   challenge/escalation control, an operated contrary-evidence example, a
   session/finding validation addressing dissent, or an attributable
   escalation example (all of which D3-RQS-002 §4 lists as the RQ's
   evidence need), RQ-018 is evidence-bounded to a **negative finding**:
   the frozen pack does not contain the material this RQ needs to be
   substantively answered.

### 2.3 Unsupported Assertions (explicitly excluded)

- That the absence of a dissent/challenge/rebuttal record proves no
  dissent or challenge ever occurred. FEF-FGRC-001 §9.3 and EP-003 §7.3
  explicitly forbid this inference, and it is not made here.
- That the absence proves dissent or challenge was *prevented* or
  *suppressed*. This is the symmetric error and is equally excluded.
- That EV-058's detection-and-recovery success is evidence that
  challenge/dissent mechanisms specifically are adequate. EV-058 is a
  different event class (§2.2.2); no such extension is made.
- That "what assurance controls... are needed" can be answered from this
  RQ's mapped evidence. No control is proposed here, because none is
  evidenced as operated, tested, or absent-and-needed within the mapped
  set; proposing one would exceed the evidence boundary.
- That any evidence-weight hierarchy, conflict-priority rule, speech or
  employment policy, D4 retention rule, or Founder disposition applies.
  These are RQ-018's own declared exclusions (D3-RQS-002 §4).

### 2.4 Uncertainty

Whether the absence of a dissent/challenge/rebuttal record reflects (a) a
review process in which no attributable disagreement has yet arisen, (b)
an absence of controls capable of preserving such disagreement if it
arose, or (c) some other cause, cannot be determined from RQ-018's mapped
evidence. The mapped evidence supports none of these three explanations
over the others.
