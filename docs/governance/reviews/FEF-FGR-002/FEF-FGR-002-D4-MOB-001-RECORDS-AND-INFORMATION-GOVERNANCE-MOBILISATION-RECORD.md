## 3. D4 Review Purpose

Subject to separate Founder mobilisation authority, D4 will examine the
governance controls required to preserve auditable records, decisions,
evidence, versions, and organisational knowledge under explicit controls.

The approved scope comprises:

- governed record types;
- registers and custodianship;
- retention and archival;
- access, confidentiality, privacy, and security;
- legal hold and deletion;
- institutional and organisational knowledge; and
- information lifecycle and integrity checkpoints.

The intended operational purpose is to examine required records, custody,
access, retention, and archival. This purpose does not predetermine a Review
Question, evidence requirement, finding, decision, or implementation design.

## 4. Domain Boundaries

### 4.1 Included Boundary

D4 is bounded to governance treatment of:

- which classes of governance records require controlled preservation;
- responsibilities associated with registers and records custody;
- retention, archival, legal-hold, and deletion governance;
- access, confidentiality, privacy, and security governance for controlled
  information;
- preservation of institutional knowledge needed for governance continuity;
- information lifecycle controls and integrity checkpoints; and
- records-domain obligations needed to support auditable governance.

These are domain boundaries only. They are not candidate Review Questions and
have no RQ identifier, wording, evidence need, lifecycle state, or admission
effect.

### 4.2 Exclusions

D4 mobilisation preparation does not:

- establish, select, design, or implement a records or information system;
- define technical storage architecture, tooling, schemas, automation,
  security controls, or operational procedures;
- reopen, reinterpret, or modify D1, D2, or D3;
- alter evidence meaning, admissibility, weighting, or frozen Evidence Packs;
- establish the D5 framework lifecycle, versioning, applicability,
  supersession, withdrawal, or controlled-evolution model;
- redesign D6 identifiers, taxonomy, programme architecture, roadmaps, or
  administrative structures;
- extract D7 constitutional principles or amend a Constitution or standard;
- answer, amend, or disposition an Open Question under D8;
- perform legal, privacy, confidentiality, or security advice;
- perform Framework Evolution or commence FEF-CCF-001; or
- authorise downstream work by implication.
