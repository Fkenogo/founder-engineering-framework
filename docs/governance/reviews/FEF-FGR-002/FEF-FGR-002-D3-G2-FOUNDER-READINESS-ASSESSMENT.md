## 6. Risk of Deciding Now

The principal risk is over-generalisation: an unconditioned disposition
of any of GF-015 through GF-023 could be read as settling a general
independence standard, revalidation-trigger rule, outcome vocabulary,
residual-risk model, assurance-cycle model, or AI-assurance-boundary
standard that the mapped evidence does not establish. A second risk
specific to this domain is treating the disclosed non-independent
condition (the same acting capacity prepared, examined, validated, and
packaged this material) as if it were independent assurance; it is not,
and this assessment does not cure that condition.

That risk can be managed only by the Founder's exact scope and
conditions. This assessment does not supply those conditions or accept
the risk.

## 7. Risk of Delaying

Delay would leave GF-015 through GF-023 undispositioned and the RQ-018
gap and EV-058/EV-059 contradictions without attributable Founder
treatment. Current FEF-FGR-002 operations remain governed by their
existing sources, but D3's own traceability chain would continue to stop
at the GF node, and later-domain reuse of D3's assurance conclusions
would lack attributable Founder input.

## 9. Independent Validation Condition

The same acting capacity prepared, examined, validated, and packaged the
S03 findings and this readiness assessment. This is not independent
assurance. The Founder may disposition a finding, condition reliance,
return it for evidence, require independent revalidation, reject it, or
validly defer it. This assessment does not select among those options.

## 10. Verdict

**Ready with Conditions.**

All nine findings are sufficiently mature for Founder consideration
within their exact evidence-bounded scope. GF-020 and GF-022 require
particular caution because their central observations (a repeatable
assurance cycle; a generally reliable deterministic-check pattern) are
each evidenced only once or are self-referentially limited. GF-017
requires particular caution of a different kind: it is a negative/gap
finding, and no disposition of it substitutes for the still-open RQ-018
gap. GF-015, GF-016, GF-018, GF-019, GF-021, and GF-023 are ready for
bounded disposition; none supports an unrestricted, framework-wide
generalisation beyond its recorded limitations.
