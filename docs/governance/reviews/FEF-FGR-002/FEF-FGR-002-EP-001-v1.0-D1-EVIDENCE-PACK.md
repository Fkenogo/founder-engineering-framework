## 2. Scope Statement

### 2.1 Purpose

Provide the first controlled evidence baseline for future examination of the eight admitted D1 Review Questions.

### 2.2 Included Subjects

- attributable Founder authority and decisions;
- approved Founder Governance Review instruments;
- recorded architectural and programme authority boundaries;
- unresolved authority-related Open Questions;
- draft RGS authority assumptions and status limitations;
- historical evidence-gap boundary;
- current operational roles, authority, independence, responsibility, and assignment controls.

### 2.3 Exclusions

- unrecoverable historical GF-001–GF-036 and FD-001–FD-033 content;
- any evidence not registered as EV-001 through EV-021;
- external legal, regulatory, professional, or project-authority evidence not yet identified;
- analysis, interpretations, answers, findings, decision options, or constitutional text;
- post-freeze material unless admitted through supplement or successor-pack control.

### 2.4 Known Gaps

- no approved Constitution exists;
- no external authority source has been identified for the initial pack;
- historical review substance is permanently unavailable from identified sources;
- some source metadata still reflects draft preparation status while separate attributable decisions establish approval;
- current validation and custody roles are held by the same Codex agent.

### 2.5 Intended Use

Support future, bounded D1 examination. Freeze confirms identity, scope, manifest, admissibility treatment, and integrity—not the truth of every proposition or any RQ answer.
