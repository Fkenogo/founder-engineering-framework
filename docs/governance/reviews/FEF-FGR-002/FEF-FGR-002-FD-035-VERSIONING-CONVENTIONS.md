## 1. Finding Reference

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-033 — Multiple Traceable Versioning Practices Are Operated, but No Approved Uniform Convention or Cross-Artefact Reconciliation Is Established**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

**Founder Observations**

FEF needs dependable version traceability, but it does not need one numbering
convention for every artefact type.

**Discussion**

The Founder considered GF-033 and its authorised lean conditions. No additional
discussion is attributed.

**Disposition**

Accept with Conditions

**Conditions**

Common conditions applying to GF-031 through GF-036:

1. Acceptance confirms the finding, not a proposed solution.
2. Any future FEF control must remain a minimum cross-project foundation.
3. Project-specific lifecycle detail remains under project authority unless a
   universal requirement is demonstrated.
4. Founder-reserved authority cannot be delegated by implication.
5. Existing valid work and historical records must not be retrospectively
   altered without explicit authority.
6. No repository-wide reconciliation, renumbering, reclassification or
   migration programme is authorised.
7. Implementation design requires separately governed follow-up after Founder
   Decision issuance.
8. All eight applicable Open Questions remain open unless separately resolved.

Finding-specific Conditions:

1. Different artefact classes may use different versioning conventions.
2. Every controlled artefact must make its permanent identity, current version
   and governing status unambiguous.
3. Approval or effective information and change history must be recorded where
   applicable.
4. Decisions and delivery activities must identify the exact governing version.
5. Existing records must not be renumbered for cosmetic uniformity.

**Rationale**

Traceability matters more than superficial numbering consistency.

**Follow-up Actions**

Develop minimum version-identification requirements by artefact class after
Founder Decision issuance.

## 3. Exact Finding Scope

**Confidence: High for the observed practices; insufficient for an approved
uniform convention.** The exact historical records support the programme,
register, and draft-instrument examples. Their authority and acquisition
limits, the absence of a reconciliation rule, and open OQ-014 prevent
generalisation.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-034 and the six mapped frozen records. It does
not decide RQ-035 through RQ-037, D6 administration, D7 constitutional effect,
or Framework Evolution.

## 4. Exact Finding Non-Effects
