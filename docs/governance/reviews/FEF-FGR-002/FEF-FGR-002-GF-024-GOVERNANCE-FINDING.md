## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> Which classes of governance record, if any, require controlled creation and preservation within FEF, and what minimum characteristics are necessary for each class to remain attributable, understandable, authoritative, and auditable without prescribing an implementation system?

This examination used only the six Evidence Records mapped to RQ-025 in
EP-004 v1.0: EV-005, EV-007, EV-008, EV-013, EV-072, and EV-073. No
unmapped evidence was introduced.

The evidence qualifications remain controlling:

- EV-005 is E2 Admitted within the Founder-approved review Charter only;
- EV-007 is E2 Admitted within the Founder-approved review Plan only;
- EV-008 is E2 Admitted as the review execution-rule baseline only;
- EV-013 is E2 Conditionally Admitted and remains a Draft v0.2,
  Founder Review Required, Not Approved research-specific proposal;
- EV-072 is E2 Conditionally Admitted as a mutable, non-authoritative
  programme index whose source records control; and
- EV-073 is E4 Admitted as one validated D3 operational traceability
  example, not as a universal model or current programme-state authority.

## 2. Evidence-Supported Observations

1. EV-005 identifies review-scoped record types through canonical
   identifier patterns for Reviews, Sessions, Evidence Records, Review
   Questions, Governance Findings, Founder Decisions, Validation Findings,
   and Deferred Matters. It requires identifiers to be unique, immutable,
   reserved if withdrawn, status-bearing rather than deleted, and cited
   canonically.
2. EV-005 prescribes type-specific control fields. Its Evidence Record
   controls include identity, source, provenance, dates, version, integrity,
   class, authority and scope, access, precise references, relationships,
   conflicts, limitations, uncertainty, admissibility, validation, and
   retention location. Its required traceability chain links Evidence
   Record, Review Question, Session Record, Governance Finding, Founder
   Decision when required, Open Question disposition, any later candidate
   or domain-standard implication, and separate downstream work.
3. EV-007 identifies the operational artefact classes required for this
   review and requires evidence packs to have identity, manifest,
   Evidence-Record citation, admissibility and limitation treatment,
   version and freeze control, integrity, preserved prior versions, and
   RQ/session mappings. These are review controls, not a general FEF
   records standard.
4. EV-008 operates linked-record and version-preservation rules:
   iteration uses new versions or linked records, preserves identifiers
   and history, updates traceability, and does not silently bypass gates.
   These rules are bounded to review execution.
5. EV-072 shows an operated programme index using controlled path, document
   type, lifecycle status, and governance-effect fields. Because EV-072 is
   mutable and non-authoritative, the listing demonstrates current index
   practice only. It does not establish that any listed class or field is
   required, complete, authoritative, or sufficient.
6. EV-073 shows one operated record that preserves identity, version,
   lifecycle, validation, and end-to-end relationships across RQ,
   Evidence, EP-003, S03, Governance Finding, and Founder Decision. It
   demonstrates auditable traceability in the closed D3 domain only.
7. Across the approved review instruments and the two operated examples,
   recurring characteristics are visible: unique identity; controlled
   source or location; version, date, status, or history; authority and
   scope; relationships and traceability; integrity or validation;
   limitations and uncertainty; and preservation. The mapped evidence
   supports these as recurring characteristics of the examined
   review-scoped records. It does not support treating every characteristic
   as mandatory for every FEF record class.

## 3. Evidence Gaps

| Gap | Evidence-Bounded Treatment |
|---|---|
| No approved FEF-wide governance-record taxonomy or records standard is present in the mapped evidence | The review-scoped types in EV-005 and EV-007 are not elevated into a universal taxonomy |
| No authoritative minimum characteristic set covering every FEF governance record class is present | Recurring characteristics are reported as observations, not converted into universal requirements |
| EV-072 does not uniformly represent ownership, version, or integrity metadata for every listed document | Its index practice is not treated as complete, necessary, sufficient, or authoritative |
| EV-073 is one closed-domain example | Its operated D3 traceability treatment is not generalised to every domain or record class |
| No independent examination or validation capacity is evidenced for this loop | Non-independent operational combination is disclosed and deterministic source, mapping, and fingerprint checks are retained as compensating controls |

The gaps remain open. Absence of an approved taxonomy or universal
characteristic set is not interpreted as proof that either is unnecessary,
prohibited, or already implicit.

## 4. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The mapped evidence does not itself decide:

- whether the recurring review-scoped characteristics should become an
  enduring FEF-wide baseline;
- which governance record classes, if any, should be controlled across FEF
  beyond the present review;
- which characteristics should be universal and which should remain
  type-specific; or
- whether a later governed activity should establish an authoritative
  taxonomy or records standard.

These are matters for later Founder judgement. This finding selects no
answer and makes no recommendation.

## 5. Conclusion

FEF-FGR-002's approved review instruments expressly identify multiple
review-scoped record types and prescribe type-specific controls that
preserve identity, authority, integrity, limitations, history, and
traceability. EV-072 and EV-073 show that record categorisation and
end-to-end traceability are also operated in the repository. The mapped
corpus therefore supports the existence of controlled, auditable
review-scoped record practice.

The same corpus does not evidence an approved FEF-wide governance-record
taxonomy or one authoritative minimum characteristic set applying to every
FEF record class. EV-013 contains broader research-specific artefact and
metadata proposals, but it is expressly Not Approved; EV-072 is a
non-authoritative index; and EV-073 is a single domain-specific example.
Whether to establish enduring FEF-wide classes or minimum characteristics
therefore requires Founder judgement and cannot be inferred from current
practice.

## 6. Contrary Evidence

No mapped source substantively contradicts the conclusion. The broader
record terminology and controls proposed in EV-013 could appear to supply a
general answer, but its Draft v0.2, Founder Review Required, Not Approved
status is controlling. EV-072's breadth could appear to constitute a
taxonomy, but its qualification expressly limits it to a mutable,
non-authoritative index whose source records control. Those sources qualify
rather than displace the conclusion.

No absence outside EP-004 or outside RQ-025's six-item mapping is claimed.

## 7. Limitations and Uncertainty

- The finding is limited to the six mapped EP-004 v1.0 Evidence Records.
- It does not assess completeness of the repository's full document corpus.
- It does not infer that repetition creates governance authority.
- It does not decide preservation periods, archival transfer, access,
  deletion, custody, continuity, or information-lifecycle rules addressed
  by RQ-026 through RQ-031.
- It does not infer D5 lifecycle rules or D6 taxonomy, identifier-
  administration, or programme architecture.
- OQ-023 remains open and unchanged; its partial mapping to RQ-025 is not a
  disposition.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.

## 8. Confidence Statement

**Confidence: Moderate.** The existence of explicit review-scoped record
types, type-specific controls, and one operated end-to-end traceability
example is directly supported by the mapped sources. Confidence is bounded
because the two broader sources are authority-limited, the operated
traceability evidence is domain-specific, and no approved FEF-wide taxonomy
or universal minimum set is mapped.

## 9. Scope

This finding is scoped strictly to FEF-FGR-002-RQ-025 and EV-005, EV-007,
EV-008, EV-013, EV-072, and EV-073 as frozen in EP-004 v1.0. It draws no
conclusion about RQ-026 through RQ-031 and states no Founder recommendation.
