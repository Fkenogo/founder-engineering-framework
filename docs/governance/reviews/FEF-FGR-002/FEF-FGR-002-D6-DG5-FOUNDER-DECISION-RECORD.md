This record captures the attributable Founder decision for GF-037 through
GF-042 exactly as supplied by the Founder, following the live Founder
Review facilitated under FEF-FGR-002-D6-DG5-FRP-001. For each finding the
Founder supplied one Disposition and one Founder Reflection. Section 3
records all twelve fields (six Dispositions, six Reflections) without

## 3. Finding-by-Finding Founder Decision

### FEF-FGR-002-GF-037 — Every Existing Role and Authority Instrument Is Scoped to This Review Only

**Disposition**

Accept

**Founder Reflection**

Framework-level administrative responsibilities should emerge only where
operational experience across multiple Founder projects demonstrates that
shared administration materially improves governance integrity.

Until then, administrative authority and stewardship remain with the
individual Founder projects, with the Framework providing only the minimum
shared governance necessary to preserve trust, consistency, and
engineering discipline.

---

### FEF-FGR-002-GF-038 — Update Triggers and a Propagation Rule Exist and Are Operated, but No Standing Verification Responsibility Is Evidenced

**Disposition**

Accept

**Founder Reflection**

The Framework presently relies on governance reviews as its verification
mechanism.

A permanent verification capability should only be considered if repeated
operational experience across multiple Founder projects demonstrates that
review-based verification is no longer sufficient or proportionate.

---

### FEF-FGR-002-GF-039 — A Narrative Dependency Register Exists and Is Operated Across Documents Without Halting Programme Progress

**Disposition**

Accept

**Founder Reflection**

The Framework should continue treating dependency management as a
mechanism for sequencing work rather than restricting progress.

Dependencies should preserve governance integrity while allowing
independent work to proceed wherever constitutional boundaries permit.

Governance should coordinate readiness rather than unnecessarily delay
execution.

---

### FEF-FGR-002-GF-040 — Explicit Precedence Notes Provide a Minimal, Operating Administrative Coherence Control

**Disposition**

Accept

**Founder Reflection**

Administrative coherence should be achieved through clear ownership,
consistent repository architecture, and authoritative programme structure
rather than comprehensive relationship cataloguing.

Only those relationships necessary to preserve governance integrity should
be documented.

The remainder should emerge naturally from sound architectural design.

---

### FEF-FGR-002-GF-041 — Simplicity and Proportionality Were Named as Review-Design Intent

**Disposition**

Accept

**Founder Reflection**

Administrative simplicity should remain an outcome of sound governance
design rather than an objective pursued in isolation.

The Framework should encourage proportional governance, applying only the
level of administration necessary to preserve integrity, traceability, and
trust while allowing individual Founder projects to adopt additional
controls where their operational context requires them.

---

### FEF-FGR-002-GF-042 — Founder-Stated Intent Favours an Evidence-Based Maturation Process for Operational Lessons

**Disposition**

Accept

**Founder Reflection**

Operational lessons should be preserved as knowledge rather than
immediately adopted as Framework administration.

Administrative practices should emerge only after demonstrating
transferability across multiple Founder projects and showing that they
materially improve governance integrity without unnecessarily reducing
project autonomy.

The Framework should learn before it legislates.
