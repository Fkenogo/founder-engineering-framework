## 3. Basis

The sequence reproduces the existing canonical RQ order, S05 Evidence
Examination Loop order, and Governance Finding identifier order. It does not
rank findings or introduce another rationale. The Founder may use another
order without changing any finding's status or meaning.

## 4. Separate Attribution

Each finding remains a separate review item and requires its own attributable
observations, discussion, disposition, conditions if any, rationale, and
follow-up actions.

## 5. Non-Effects

This sequencing performs no live Founder Review, creates no decision or
identifier, amends no finding, resolves no Open Question or evidence gap, and
introduces no recommendation, dependency inference, constitutional
interpretation, lifecycle model, or cross-finding synthesis.
