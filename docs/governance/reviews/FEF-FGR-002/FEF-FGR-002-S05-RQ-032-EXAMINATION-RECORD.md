## 3. Examination Results by Analytical Classification

### 3.1 Directly Established by Evidence

1. EV-013 directly records one pre-approval state combination for that
   instrument: `Draft v0.2`, `Founder Review Required`, and `Not Approved`.
   Because EV-013 is conditionally admitted, this establishes only the state
   of that draft, not an approved general model.
2. EV-075 and EV-076 directly establish one bounded domain-mobilisation chain:
   preparation did not itself make mobilisation effective; an attributable
   Founder `Approve with Conditions` disposition confirmed the transition to
   `Mobilised — Effective`, subject to four conditions.
3. EV-074 directly establishes one bounded milestone example in which an
   attributable Founder `Approve with Conditions` disposition was recorded
   and separately validation-passed.
4. EV-005 reserves Founder decisions to the Founder and separates operational
   review roles. EV-075 expressly distinguishes preparation authority from
   Founder mobilisation authority. None of EV-005, EV-007, EV-008, EV-074,
   EV-075, or EV-076 records a non-Founder delegate confirming an
   instrument-level state transition.
5. EV-008 directly states that DG labels are rule labels and do not prove that
   a gate occurred. EV-077 separately records `Entry-Gate State` and `Session
   State`. These controls establish that gate verdicts and session states are
   distinct control axes and are not, without further evidence, governance-
   instrument lifecycle states.
6. EV-072 records materially varied repository labels at the governed
   acquisition point. Its own qualification makes the index non-authoritative
   and mutable; the labels therefore establish observed usage, not an approved
   vocabulary or equivalence among terms.

### 3.2 Reasonably Supported

1. The mapped evidence reasonably supports a bounded pattern containing:
   pre-approval drafting/review labels; an attributable Founder approval or
   approval-with-conditions act; and, in the D5 mobilisation example, a later
   effective state. The support is for those examples only, not a universal
   sequence.
2. The evidence reasonably supports that the Founder confirmed the two
   examined operational transitions represented by EV-074 and EV-075/EV-076.
3. The evidence reasonably supports keeping at least four concepts separate:
   instrument state, decision-gate outcome, assurance verdict, and session
   state. Similar words appearing across those axes do not establish that the
   underlying controls are interchangeable.

### 3.3 Unsupported

The mapped evidence does not support:

- an approved FEF-wide lifecycle or status vocabulary;
- a mandatory universal sequence through Draft, Review Required, Approved,
  Active, Effective, Closed, Withdrawn, or Superseded;
- equivalence between `Approved`, `Active`, `Mobilised`, and `Effective`;
- an operated instrument-level `Withdrawn` or `Superseded` example;
- a general rule identifying which transitions may be confirmed by delegated
  non-Founder authority; or
- a conclusion that role assignment to RA-002–RA-006 delegates Founder-
  reserved decision authority.

### 3.4 Contradictory or Qualifying Matters

No mapped source directly contradicts the bounded examples above. Material
qualifications remain:

- the sources use overlapping or inconsistent labels without reconciling
  them;
- `Approve` appears both as decision-gate vocabulary and as an attributable
  Founder disposition, but the mapped evidence does not establish that these
  are one lifecycle act;
- EV-013 could appear to provide a draft-to-approval model, but its `Not
  Approved` status precludes reliance on its proposed content; and
- EV-072 could appear to supply a vocabulary catalogue, but its conditional,
  mutable, non-authoritative treatment precludes that conclusion.

### 3.5 Uncertain

It remains uncertain whether FEF intends a common instrument lifecycle, which
observed labels are equivalent or sequential, and whether any
instrument-level transition may be delegated to a non-Founder authority.
Absence of a mapped delegated example is not evidence that delegation is
prohibited or impossible.
