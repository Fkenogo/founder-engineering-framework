## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What residual cross-project programme sequencing, dependency
> administration, and release readiness relationships, if any, remain
> unsettled after D1, D4, and D5, and what minimum administrative
> treatment — one that preserves programme readiness and sequencing
> rather than constraining programme progress — do they require, without
> deciding the disposition of any specific blocked item?

This finding uses only EV-080 (restricted to §4, the Reconciled
Work-Package Register) and EV-088 (restricted to §7 and §8) as frozen for
RQ-040 in EP-006 v1.0/MAN-001. No unmapped evidence or later live
administrative version was used. FEF-P0-004's disposition is not decided
by this finding.

## 2. Finding

The mapped evidence establishes that a structured, populated dependency
and sequencing capability exists and has been operated, at a modest but
real level: EV-080's Reconciled Work-Package Register records 22 work
items with current status (12 Completed, 3 Blocked, 7 Pending) and a
free-text dependency note for each; EV-088 independently references one
of those items — FEF-P0-004, Blocked under OQ-016 — in terms consistent
with EV-080, demonstrating that dependency information is actually
carried and reused across governed documents, not recorded once and
left isolated. The same source also shows that this treatment has not,
in the instances observed, constrained overall programme progress:
Blocked and Pending items have coexisted with continued forward
sequencing through D1–D5 closure and the D6 gates completed to date.

The evidence does not establish more than this. Applying the
documented-intent / administrative-capability / demonstrated-operation /
verified-operation distinction: what exists is a capability
(the register) that has been operated (populated, referenced
consistently at least once); what is **not** evidenced is any
**verification** of that consistency — no mechanism confirms the
register remains accurate or that cross-document references stay
aligned; that alignment is, in the mapped evidence, an observed instance,
not an attested property. Nor does the evidence establish any minimum
administrative treatment for relationships spanning multiple separate
Founder projects (none yet exists to have such a relationship with), or
any release-readiness criteria beyond the bare status field "Framework
release: None."

## 4. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source directly contradicts another. Two qualifications
prevent overstatement:

- the consistency observed between EV-080 and EV-088 regarding
  FEF-P0-004 is demonstrated operation, not verified operation — no
  check or reconciliation process is evidenced as producing that
  consistency, so it must not be read as proof of a standing
  verification capability; and
- "cross-project" is evidenced here only as relationships among FEF's
  own internal work packages and legacy plans, not as relationships
  among multiple separate Founder-adopter projects.

## 5. Limitations, Uncertainty, and Open Questions

- Confidence is high that a populated, structured dependency register
  exists and has been operated, including at least one instance of
  consistent cross-document reference.
- Confidence is high that blocked and pending items have, to date,
  coexisted with continued programme progress rather than halting it.
- No positive or negative confidence is assigned to whether cross-document
  consistency is verified by any mechanism — this is recorded as
  genuinely uncertain, consistent with the Founder's direction not to
  infer absence where evidence is inconclusive.
- No positive confidence is assigned to any cross-project (multi-project)
  administrative treatment or release-readiness criteria, because neither
  is evidenced to exist at any level.
- OQ-016 remains open and unchanged (direct interface — administrative
  consequence of FEF-P0-004's blocked state only). This finding neither
  answers nor dispositions it, and does not decide FEF-P0-004's
  disposition.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.

## 6. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The evidence does not decide whether a formal dependency-verification
mechanism should be established, what minimum cross-project treatment
would be proportionate once other Founder projects adopt the Framework,
or what release-readiness criteria should eventually apply. Those remain
matters for separately governed Founder judgement or later authorised
work. This finding makes no recommendation.

## 7. Scope and Confidence

**Confidence: High for the bounded observation that a dependency register
exists, is operated, and has coexisted with continued programme progress;
not applicable to any cross-project model or release-readiness standard,
because neither is evidenced to assess; genuinely inconclusive for
whether cross-document consistency is a verified property.** The directly
cited register entries and the directly cited cross-document reference to
FEF-P0-004 strongly support the bounded conclusion. The absence of any
second adopting project and any release-readiness criterion is the direct
and sufficient basis for those two negative findings.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-040 and the two mapped frozen records. It
does not decide RQ-038 or RQ-039 (already answered in GF-037 and GF-038),
RQ-041 through RQ-043, or any D7 interface. It does not dispose of
FEF-P0-004.

## 8. Non-Effects

This finding does not adopt or recommend a dependency-verification
mechanism, cross-project administrative treatment, or release-readiness
standard. It does not modify frozen evidence or evidence treatment,
resolve OQ-016, dispose of FEF-P0-004, examine RQ-041 through RQ-043,
issue a Founder disposition or Founder Decision, close S06, or commence
D7. It remains Presented and pending Founder disposition.
