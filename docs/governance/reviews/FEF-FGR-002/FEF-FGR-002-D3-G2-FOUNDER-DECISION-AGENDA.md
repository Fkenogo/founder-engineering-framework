## 3. Permitted Disposition Vocabulary

Each item may later receive one of the existing FEF-FGR-002 permitted
treatments:

- Accept;
- Accept with Conditions;
- Return for Additional Evidence;
- Require Independent Revalidation;
- Reject;
- Defer, only with attributable rationale, dependencies, and a
  reconsideration trigger.

This agenda does not add a new lifecycle label for GF-017's negative/gap
status; it uses the same controlled vocabulary as every other finding. A
bounded treatment may be expressed through exact Founder conditions
without changing the controlled vocabulary.

## 4. Review Boundary

The Founder may change the consideration order. Each finding must retain
a separate attributable disposition because its evidence, conditions, and
later-domain interfaces differ. Dispositioning GF-017 does not resolve
the RQ-018 gap; that gap remains open regardless of the disposition
selected.

## 5. Non-Effects

This agenda selects no option, drafts or allocates no Founder Decision,
modifies no finding or Open Question, creates no candidate or deferral,
and creates no constitutional or downstream authority effect.
