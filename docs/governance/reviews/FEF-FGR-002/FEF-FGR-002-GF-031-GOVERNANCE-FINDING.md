## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What drafting, review, and operational states, if any, do FEF governance
> instruments pass through, and by what Founder or delegated authority is
> each state transition confirmed?

This finding uses only EV-005, EV-007, EV-008, EV-013, EV-072, EV-074,
EV-075, EV-076, and EV-077 as frozen for RQ-032 in EP-005 v2.0. EV-013 and
EV-072 remain Conditionally Admitted. EV-072 is used only at the historical
RQ-032 acquisition state. No unmapped evidence or later live administrative
version was used.

## 2. Finding

The mapped evidence establishes three bounded kinds of instrument-state
example:

1. EV-013 records its own pre-approval combination of `Draft v0.2`, `Founder
   Review Required`, and `Not Approved`. Its unapproved content does not
   establish policy.
2. EV-074 records one attributable Founder milestone disposition of `Approve
   with Conditions`, followed by separately recorded validation.
3. EV-075 and EV-076 record one domain-mobilisation transition in which
   preparation did not create operative effect and an attributable Founder
   `Approve with Conditions` disposition confirmed `Mobilised — Effective`,
   subject to four conditions.

EV-005, EV-007, EV-008, and EV-077 show that operational roles, gate outcomes,
validation results, and session states are separately controlled. EV-072
shows varied status words in repository practice, but its mutable,
non-authoritative status prevents those labels from becoming an approved
model merely through listing or repetition.

The mapped evidence therefore supports a bounded conclusion: the Founder is
the evidenced confirming authority for the two examined approval/effect
transitions, and preparation or role assignment is not itself that
confirmation. It does not evidence a complete FEF-wide lifecycle, a universal
ordering or equivalence of status words, or a delegated non-Founder authority
confirming an instrument-level transition.

## 3. Evidence Gaps and Unsupported Matters

| Matter | Finding treatment |
|---|---|
| Approved common instrument lifecycle/status vocabulary | Not evidenced; no model adopted |
| Universal ordering or equivalence of Draft, Review Required, Approved, Active, Mobilised, Effective, Closed, Withdrawn, and Superseded | Unsupported |
| Operated instrument-level Withdrawn or Superseded transition | Not evidenced |
| Delegated non-Founder confirmation of an instrument-level state transition | Not evidenced; absence is not read as prohibition |
| Assignment of RA-002–RA-006 roles as delegation of Founder authority | Unsupported; functional assignment remains distinct from authority delegation |
| Completeness or sufficiency of labels listed in EV-072 | Unsupported because the index is mutable and non-authoritative |

These gaps are part of the answer at finding level; they are not silently
filled by inference.

## 4. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source directly contradicts the bounded examples. Four
qualifications prevent overstatement:

- EV-013 is Not Approved and supports only its own status header;
- EV-072 is a mutable, non-authoritative index whose source records control;
- `Approve` appears in different control contexts without evidence that the
  acts are equivalent; and
- EV-074 and EV-075/EV-076 are one milestone and one domain-mobilisation
  example, not a complete lifecycle corpus.

## 5. Limitations, Uncertainty, and Open Questions

- Confidence is high that the bounded examples and their attributable Founder
  authority are accurately represented.
- Confidence is moderate that the mapped evidence demonstrates a deliberate
  separation among instrument, gate, assurance, and session state axes.
- No positive confidence is assigned to a universal lifecycle, vocabulary,
  sequencing rule, or delegated-authority model because the evidence does not
  establish one.
- OQ-004 and OQ-022 remain open and unchanged. This finding neither answers nor
  dispositions them.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.

## 6. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The evidence does not decide whether FEF should adopt an enduring common
lifecycle, which status terms should be standardised, how states should be
ordered, or whether and how any transition authority should be delegated.
Those remain matters for separately governed Founder judgement or later
authorised work. This finding makes no recommendation.

## 7. Scope and Confidence

**Confidence: Moderate to High for the bounded finding; insufficient for a
universal model.** The directly attributable records strongly support the
specific examples and the Founder-confirmation conclusion. The conditional
sources, small number of transition examples, vocabulary variation, and lack
of any delegated example prevent generalisation.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-032 and the nine mapped frozen records. It
does not decide RQ-033 through RQ-037 or any D6/D7 interface.

## 8. Non-Effects

This finding does not adopt or recommend a lifecycle vocabulary, approval
model, delegation model, register redesign, or constitutional definition. It
does not modify frozen evidence or evidence treatment, resolve OQ-004 or
OQ-022, examine RQ-033 through RQ-037, issue a Founder disposition or Founder
Decision, close S05 or D5, or commence D6 or D7. It remains Presented and
pending Founder disposition.
