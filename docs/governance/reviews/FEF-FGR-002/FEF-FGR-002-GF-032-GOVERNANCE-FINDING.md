## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> When a FEF governance instrument is approved, amended, or withdrawn, does
> it apply retrospectively to work already underway, only to work commenced
> after its effective date, or under some other transitional rule, and who
> decides which rule applies?

This finding uses only EV-009, EV-010, EV-012, EV-013, EV-070, EV-073,
EV-078, and EV-079 as frozen for RQ-033 in EP-005 v2.0. EV-013 remains
Conditionally Admitted. EV-078 remains correction/recovery-only and is never
treated as an ordinary amendment. No unmapped evidence or later live version
was used.

## 2. Finding

The mapped evidence establishes four bounded treatments relevant to
applicability and transition:

1. EV-009 records scoped Founder architectural decisions as current
   constraints and direction while assigning no effective status or effective
   date and supplying no approval date.
2. EV-010 records a Founder-approved pilot whose commencement is tied to
   completion and validation of a named work package. That is one
   event-triggered, pilot-specific treatment, not a universal rule.
3. EV-078 records a Founder-authorised recovery that restored active controls
   to the last authorised boundary, quarantined unauthorised work, and treated
   the affected identifiers as not currently allocated while preserving the
   material for audit and possible later reuse.
4. EV-079 records an explicit correction that superseded false closure and
   authority claims for active reliance while preserving the original version
   in repository history.

EV-070 requires version, authority, limitation, and purpose traceability
through later outputs and decisions; EV-073 demonstrates one version-stamped
D3 traceability chain. These controls support preservation of what governed a
decision, but they do not select a transitional rule.

The evidence therefore supports a bounded conclusion: FEF practice contains
decision-specific commencement and defect-correction treatments, and the
Founder is the evidenced authority behind the mapped architectural and
recovery examples. It does not establish a general retrospective,
prospective, hybrid, or grandfathering rule for valid work underway when an
instrument is approved, ordinarily amended, or withdrawn. It also does not
establish delegated non-Founder authority to select such a rule.

These gaps are part of the answer at finding level and remain visible rather

## 4. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source directly contradicts the bounded examples. Four
qualifications prevent overstatement:

- EV-009's current-constraint treatment does not say how prior or underway
  work is affected.
- EV-010's commencement trigger is scoped to one pilot and contains no
  retrospective or grandfathering treatment.
- EV-078 and EV-079 concern invalid or inaccurate prior states, not ordinary
  amendment or withdrawal of a valid instrument.
- EV-013 is Not Approved and its proposed content has no current authority.

## 5. Limitations, Uncertainty, and OQ-017

- Confidence is high that the four bounded examples and their recorded
  authority/scope are accurately represented.
- Confidence is moderate that the examples demonstrate decision-specific
  treatment rather than one operated common transitional model.
- No positive confidence is assigned to a universal rule, a default for work
  underway, or delegated rule selection because the mapped evidence does not
  establish any of them.
- OQ-017 remains open, unchanged, and undispositioned. Its mapping is clarified
  by this finding, but the question is not resolved.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.

## 6. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The evidence does not decide whether FEF should adopt a general
retrospective, prospective, hybrid, grandfathering, or case-specific rule;
which date or event should make an instrument effective; how ordinary
amendment or withdrawal should affect valid work underway; or whether any
rule-selection authority should be delegated. Those remain matters for later
separately governed Founder judgement. This finding makes no recommendation.

## 7. Scope and Confidence

**Confidence: Moderate to High for the bounded examples; insufficient for a
general transitional model.** The attributable records strongly support the
specific commencement, recovery, correction, and traceability observations.
The absence of an ordinary-amendment example, the open OQ-017, the
non-authoritative draft, and the lack of a delegated example prevent
generalisation.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-033 and the eight mapped frozen records. It
does not decide RQ-034 through RQ-037, D6 administration, or D7 constitutional
transition.

## 8. Non-Effects

This finding does not adopt, recommend, or apply a retrospective,
prospective, hybrid, grandfathering, amendment, withdrawal, correction, or
effective-date rule. It does not amend FEF-RGS-000; resolve OQ-017; modify
frozen evidence or evidence treatment; alter GF-031; issue a Founder
disposition or Founder Decision; examine RQ-034 through RQ-037; close S05 or
D5; or commence D6 or D7. It remains Presented and pending Founder
disposition.
