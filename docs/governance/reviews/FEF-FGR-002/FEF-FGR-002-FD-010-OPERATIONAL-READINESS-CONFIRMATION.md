## 2. Conditions

### 2.1 Post-approval synchronisation and validation

D2 shall not commence until this Founder disposition has been:

* recorded in the canonical Founder Decision record;
* synchronised across all affected registers and programme records;
* linked bidirectionally to ORC-001;
* subjected to bounded post-approval validation.

### 2.2 Founder authority remains non-automated

Governed execution loops may support deterministic, repeatable, and traceable activities.

They shall not exercise, simulate, infer, or automate:

* Founder judgement;
* Founder dispositions;
* Founder approval;
* constitutional authority;
* interpretation of materially ambiguous evidence.

### 2.3 D1 is a reference implementation, not a mandatory duplication model

Future review domains shall reuse the controls and lifecycle proven through D1.

They shall not reproduce every D1 artefact or narrative section where the governance risk is already controlled through an existing authoritative record.

### 2.4 Artefact-necessity gate

Any proposed new governance artefact must demonstrate:

> What governance risk does this reduce that is not already controlled elsewhere?

No additional artefact shall be created where no distinct governance risk is reduced.

### 2.5 Framework changes remain evidence-driven

Operational Mode does not authorise continual redesign or expansion of the Framework.

Framework changes may be proposed only where execution evidence identifies a genuine governance deficiency.

### 2.6 FEF-P1-002 remains pending

The pending document-governance standard and authority/source hierarchy under FEF-P1-002 remain unauthorised.

This decision does not approve, substitute for, or prejudge FEF-P1-002.

### 2.7 Canonical identifier usage

The full identifier FEF-FGR-002-FD-010 shall be used wherever ambiguity could arise with the protected historical FD-010 placeholder in FEF-FGR-001.

## 3. Programme Authorisation

Following successful recording, synchronisation, and validation of this disposition:

* ORC-001 may be marked complete under its governed status model;
