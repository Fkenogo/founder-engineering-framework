## 1. Purpose and Authority

This record prepares the smallest complete and separately examinable set of
neutral D4 Review Question candidates supported by the bounded scope in
[FEF-FGR-002-D4-MOB-001](FEF-FGR-002-D4-MOB-001-RECORDS-AND-INFORMATION-GOVERNANCE-MOBILISATION-RECORD.md)
v1.1.

Preparation is governed by the Founder-approved
[Charter](../FEF-FGRC-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-CHARTER.md),
[Agenda](../FEF-FGRA-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-AGENDA.md),
[Plan](../FEF-FGRP-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-PLAN.md), controlled
[Execution Rules](../FEF-FGRER-001-REVIEW-EXECUTION-RULES.md), and
[FEF-RQS-001](../FEF-RQS-001-REVIEW-QUESTION-SPECIFICATION.md).

D4 mobilisation became effective through
[FEF-FGR-002-D4-FMAR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D4-FOUNDER-MOBILISATION-AUTHORISATION-RECORD.md),
validated in
[FEF-FGR-002-D4-FMAVR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D4-FOUNDER-MOBILISATION-AUTHORISATION-VALIDATION-REPORT.md).
That authority permits candidate preparation as the next governed activity. It
does not allocate, admit, or answer a canonical RQ and does not authorise
evidence activity.

The temporary references `D4-RQC-01` through `D4-RQC-07` are drafting aids
only. They are not canonical RQ identifiers, do not instantiate canonical RQ
lifecycle entries, and are not entered in the Review Question Register.

## 2. Preparation Method

The Review Analyst:

1. used the seven approved D4 strategic examination areas as coverage inputs;
2. retained one candidate for each concern because each has a distinct
   governance object, intended evidence domain, and potential disposition;
3. bounded governed record classes separately from register and custody
   responsibilities;
4. bounded retention and archival separately from legal hold and deletion
   because ordinary preservation and exceptional restriction or destruction
   require different authority and traceability treatment;
5. kept access, confidentiality, privacy, and security together as one
   classification-and-authorisation concern without defining technical
   controls;
6. separated organisational knowledge continuity from general information
   lifecycle integrity while preserving FEF-CCF-001 as future work only; and
7. treated intended evidence domains as generic classes and characteristics,
   not identified sources, registered evidence, or conclusions.

No candidate states a preferred answer, record architecture, retention period,
access model, technical system, legal conclusion, lifecycle model, or
implementation design.

Every candidate:

- treats D1 authority, D2 evidence governance, and D3 assurance as closed
  dependencies rather than questions to reopen;
- distinguishes records governance from evidence meaning, assurance verdicts,
  Founder judgement, D5 lifecycle architecture, and D6 administration;

- remains independent of technical storage, tooling, automation, and project
  implementation;
- creates no FEF-CCF-001 content or Framework Evolution effect; and
- creates no constitutional wording, candidate, or effect.

## 5. Proportionality and Overlap Assessment

Seven candidates are proportionate because:

- each approved strategic examination area has a distinct governance object
  and intended evidence domain;
- record-class definition remains separate from responsibility and custody;
- ordinary retention and archival remain separate from exceptional legal hold,
  deletion, and irreversible disposition;
- access classification remains separate from retention and disposal;
- organisational knowledge continuity remains separate from end-to-end
  information integrity; and
- lifecycle integrity is bounded to information handling and does not create
  the D5 governance lifecycle model.

No candidate exists merely to increase question count. No merge, split,
removal, or additional candidate is proposed at this stage.

## 6. Intended Evidence Boundary

characteristics only. They do not:

- name or identify a source;

- allocate an Evidence Record identifier;
- determine provenance, admissibility, permitted use, treatment, or weight;
- create an evidence catalogue or requirement matrix;
- assemble or freeze an Evidence Pack; or
- state what any evidence proves.

Exact evidence requirements may be derived only after a later Founder
admission and canonical RQ gate.
