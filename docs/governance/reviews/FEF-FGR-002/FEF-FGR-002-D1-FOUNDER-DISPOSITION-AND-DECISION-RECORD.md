**Status:** Operational

Framework Engineering remains available but is no longer the default operating mode.

**Operational principles**

1. Governance artefacts shall only be created where they reduce a governance risk not already controlled elsewhere.
2. Engineer once. Execute many times.
3. Governed execution loops should be used wherever execution is deterministic, repeatable, traceable, and already governed.
4. Execution may loop. Founder authority shall never be automated.
5. Framework improvement shall be exception-driven rather than activity-driven.

```text
Mobilize
↓
Review
↓
Validate
↓
Founder Review
↓
Founder Decisions
↓
Validation
↓
Close Review
```
