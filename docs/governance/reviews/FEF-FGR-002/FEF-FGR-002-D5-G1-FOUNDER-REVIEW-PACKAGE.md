## Purpose

This package assembles the six validated D5 temporary Review Question
candidates for Founder review at gate D5-G1. It reproduces, without
reinterpretation, the exact candidate wording from FEF-FGR-002-D5-RQC-001
and the exact validation findings from FEF-FGR-002-D5-RQCVR-001, and adds
blank sections for the Founder to record observations, discussion,
disposition, rationale, and follow-up actions.

This package does not perform the Founder Review. It does not recommend a
disposition for any candidate. It does not allocate a canonical Review
Question identifier, admit a Review Question, update a governance register,
qualify or register evidence, create an Evidence Pack, commence
examination, create a Governance Finding, create a Founder Decision, or
amend constitutional content.

Disposition options available to the Founder for each candidate are:

- **Accept**
- **Accept with Amendment**
- **Merge**
- **Split**
- **Reject**
- **Defer**

## Set-Level Context (from FEF-FGR-002-D5-RQC-001 and its validation)

- Six candidates were prepared against all nine Agenda §6.5 strategic-examination items (drafting/review states; approval versus operational treatment; applicability and transition; versioning; amendment; supersession and withdrawal; exceptions and expiry; controlled evolution; treatment of legacy governance); no candidate exists merely to mirror a theme number, and no merge or removal was proposed at the validation pass.
- Every candidate treats D1, D3, and D4 outcomes as dependencies, not as questions to reopen; identifies D6–D8 only as bounded interfaces; and preserves the exact wording and open status of every referenced Open Question (OQ-004, OQ-012, OQ-013, OQ-014, OQ-016, OQ-017, OQ-021, OQ-022).
- FEF-FEV-001-FEC-001, FEF-CCF-001, and CE1–CE6 are explicitly excluded from evaluation by D5-RQC-05, consistent with Founder Condition 3 on D5 mobilisation ([FEF-FGR-002-D5-FMAR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D5-FOUNDER-MOBILISATION-AUTHORISATION-RECORD.md)).
- The Validator pass (FEF-FGR-002-D5-RQCVR-001) recorded **Pass with
  Conditions** at both candidate level and set level. The seven set-level

  1. the temporary references remain drafting aids only, not admitted or

  2. Founder Candidate Review of this set must occur before any canonical

## D5-RQC-01

### Candidate Reference

D5-RQC-01

### Candidate Wording

> What drafting, review, and operational states, if any, do FEF governance instruments pass through, and by what Founder or delegated authority is each state transition confirmed?

### Constitutional Intent

D1–D4 already use varied status vocabulary (e.g., "Draft," "Founder Review
Required," "Mobilised — Effective," "Closed") without an approved common
state model. This candidate asks what states exist and who confirms each
transition, without proposing a specific vocabulary or approval threshold.

### Validation Summary

Validator result: **Retain.** Assessed as neutral and singular — status and
approval form one indivisible question. D5 scope: Pass. D1/D3/D4
preservation: Pass. Open Question treatment: OQ-004 and OQ-022 direct.
Evidence-addressable: Pass. Overlap treatment: bounded from the transition
mechanics examined in D5-RQC-04, to prevent this candidate from absorbing
that question.

### Coverage Notes

- **Dependencies:** D1 — Founder authority and reserved decision-gates
  remain the approving mechanism for any transition; not reopened. D3 —
  assurance verdicts (Pass, Pass with Conditions) are a distinct concept
  from instrument status and must not be conflated with it. D4 — registers
  already carry structured "Lifecycle State" fields; D5 examines whether
  that vocabulary generalises.
- **Cross-domain interfaces:** D6 will need a stable vocabulary to
  administer; D7 may require a distinct model for constitutional material.
- **Exclusions:** no new status vocabulary is adopted; no register
  redesign; no constitutional status definition.
- **Overlap risk:** could overlap with D5-RQC-04 where a status-label
  change is itself an amendment; bounded to the state model and approving
  authority, not the transition mechanics.

## D5-RQC-03

### Candidate Reference

D5-RQC-03

### Candidate Wording

> What versioning and release-state conventions, if any, should apply uniformly across FEF governance instruments, and how does this relate to the version-history practice already separately operated by the Master Programme and individual review records?

### Constitutional Intent

The repository already operates several distinct versioning conventions
(Master Programme `v0.NN`, FEF-RGS-000 `Draft v0.2`, Review Identity
`v1.NN`, individual FEF-FGR-002 records `v1.0`/`v1.1`) without an approved
common standard. This candidate asks whether a uniform convention is
needed, without proposing one.

### Validation Summary

Validator result: **Retain.** Assessed as neutral and singular —
versioning/release convention. D5 scope: Pass. D1/D3/D4 preservation: Pass.
Open Question treatment: OQ-014 direct. Evidence-addressable: Pass. Overlap
treatment: status vocabulary remains with D5-RQC-01.

### Coverage Notes

- **Dependencies:** D1 — authority to approve any adopted convention is
  separate from and reserved above the numbering scheme itself. D3 —
  assurance/validation reports' `v1.0 — Pass` style versioning is an
  operated example, not a settled standard. D4 — registers already use
  structured version fields; direct existing precedent.
- **Cross-domain interfaces:** D6 administration would implement any
  adopted convention; D7 constitutional documents may need a distinct
  versioning treatment.
- **Exclusions:** no versioning scheme is adopted; no existing document is
  renumbered by this candidate.
- **Overlap risk:** overlaps with D5-RQC-01 since a version bump often
  accompanies a status change; bounded to the numbering/release convention
  itself, not the state vocabulary.
