## 1. Finding Reference

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-040 — Explicit Precedence Notes Provide a Minimal, Operating Administrative Coherence Control in at Least Two Documents, but No Verification, Reconciliation, or Completeness Mechanism Is Evidenced**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

**Disposition**

Accept

**Founder Reflection**

Administrative coherence should be achieved through clear ownership,
consistent repository architecture, and authoritative programme structure
rather than comprehensive relationship cataloguing.

Only those relationships necessary to preserve governance integrity should
be documented.

The remainder should emerge naturally from sound architectural design.

No Conditions, additional Discussion, or Follow-up Actions were supplied
by the Founder beyond the Disposition and Founder Reflection recorded
above. The Founder Reflection is explanatory only; it creates no
governance authority, Framework policy, or implementation instruction.

currently-operating precedence-note pattern exists in at least two
controlled documents and that the Review Question Register applies a
