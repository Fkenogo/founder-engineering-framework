This record prepares the bounded mobilisation-planning state for **D5 —
Governance Lifecycle and Evolution**. It defines the domain's existing
purpose, scope, exclusions, dependencies, interfaces, assumptions,
limitations, evidence outlook, roles, validation result, and next governed
activity. Version 1.0 prepared the record without making mobilisation
effective, pending a separate, attributable Founder authorisation recorded

to D5's distinct subject matter.

## 3. D5 Review Purpose (As Already Defined — Not Newly Inferred)

The Charter, Agenda, and Plan already define D5's purpose consistently. This
record does not infer a new purpose from prior-domain observations; it
restates the existing definition:

> Examine governance status, versioning, applicability, supersession,
> withdrawal, controlled evolution, and transitional treatment — i.e., how
> the framework's own governance instruments change over time without
> losing history, authority, applicability, or clarity — without activating
> a lifecycle model until a Founder decision does so.

- exceptions and expiry;
- controlled evolution;
- treatment of legacy governance.

This purpose does not predetermine a Review Question, evidence requirement,
finding, decision, or lifecycle-model design.

## 4. Domain Boundaries

### 4.1 Included Boundary

D5 is bounded to governance treatment of:

- what "Draft," "Approved," "Active," "Superseded," "Withdrawn," and similar
  status labels mean and who may change them;
- versioning and release-state conventions already observed in this
  repository's own practice (e.g., Master Programme's `v0.NN` revisions,
  FEF-RGS-000's "Draft v0.2 — Founder Review Required");
- applicability and transition rules (does an approved instrument bind work
  already underway, or only work commissioned after its effective date);
- amendment, supersession, and withdrawal mechanics;
- exceptions, expiry, and controlled-evolution treatment; and
- how legacy (pre-FEF-FGR-002) governance material is treated under any
  future lifecycle model.

These are domain boundaries only. They are not candidate Review Questions
and carry no RQ identifier, wording, evidence need, lifecycle state, or
admission effect.

### 4.2 Exclusions

D5 mobilisation planning does not:

- activate, select, or implement a lifecycle model;
- reopen, reinterpret, or modify D1, D2, D3, or D4;
- alter evidence meaning, admissibility, weighting, or any frozen Evidence Pack (EP-001–EP-004);
- redesign D6 identifiers, taxonomy, registers, roadmaps, or administrative structures;
- extract D7 constitutional principles or amend the Constitution or FEF-RGS-000;
- answer, amend, or disposition an Open Question under D8;
- perform Framework Evolution or disposition CE1–CE6 (FEF-GER-D3-001 / FEF-FAP-001);
- register, evaluate, or disposition any FEF-FEV-001 candidate, including FEF-FEV-001-FEC-001;
- implement FRAS or any repository-architecture standard;
- perform constitutional consolidation;
- restructure the repository, rename identifiers, or rewrite Git history; or
- authorise downstream work by implication.
