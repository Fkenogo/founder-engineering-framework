## 2. Purpose and Boundary

This pack assembles the exact evidence corpus qualified for RQ-038
through RQ-043 in a single consolidated D6 evidence mobilisation pass
(FEF-FGR-002-D6-ERC-001, FEF-FGR-002-D6-EQR-001), validated ready for
freeze in FEF-FGR-002-D6-EMVR-001. It creates a stable baseline for a
future, separately governed D6 session-entry decision (DG-4).

The pack contains:

- 13 unique Evidence Records (9 reused or reobserved, 4 newly registered);
- 22 source-to-RQ mappings;
- 9 evidence requirements across six admitted Review Questions.

This is the smallest domain Evidence Pack in FEF-FGR-002 to date,
consistent with the Founder's explicit direction that D6's evidence base
should be smaller than D5's and that evidence should not be collected
merely because a controlled artefact exists. Every item's inclusion was
individually tested against the Founder's Administrative Evidence
Sufficiency Check in FEF-FGR-002-D6-EMVR-001 §6 before this assembly; all
13 passed.

Assembly and freeze do not answer a Review Question, evaluate evidence
sufficiency beyond the sufficiency check already performed, resolve an
Open Question, produce a Governance Finding, prepare a Founder Decision,
create or open a session, commence examination, commence D7, design
FEF-CCF-001, amend methodology, or perform Framework Evolution.
