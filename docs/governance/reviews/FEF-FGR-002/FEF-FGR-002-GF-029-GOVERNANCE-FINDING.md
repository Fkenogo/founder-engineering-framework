## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What governance records and continuity controls, if any, are necessary to preserve the context, rationale, dependencies, limitations, and handover knowledge required for later understanding and responsible use of FEF governance outputs without defining a Context Continuity Framework or implementation artefact?

This examination used only the eleven Evidence Records mapped to RQ-030 in
EP-004 v1.0: EV-005, EV-008, EV-012, EV-013, EV-016, EV-020, EV-021,
EV-070, EV-071, EV-073, and EV-074. No unmapped evidence was introduced.

The evidence qualifications remain controlling:

- EV-005 and EV-008 are E2 Admitted within the approved review Charter and
  execution-rule boundaries;
- EV-012 is E2 Admitted as an Open Questions Register entry that records
  OQ-021 and OQ-023 but supplies no answer;
- EV-013 is E2 Conditionally Admitted and remains Draft v0.2, Founder
  Review Required, Not Approved, with research-specific proposals only;
- EV-016, EV-020, and EV-021 are E2 Admitted within FEF-FGR-002's
  review-operational role, assignment-procedure, and assignment-register
  boundaries;
- EV-070 is E1 Admitted for mandatory evidence-to-output traceability and
  controlled reuse without authority elevation, not for a general
  organisational-knowledge model;
- EV-071 is E1 Admitted for its exact evidence-custody boundary; role
  handover and succession requirements remain outside that decision;
- EV-073 is E4 Admitted as one validated, closed-domain operational
  traceability example, not as proof of universal sufficiency; and
- EV-074 is E1 Admitted only for the prospective Framework Evolution,
  non-retrospective-treatment, and future FEF-CCF-001 boundary. It supplies
  no Context Continuity Framework design or D4 answer.

## 2. Evidence-Supported Observations

1. EV-005 requires review records to preserve stable identity, authority,
   provenance, status, history, evidence references, limitations,
   dependencies, rationale where applicable, and a complete audit trail.
   Its required chain connects questions, evidence, sessions, findings,
   Founder dispositions, decisions, and later effects. These are
   review-scoped controls.
2. EV-008 requires dependency states, conditions, rationale, prior state,
   linked revision, correction, reopening triggers, identifiers, history,
   and traceability to remain explicit. An unsatisfied dependency cannot be
   silently represented as satisfied, and partial answers preserve the
   unresolved remainder.
3. EV-012 records OQ-021, “Which governance-chain transitions require
   explicit approval, and who owns each transition record?”, and OQ-023,
   “Which research artefacts require separate controlled documents, and
   who maintains the Research Register?” Both remain open and supply no
   ownership or maintenance answer.
4. EV-013 proposes research-specific record, reconstruction, preservation,
   closure, context, rationale, dependency, limitation, and continuity
   terminology. Because EV-013 is Not Approved, those proposals provide
   bounded context only and cannot establish current governance.
5. EV-016 assigns review-scoped administration, analysis, recording,
   custody, and validation responsibilities while preserving role
   limitations, conflicts, vacancies, temporary arrangements, traceability,
   access treatment, and authority boundaries. It does not create permanent
   organisational roles or D6 architecture.
6. EV-020 requires reassignment records to identify reason, affected role,
   incumbent, successor, effective date, handover, access changes,
   reassessed conflicts, approval, validation, and linkage between prior and
   successor records. Its handover section requires preservation of current
   registers and artefact states, open defects, conflicts, dependencies,
   escalations, access custody, pack integrity, upcoming gates, working
   records, acknowledgements, and transfer validation. The prior assignment
   remains preserved, and succession cannot expand role authority.
7. EV-021 records the effective current FEF-FGR-002 assignments, authority
   sources, combinations, limitations, and compensating controls. It is an
   assignment snapshot and does not evidence an operated reassignment,
   succession, handover, successor acknowledgement, or transfer validation.
8. EV-070 gives attributable Founder authority that substantive outputs
   trace to precise Evidence Records through RQs, sessions, findings,
   decisions, and later effects. Reuse must preserve identity, version,
   class, authority, admissibility, limitations, permitted use, and exact
   purpose without substituting a summary or elevating authority. It does
   not establish permanent D6 architecture or complete D2–D8 mechanics.
9. EV-071 gives attributable Founder authority for evidence custody to
   preserve identity, provenance, access state, integrity, versions, pack
   custody, and traceability without conferring meaning, approval, or
   Founder authority. Role handover and succession remain expressly outside
   the decision, and no operated custody succession is evidenced.
10. EV-073 demonstrates one complete, validated D3 chain connecting each RQ
    to evidence, EP-003, S03, a finding, Founder disposition, Decision
    Record, and closure treatment. It evidences operated traceability for
    one closed domain, not universal organisational continuity or later-user
    context recovery.
11. EV-074 records attributable Founder authority that post-milestone
    improvements are prospective Framework Evolution and must not
    retrospectively alter or reinterpret closed D1–D3 domains. It places
    FEF-CCF-001, Engineering Context Snapshots, and related continuity
    artefacts in future Framework Evolution outside the current decision.

## 4. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The mapped evidence does not itself decide:

- whether a FEF-wide minimum set of context, rationale, dependency,
  limitation, recovery, continuity, or handover records should be required;
- which governance-chain transitions, contextual records, and artefacts
  require controlled status, approval, ownership, validation, correction,
  maintenance, or transfer;
- what continuity evidence would demonstrate that later users can recover
  context and responsibly use an output without the original participants;
- what enduring role-handover, custody-succession, acknowledgement,
  transfer-validation, or continuity-recovery controls should apply;
- which later D6 administrative ownership or maintenance structures should
  implement any Founder-approved D4 treatment; or
- whether, when, or how FEF-CCF-001 should later be evaluated through the
  separate Framework Evolution process.

These are matters for later Founder judgement. This finding selects no
answer and makes no recommendation.

## 5. Conclusion

The mapped evidence establishes bounded continuity-supporting controls for
the Founder Governance Review: controlling records preserve identity,
authority, provenance, history, dependencies, limitations, rationale,
permitted use, and end-to-end traceability; review-scoped role procedures
define detailed reassignment and handover content; and one closed D3 domain
demonstrates an operated evidence-to-decision chain.

The same corpus does not establish an approved FEF-wide
organisational-knowledge or continuity model, a universal minimum context
record, enduring ownership and maintenance, an operated handover or
continuity-recovery event, or later-user recovery without original
participants. OQ-021 and OQ-023 remain open; EV-013 is Not Approved;
EV-016, EV-020, EV-021, EV-070, EV-071, and EV-073 retain review,
evidence, or single-domain limits; and EV-074 places FEF-CCF-001 outside
this review loop as future Framework Evolution. Broader treatment therefore
requires Founder judgement and cannot be inferred from traceability,
document existence, or an unoperated procedure.

## 6. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source substantively contradicts the conclusion. EV-020 defines a
detailed handover procedure, but no mapped source records that the procedure
has been operated. EV-073 supplies a complete D3 traceability chain, but it
does not test role succession, knowledge recovery, or responsible reuse by
a later user without original participants.

EV-013 proposes broader research-record and continuity terminology, but its
Not Approved status is controlling. EV-074 authorises only prospective
evaluation of future FEF-CCF-001 work and prohibits retrospective alteration
or reinterpretation of D1–D3; it cannot be used as current continuity
governance or design content.

No absence outside EP-004 or outside RQ-030's eleven-item mapping is
claimed.

## 7. Limitations and Uncertainty

- The finding is limited to the eleven mapped EP-004 v1.0 Evidence Records.
- Most approved controls govern this review or its evidence, not all FEF
  governance records.
- EV-012 records OQ-021 and OQ-023 as open and cannot answer them.
- EV-013 remains Conditionally Admitted within its exact Not Approved,
  research-specific boundary.
- EV-020 defines a review-scoped procedure; no operated handover is mapped.
- EV-021 is a current assignment snapshot, not a continuity test.
- EV-070 and EV-071 establish evidence traceability and custody boundaries,
  not a general organisational-knowledge model.
- EV-073 is one domain-specific traceability example.
- EV-074 supplies only prospective, non-retrospective, future-work
  boundaries and no FEF-CCF-001 content.
- No Context Continuity Framework, Engineering Context Snapshot, template,
  handover system, knowledge base, tooling, or D6 architecture is defined.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.

Approved, no handover or continuity-recovery event is mapped, and no
