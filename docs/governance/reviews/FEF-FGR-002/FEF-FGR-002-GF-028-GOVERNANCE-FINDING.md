## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What governance authority, safeguards, records, and review controls, if any, are required for legal hold, deletion, disposal, or irreversible restriction of FEF records so that preservation duties, exceptions, conflicts, and accountability remain explicit without making legal or technical implementation decisions?

This examination used only the nine Evidence Records mapped to RQ-029 in
EP-004 v1.0: EV-005, EV-007, EV-008, EV-012, EV-013, EV-023, EV-066,
EV-069, and EV-071. No unmapped evidence was introduced.

The evidence qualifications remain controlling:

- EV-005, EV-007, and EV-008 are E2 Admitted within the approved review
  Charter, Plan, and execution-rule boundaries;
- EV-012 is E2 Admitted as an Open Questions Register entry that records
  OQ-011 and OQ-012 but supplies no answer;
- EV-013 is E2 Conditionally Admitted and remains Draft v0.2, Founder
  Review Required, Not Approved, with research-specific proposals only;
- EV-023 is E2 Conditionally Admitted as an Evidence Pack preparation
  baseline with a retained draft header, not as a generally approved
  Framework standard;
- EV-066 is E1 Admitted for its exact evidence-qualification and
  permitted-reliance boundary; external, restricted, legal, privacy,
  security, professional, and project-specific source policy remains
  outside its scope;
- EV-069 is E1 Admitted for immutable frozen-pack baselines and
  attributable change control; its detailed D5 lifecycle treatment remains
  provisional and it supplies no general deletion or legal-hold rule; and
- EV-071 is E1 Admitted for its exact evidence-custody boundary; it
  preserves identity, versions, access state, and traceability but expressly
  leaves deletion and legal-hold rules to D4.

## 2. Evidence-Supported Observations

1. EV-005 requires identifiers to be unique, stable, reserved, and not
   silently reused. Rejected, returned, superseded, or deferred review
   records remain visible through status and audit history rather than
   disappearing. It also requires authority, exception, and escalation
   boundaries to remain explicit. These are review controls only.
2. EV-007 assigns records and information governance to D4, preserves
   evidence and decision history through iteration, and requires external
   legal, security, privacy, professional, or other dependencies to remain
   visible rather than being resolved through assumption.
3. EV-008 preserves prior and failed review states, requires attributable
   authority and recorded triggers for reopening or material change, and
   provides stop, exception, and escalation treatment for unresolved legal
   or authority defects. It does not establish substantive legal-hold,
   deletion, or disposal rules.
4. EV-012 records OQ-011, “What retention, archival, legal-hold, and
   deletion rules apply to research records?”, and OQ-012, “What exception,
   deviation, expiry, and escalation mechanism applies to research
   standards?” Both remain open and supply no governing answer.
5. EV-013 states, as a Not Approved research-specific proposal, that
   retention periods, archival formats, and deletion authority remain open.
   Its proposed exception and deviation record would identify authority,
   rationale, risk, duration, controls, approval, review, expiry, and the
   resulting record. Those proposals cannot be elevated into approved
   governance.
6. EV-023 provides bounded Evidence Pack controls for immutable frozen
   baselines, retained prior versions, Withdrawn or Archived states,
   retention routing, successor treatment, changed-source handling,
   validation, and audit. These controls apply to Evidence Pack preparation
   and do not create FEF-wide legal-hold or deletion authority.
7. EV-066 confirms that evidence qualification and reliance do not
   establish external legal completeness or policy for protected,
   restricted, legal, privacy, security, professional, or project-specific
   sources. D4 deletion and information-lifecycle control was outside that
   decision.
8. EV-069 establishes that a material removal or change affecting a frozen
   Evidence Pack must preserve the prior baseline and proceed through an
   attributable successor or supplemental path with impact assessment,
   revalidation or refreeze, and linkage. It does not authorise destruction,
   deletion, disposal, legal hold, or irreversible restriction of the
   underlying FEF record.
9. EV-071 requires evidence custody to preserve identity, versions, access
   state, and traceability, and confirms that custody or repository access
   confers no evidential, approval, or Founder authority. It supplies no
   deletion or legal-hold authority and leaves those matters to D4.

## 4. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The mapped evidence does not itself decide:

- whether FEF-wide legal-hold triggers, issuing authority, scope, notice,
  acknowledgement, review, release, conflict, or audit controls should be
  established;
- who may authorise deletion, disposal, destruction, irreversible
  restriction, suspension, exception, release, reversal, or recovery;
- what disposition criteria, schedules, safeguards, approvals,
  notifications, verification, records, and accountability should apply;
- how preservation duties should take precedence or be reconciled with
  deletion, privacy, security, confidentiality, access, or withdrawal
  duties;
- how applicable external legal, regulatory, contractual, security,
  privacy, or professional obligations and advisers should be engaged; or
- which later D5 lifecycle mechanisms should implement any
  Founder-approved D4 treatment.

These are matters for later Founder judgement. This finding selects no
answer and makes no recommendation.

## 5. Conclusion

The mapped evidence establishes bounded preservation controls for the
Founder Governance Review and its Evidence Packs: identifiers and prior
states remain visible, custody preserves identity and versions, frozen
baselines remain immutable, and material pack removal or change requires an
attributable successor or supplemental path with preserved history,
assessment, validation, and linkage.

The same corpus does not establish FEF-wide legal-hold authority, deletion
or disposal authority, destruction or irreversible-restriction safeguards,
disposition criteria, exception and release controls, conflict precedence,
required operational records, or external legal obligations. OQ-011 and
OQ-012 remain open; EV-013 and EV-023 remain authority-limited; EV-066,
EV-069, and EV-071 expressly preserve narrower boundaries; and no operated
case or external authority is mapped. Broader treatment therefore requires
Founder judgement and cannot be inferred from review-history or
Evidence-Pack controls.

## 6. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source substantively contradicts the conclusion. EV-023 includes
retention routing, Withdrawn and Archived states, changed-source treatment,
and successor controls, but its Evidence Pack scope and retained draft
header prevent treating those controls as generally approved legal-hold,
deletion, or disposition governance. EV-013 proposes exception and
deviation fields and identifies retention and deletion questions, but its
Not Approved status is controlling.

EV-069 makes frozen-baseline preservation and attributable pack change
authoritative while leaving detailed D5 lifecycle treatment provisional.
EV-066 and EV-071 expressly exclude or defer the broader legal-hold and
deletion policy. Those qualifications narrow rather than displace the
conclusion.

No absence outside EP-004 or outside RQ-029's nine-item mapping is claimed.

## 7. Limitations and Uncertainty

- The finding is limited to the nine mapped EP-004 v1.0 Evidence Records.
- Most approved controls govern this review or its Evidence Packs, not all
  FEF records.
- EV-012 records OQ-011 and OQ-012 as open and cannot answer them.
- EV-013 and EV-023 remain Conditionally Admitted within their exact limits.
- EV-066 supplies no external or restricted-source legal policy.
- EV-069 supplies frozen-pack change control, not deletion or legal-hold
  authority; detailed D5 lifecycle treatment remains provisional.
- EV-071 supplies custody boundaries, not deletion or legal-hold rules.
- No legal determination, technical implementation, retention schedule,
  disposition schedule, archival policy, or D5 lifecycle state is selected.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.
