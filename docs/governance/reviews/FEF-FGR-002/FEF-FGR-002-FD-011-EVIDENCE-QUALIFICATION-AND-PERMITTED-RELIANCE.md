## 1. Decision Question

What attributable disposition and enduring treatment applies to
FEF-FGR-002-GF-009?

## 2. Exact Validated Finding

> Evidence may support an FEF governance conclusion only when an attributable
> qualification record establishes its identity, provenance, acquisition route,
> issuer or source authority, version or temporal state, integrity, relevance,
> access state, limitations, and permitted use. Registration or pack inclusion
> does not itself establish admissibility, truth, or unrestricted reliance.

## 3. Exact Founder Disposition and Decision

**Disposition: Accept with Conditions**

Accept as an enduring minimum baseline for FEF governance reviews that evidence
may support a governance conclusion only where an attributable qualification
record establishes:

- identity;
- provenance;
- acquisition route;
- issuer or source authority;
- version or temporal state;
- integrity;
- relevance;
- access state;
- limitations;
- permitted use.

Registration or Evidence Pack inclusion shall not by itself establish
admissibility, truth, or unrestricted reliance.

The following remain outside this disposition and require later governed
treatment:

- the permanent mandatory-versus-conditional field catalogue;
- external, mutable, protected, restricted, and project-specific source policy;
- legal, privacy, security, and professional evidence requirements;
- D4 retention, archival, deletion, and information-lifecycle controls;
- any general authority/source hierarchy under FEF-P1-002.

## 4. Decision Treatment

The exact finding is accepted as an enduring minimum baseline subject to every
recorded exclusion above. Current evidence-qualification operation may rely on
this decision, but the excluded subjects require their own later authority and
evidence.

## 5. Rationale, Conditions, and Limitations

No separate Founder rationale was supplied. The disposition itself supplies
the controlling conditions and exclusions. The finding's non-independent
validation, absence of E3 external evidence, untested restricted evidence, and
draft-status preparation-control limitations remain visible and are not cured
by acceptance.
