## 1. Finding Reference

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-042 — Founder-Stated Intent Favours an Evidence-Based Maturation Process for Operational Lessons, but No Such Process, or Criteria for Testing Single Source of Truth Against It, Is Evidenced**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

**Disposition**

Accept

**Founder Reflection**

Operational lessons should be preserved as knowledge rather than
immediately adopted as Framework administration.

Administrative practices should emerge only after demonstrating
transferability across multiple Founder projects and showing that they
materially improve governance integrity without unnecessarily reducing
project autonomy.

The Framework should learn before it legislates.

No Conditions, additional Discussion, or Follow-up Actions were supplied
by the Founder beyond the Disposition and Founder Reflection recorded
above. The Founder Reflection is explanatory only; it creates no
governance authority, Framework policy, or implementation instruction.

## 3. Exact Finding Scope

**Confidence: High for the bounded observation that the Founder has
stated a consistent intent favouring evidence-based maturation and that
Single Source of Truth is designated the first examined example; not
applicable to any defined maturation process or testable criteria,
because neither is evidenced to exist; genuinely inconclusive for
whether Single Source of Truth's direct-recommendation adoption itself
satisfies the Founder's stated rationale.** The directly quoted rationale
and Observation 5 text in EV-089, and the directly quoted adoption
statement in EV-080, strongly support the bounded conclusion. The
absence of any defined process or criteria is the direct and sufficient
basis for the negative findings in §3.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-043 and the four mapped frozen records.
It does not decide RQ-038 through RQ-042 (already answered in GF-037
through GF-041), and does not decide D7's future maturation treatment of
constitutional documents.
