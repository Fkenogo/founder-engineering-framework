## 1. What D2 Examined

D2 examined seven admitted Review Questions using frozen EP-002 v1.0 only.
The session considered evidence identity, provenance, authority, integrity,
classification, weighting, admissibility, sufficiency, conflict, gaps, freeze,
change control, traceability, reuse, custody, and operational responsibility.

S02 closed after validation passed with the disclosed non-independent
condition. It produced six findings and no Founder Decision, Constitutional
Candidate, Deferred Matter, or Open Question disposition.

## 2. What the Findings Collectively Establish

The evidence supports a bounded control chain:

1. evidence must be qualified for a permitted use before it may support a
   governance conclusion;
2. evidence class limits permitted use but cannot automatically determine
   decisive weight;
3. sufficiency is proposition-specific and requires explicit coverage,
   conflict, limitation, gap, and proceed/stop treatment;
4. frozen packs preserve the evidence baseline actually examined;
5. traceability preserves how that baseline supports later outputs and prevents
   authority elevation through reuse; and
6. custody preserves the evidential chain but does not decide meaning or
   exercise Founder authority.

## 3. What Remains Unresolved

- permanent mandatory versus conditional evidence fields;
- external, protected, restricted, mutable, and project-source policy;
- detailed weighting hierarchy and conflict-priority rules;
- universal quantitative sufficiency thresholds;
- operated supplement and successor-pack controls;
- complete D2–D8 reuse and permanent register architecture;
- independent assurance, role handover, and custody succession;
- D4 retention, archival, deletion, legal-hold, and information-lifecycle rules;
- D7 constitutional-candidate treatment;
- OQ-005, OQ-006, OQ-010, OQ-011, and OQ-018.
