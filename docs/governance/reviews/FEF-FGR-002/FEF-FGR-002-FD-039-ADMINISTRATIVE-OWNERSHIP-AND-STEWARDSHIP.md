## 1. Finding Reference

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-037 — Every Existing Role and Authority Instrument Is Scoped to This Review Only; No Framework-Level Cross-Project Administrative Responsibility or Delegation Boundary Is Yet Evidenced**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

**Disposition**

Accept

**Founder Reflection**

Framework-level administrative responsibilities should emerge only where
operational experience across multiple Founder projects demonstrates that
shared administration materially improves governance integrity.

Until then, administrative authority and stewardship remain with the
individual Founder projects, with the Framework providing only the minimum
shared governance necessary to preserve trust, consistency, and
engineering discipline.

No Conditions, additional Discussion, or Follow-up Actions were supplied
by the Founder beyond the Disposition and Founder Reflection recorded
above. The Founder Reflection is explanatory only; it creates no
governance authority, Framework policy, or implementation instruction.

## 3. Exact Finding Scope

**Confidence: High for the bounded observation that no Framework-level
cross-project administrative instrument currently exists; not applicable
to any cross-project model, because none is evidenced to assess.** The
directly attributable scope statements in every role instrument, and the
Framework's own "Not Yet Adoptable" / "Framework release: None" status
records, strongly support the bounded conclusion. The absence of any
second adopting project is the direct and sufficient reason no
cross-project responsibility or delegation boundary can yet be evidenced.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-038 and the eight mapped frozen records.
It does not decide RQ-039 through RQ-043 or any D7 interface.
