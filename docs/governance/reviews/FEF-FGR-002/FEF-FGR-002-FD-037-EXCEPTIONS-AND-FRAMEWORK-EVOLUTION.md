## 1. Finding Reference

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-035 — A Charter-Specific Exception Rule and Conditioned Dispositions Are Evidenced, but No General Exception, Expiry, or Evolution-Boundary Model Is Established**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

**Founder Observations**

FEF needs minimum exception controls, but temporary exceptions must not become
an alternative route for changing the framework.

**Discussion**

The Founder considered GF-035 and its authorised lean conditions. No additional
discussion is attributed.

**Disposition**

Accept with Conditions

**Conditions**

Common conditions applying to GF-031 through GF-036:

1. Acceptance confirms the finding, not a proposed solution.
2. Any future FEF control must remain a minimum cross-project foundation.
3. Project-specific lifecycle detail remains under project authority unless a
   universal requirement is demonstrated.
4. Founder-reserved authority cannot be delegated by implication.
5. Existing valid work and historical records must not be retrospectively
   altered without explicit authority.
6. No repository-wide reconciliation, renumbering, reclassification or
   migration programme is authorised.
7. Implementation design requires separately governed follow-up after Founder
   Decision issuance.
8. All eight applicable Open Questions remain open unless separately resolved.

Finding-specific Conditions:

1. A controlled exception must identify the affected control, reason, scope,
   risk, compensating controls, approving authority and expiry or review point.
2. An exception cannot bypass Founder-reserved authority.
3. An exception cannot reconstruct missing evidence or legitimise unauthorised
   work.
4. Permanent changes belong under Framework Evolution.
5. No elaborate universal exception register, renewal workflow or monitoring
   bureaucracy is authorised at this stage.

**Rationale**

Exceptions sometimes enable practical delivery, but weak exception control can
quietly undermine the framework.

**Follow-up Actions**

Design only the minimum exception record and its boundary with Framework
Evolution.
