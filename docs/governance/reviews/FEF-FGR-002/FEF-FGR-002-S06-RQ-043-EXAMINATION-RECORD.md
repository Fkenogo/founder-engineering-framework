## 3. Examination Method Note

Consistent with Loops 001–005, every observation below was tested
against the four-level distinction: **documented intent** (a stated
plan, rule, or expectation) is not the same as **administrative
capability** (a structured artefact or mechanism that exists and could
be used); capability is not the same as **demonstrated operation** (the
mechanism has actually been used, observably, at least once); and
demonstrated operation is not the same as **verified operation** (some
check confirms the mechanism continues to work correctly). No level is
inferred from evidence of a lower level alone, and absence of evidence
for a higher level is not read as proof that level does not exist — it
is recorded as inconclusive where genuine. Consistent with this loop's
specific direction, Framework-wide administrative capability is not
inferred merely because the Framework currently governs itself
successfully; a pattern demonstrated within FEF-FGR-002's own internal
documents is not treated as evidence of cross-project stewardship.

## 4. Examination Results by Analytical Classification

### 4.1 Directly Established by Evidence

1. EV-089 directly establishes the Founder's own stated rationale for
   RQ-043's origin (D6-RQC-06): "The Framework should not immediately
   adopt operational lessons. Operational experience should mature
   through an evidence-based administrative process before any
   consideration of constitutional adoption." This is documented intent
   that a maturation process should exist.
2. EV-089 directly establishes, as non-authoritative decision context
   (Founder Observation 5), a materially identical statement: "Operational
   experience should inform the Framework through governed maturation
   rather than immediate adoption. Potential governance improvements
   arising from Founder projects should mature through an administrative
   evaluation process before any consideration of becoming Framework
   practice or constitutional principle." EV-089 also directly
   establishes the Founder's exact constraint on this and the other four
   observations: they "are not to be interpreted as adopted Framework
   practices," remain "observations; future administrative
   considerations; decision context," and "shall not be inserted into
   constitutional documents or treated as established Framework policy."
3. EV-080 §1.1 directly establishes that the Single Source of Truth
   architecture itself was "Adopted 2026-08-04, on Founder
   recommendation, as a permanent programme-governance architecture
   principle." The mapped evidence records this adoption as a direct
   Founder recommendation; it does not, on its own text, attribute the
   adoption to any separately documented multi-step evaluation or
   maturation procedure.
4. EV-086 and EV-087 directly establish, consistent with Loops 001, 004,
   and 005, that the Single Source of Truth precedence-note pattern is
   currently and actually operating within at least three of
   FEF-FGR-002's own internal controlled documents (Master Programme,
   Dashboard, Review Identity) — demonstrated operation of the outcome,
   not evidence of the process by which it arose.
5. EV-089 directly establishes the Founder's exact instruction that "the
   Single Source of Truth architecture should be treated as the first
   examined example rather than the predetermined subject."

### 4.2 Reasonably Supported

1. The evidence reasonably supports that the Founder regards direct,
   immediate adoption and evidence-based, matured adoption as distinct
   pathways in principle, since the rationale in EV-089 explicitly
   contrasts "immediately adopt" against maturing "through an
   evidence-based administrative process."
2. The evidence reasonably supports that Single Source of Truth is an
   appropriate first candidate against which any future maturation
   process could be tested, consistent with the Founder's own exact
   instruction, without that instruction itself constituting the
   process's definition or SSoT's evaluation against it.

### 4.3 Unsupported

Applying the four-level test in §3, the mapped evidence does not
support:

- that any defined governance process — steps, roles, criteria,
  triggers, or decision points — for maturing operational lessons into
  Framework administrative practices currently exists; only stated
  intent that one should exist is evidenced;
- that any explicit criteria exist against which Single Source of Truth,
  or any other pattern, could be tested for having "matured" adequately,
  because no such criteria are defined in any mapped source;
- that any operational lesson has arisen from, and matured through
  observed cross-project experience across, multiple separate Founder
  projects — Single Source of Truth's demonstrated operation (EV-080,
  EV-086, EV-087) is confined to FEF-FGR-002's own internal documents,
  and no second adopting Founder project currently exists; and
- that Single Source of Truth's own adoption passed through any defined,
  documented, multi-step evaluation or maturation procedure separate
  from direct Founder recommendation.

### 4.4 Contradictory or Qualifying Matters

No mapped source directly contradicts another. Two qualifications
apply:

- a textual tension exists between the Founder's stated rationale and
  Observation 5 (that operational lessons should mature through an
  evidence-based or administrative evaluation process before adoption)
  and EV-080's direct record that Single Source of Truth was itself
  "Adopted... on Founder recommendation" as a permanent principle,
  without the mapped evidence showing an intervening, separately
  documented maturation procedure. This is recorded as a neutral textual
  observation only; whether direct Founder recommendation is itself an
  adequate or intended maturation pathway, and whether it satisfies the
  rationale's own terms, is a matter for Founder judgement and is not
  decided by this examination; and
- EV-086's and EV-087's demonstrated operation of the Single Source of
  Truth pattern must not be read as evidence that the pattern is
  Framework-wide-capable or has been tested across Founder projects; it
  is demonstrated operation within one review's own internal
  documentation only.

### 4.5 Uncertain

It is genuinely inconclusive, not merely unevidenced, whether direct
Founder recommendation constitutes, in the Founder's own view, a form of
"evidence-based administrative process" or "administrative evaluation
process" satisfying the rationale recorded in EV-089, or whether a
separate, more formal maturation process is intended and has simply not
yet been created. It is likewise genuinely inconclusive whether Single
Source of Truth, having already been adopted, is intended to be
evaluated retrospectively as the "first examined example" against a
future-defined maturation process, or whether its already-completed
adoption places it outside such a process's intended scope. Neither
matter is resolved by inference in either direction.

## 6. Examination Conclusion

The evidence answers RQ-043 only at a bounded finding level. It
establishes that the Founder has stated, both in the rationale for
RQ-043's own origin and in non-authoritative Observation 5, that
operational lessons should mature through an evidence-based
administrative process before consideration of adoption, and that Single
Source of Truth is to be treated as the first examined example against
any such process rather than the predetermined subject. It does not
establish that any such governance process currently exists in defined
form, nor any criteria against which Single Source of Truth's — or any
other pattern's — satisfaction of that process could be tested. The
mapped evidence records Single Source of Truth's own adoption as a
direct Founder recommendation, without documenting an intervening
maturation procedure; whether that satisfies the Founder's own stated
rationale is recorded as a qualifying observation, not resolved by this
examination. Genuinely uncertain matters are recorded as uncertain, not
resolved by inference in either direction, and no Framework-wide
administrative capability is inferred from the Framework's demonstrated
ability to govern its own internal documents.

One candidate Governance Finding, FEF-FGR-002-GF-042, records this
bounded conclusion. No recommendation or Founder disposition is embedded
in it. No Founder Observation is converted into Framework policy.
