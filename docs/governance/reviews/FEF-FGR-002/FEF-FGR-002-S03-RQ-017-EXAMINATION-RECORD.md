## 2. Phase 2 — Evidence Examination

### 2.1 Established Evidence

- EV-016 (Operational Governance Roles Standard, §9.6) states: "The
  Validator should be independent of the preparation being validated. At
  minimum, the validator must not be the sole preparer and sole validator
  of the same artefact without a recorded exception, stated limitation,
  and later independent revalidation or Founder-accepted procedural
  risk."
- EV-016 §4.6 and §6.3 (Founder mandatory independence) apply specifically
  to the Founder personally holding operational roles; the Founder here
  holds only RA-001 (§ EV-021 below), so this specific clause is not the
  operative one for the RA-002–RA-006 combination.
- EV-017 (Operational Authority Boundary, §5) states: "A validation pass
  ... does not mean ... risk is accepted unless the Founder expressly
  accepts it."
- EV-018 (Independence and Conflict Rules, §6.2) lists "Validator with
  any preparation role for the same artefact," "Evidence Custodian and
  Validator for the same Evidence Pack," "Review Analyst and Validator
  for the same GF," and "Review Recorder and Validator for the same
  session or FD record" as **High-Risk Combinations** that "require
  explicit Founder approval and compensating controls" (§6.2).
- EV-018 §6.3 separately addresses the case where "the Founder is also
  the sole Validator" — a different configuration from the one recorded
  in EV-021 (below), where the Founder (RA-001) is not the Validator.
- EV-021 (Role Assignment Register, v1.1) records six effective
  assignments dated 2026-07-24. For each of RA-002 through RA-006, the
  "Assignment Authority" column reads **"Founder"** and the "Authority
  Source" column reads "FEF-FGR-002-002 instruction" — the combined-role
  structure itself was assigned under recorded Founder authority at
  review mobilisation, not self-assigned by the operational capacity.
  Each row's "Limitations / Controls" column lists a distinct
  compensating-control set (capacity labels, sequential passes, immutable
  source comparison, automated checks, exact mandatory-field checklists,
  disclosed independence limitations). RA-006's row states: "independent
  revalidation may be required before reliance if Founder directs" —
  phrased as a conditional future action.
- EV-021's own control fields show "Last validation date: 2026-07-24" —
  the register has not been revalidated since the initial six
  assignments, despite D2 and D3 activity since occurring under the same
  assignments.
- EV-060 (D3 Founder Review Package) states, in its neutral framing of
  the RQ-017 candidate: "D1 and D2 accepted the current non-independent
  role combination for this review without establishing an enduring
  independence model."
- EV-061 (D3 Founder Disposition Record) records, for candidate
  D3-RQC-02 (the RQ-017 candidate wording): **Founder Disposition:
  Accept**; **Founder Observations: None recorded**; **Founder
  Rationale: None recorded**. The disposition accepts the *Review
  Question wording* for future examination; it records no Founder
  statement accepting or assessing the operational role combination or
  any residual procedural risk arising from it.

### 2.2 Supported Observations

1. Two distinct forms of Founder involvement in the combined-role
   structure are evidenced, and they answer different parts of RQ-017:
   (a) **explicit Founder approval of the combination's existence**,
   evidenced by EV-021's "Assignment Authority: Founder" for RA-002
   through RA-006 — this satisfies EV-018 §6.2's "explicit Founder
   approval" requirement for High-Risk Combinations, at the level of
   assigning the structure; and (b) **extensive, consistently operated
   compensating controls**, evidenced across every EV-021 row and
   corroborated by the pattern already established in RQ-016's evidence
   — satisfying EV-018 §6.2's "compensating controls" requirement.
2. A narrower, artefact-level test from EV-016 §9.6 is only partially
   evidenced: a "recorded exception" and "stated limitation" are present
   for every combined role (EV-021's Limitations/Controls column), but
   neither of §9.6's two alternative closing conditions — "later
   independent revalidation" or "Founder-accepted procedural risk" — is
   evidenced as having occurred for any artefact within RQ-017's mapped
   set. No independent revalidation is recorded anywhere in the mapped
   evidence, and EV-061's "Accept" of the RQ-017 candidate does not
   constitute Founder-accepted procedural risk in the sense EV-017 §5
   requires ("risk is accepted unless the Founder expressly accepts
   it") — it is Founder acceptance of a question to examine later, with
   no Observations or Rationale recorded.
3. The distinction in §2.2.1–2 suggests a candidate answer to "when may
   combined roles remain usable": the mapped evidence supports usability
   at the **structural/assignment level** (explicit Founder approval of
   the combination existing, plus disclosure and compensating controls)
   but does not yet evidence satisfaction of the **artefact-level**
   closing condition in EV-016 §9.6 (independent revalidation or express
   Founder risk-acceptance) for any specific combined-role artefact
   produced since assignment.
4. EV-021's own staleness (unrevalidated since 2026-07-24, spanning all
   of D2 and D3 activity to date) is itself evidence relevant to "what
   degree of independence is required" — the register that would record
   any change in the required degree of independence, or any operated
   independent revalidation, has not been updated to reflect two further
   completed domains of operation under the same combination.

### 2.3 Unsupported Assertions (explicitly excluded)

- That the "explicit Founder approval" evidenced at assignment (§2.2.1a)
  is sufficient, standing alone, to satisfy EV-016 §9.6's stricter
  artefact-level test. The two tests (§6.2's combination-level approval
  and §9.6's artefact-level closing condition) are not asserted to be
  interchangeable; RQ-017's mapped evidence does not state that one
  substitutes for the other.
- That the "degree of independence required for different assurance
  risks" (the RQ's own wording) has been fully answered. The mapped
  evidence establishes what has and has not occurred procedurally; it
  does not itself state a risk-differentiated independence standard, and
  no such standard is inferred here.
- That EV-060's framing statement ("D1 and D2 accepted the current
  non-independent role combination... without establishing an enduring
  independence model") is itself an independent primary act of
  acceptance beyond the EV-021 assignment record. EV-060 is a neutral
  candidate-framing document prepared by the Analyst capacity; it
  characterises the review's operational history but is not treated here
  as an additional Founder decision.
- That this pattern extends to D4 or D6. RQ-017's declared exclusions bar
  any complete D4/D6 model.

### 2.4 Uncertainty

Whether "independent revalidation" or "Founder-accepted procedural risk"
(EV-016 §9.6) will be required before any specific D1/D2/D3 artefact may
be relied upon downstream cannot be determined from the mapped evidence;
EV-021 records this as conditional on future Founder direction, not as a
standing requirement with a stated trigger or timetable.
