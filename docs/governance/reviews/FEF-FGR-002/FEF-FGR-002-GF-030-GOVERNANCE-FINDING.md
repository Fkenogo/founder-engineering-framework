## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What information-integrity checkpoints and traceability, if any, are required from record creation through maintenance, correction, transfer, archival, restriction, and authorised disposition to preserve identity, version lineage, completeness, accessibility, and reproducibility without establishing the D5 governance lifecycle model?

This examination used only the eleven Evidence Records mapped to RQ-031 in
EP-004 v1.0: EV-005, EV-008, EV-012, EV-013, EV-023, EV-059, EV-069,
EV-070, EV-071, EV-073, and EV-074. No unmapped evidence was introduced.

The evidence qualifications remain controlling:

- EV-005 and EV-008 are E2 Admitted within the approved review Charter and
  execution-rule boundaries;
- EV-012 is E2 Admitted as an Open Questions Register entry that records
  OQ-021 and OQ-022 but supplies no answer;
- EV-013 is E2 Conditionally Admitted and remains Draft v0.2, Founder
  Review Required, Not Approved, with research-specific proposals only;
- EV-023 is E2 Conditionally Admitted as an Evidence Pack preparation
  baseline with a retained draft header, not as a generally approved
  Framework or information-lifecycle standard;
- EV-059 is E2/E4 Admitted with its v1.0/v1.1 contradiction preserved as
  one operated same-capacity correction example, not a general lifecycle
  rule or proof of sufficient integrity;
- EV-069 is E1 Admitted for immutable frozen baselines and attributable
  change control; detailed lifecycle mechanics remain provisional for D5;
- EV-070 is E1 Admitted for mandatory evidence-to-output traceability and
  controlled reuse, not complete D2–D8 lifecycle mechanics;
- EV-071 is E1 Admitted for evidence-custody preservation and
  non-conferment of authority; broader information lifecycle remains
  outside that decision;
- EV-073 is E4 Admitted as one validated, closed-domain traceability and
  lifecycle snapshot, not a general D5 model; and
- EV-074 is E1 Admitted only for prospective Framework Evolution and
  non-retrospective treatment. It establishes no lifecycle state or
  transition authority.

## 2. Evidence-Supported Observations

1. EV-005 requires review records to preserve unique stable identifiers,
   versions, authority, provenance, lifecycle status, history, evidence
   references, limitations, dependencies, and audit trails. It connects RQ,
   evidence, session, finding, disposition, Decision Record, and later
   effect, while keeping returned or rejected records in the history. These
   are review-scoped controls.
2. EV-008 requires failed and prior versions to be preserved, correction
   authority and linked revision to be attributable, identifiers and history
   to remain intact, reopening triggers to be recorded, and dependency
   changes to update traceability. It prohibits silent rewrite but does not
   define a general information lifecycle.
3. EV-012 records OQ-021, “Which governance-chain transitions require
   explicit approval, and who owns each transition record?”, and OQ-022,
   “Are Approved and Active separate research-standard states, and what
   authority controls every lifecycle transition?” Both remain open and
   supply no transition or ownership rule.
4. EV-013 proposes research-specific lifecycle, transition, archival,
   supersession, withdrawal, migration, effective-date, and authority
   terminology. Because EV-013 is Not Approved, none of those proposed
   states or transitions is current governance.
5. EV-023 defines bounded Evidence Pack identity, version, freeze,
   successor, transfer, restriction, accessibility, redaction, archival,
   retention-routing, and traceability controls. Its Evidence Pack scope and
   retained draft header prevent treating those controls as a universal
   information-lifecycle model.
6. EV-059 is an operated correction example: v1.1 corrects an unauthorised
   closure classification while preserving the conflicting v1.0 claim in
   repository and document history. The corrected version governs active
   reliance, but the example was performed and checked by the same combined
   capacity and does not establish a general transition rule.
7. EV-069 gives attributable Founder authority that a frozen Evidence Pack
   remains immutable and versioned; material change requires prior-pack
   preservation, an attributable successor or Supplemental Pack, impact
   assessment, renewed validation, renewed freeze, and explicit session and
   output linkage. Detailed successor, reopening, supersession, and
   framework-wide lifecycle mechanics remain provisional for D5.
8. EV-070 gives attributable Founder authority that substantive governance
   outputs trace through precise Evidence Records, RQs, sessions, findings,
   later Decisions, and authorised effects. Controlled reuse preserves
   identity, version, class, authority, admissibility, limitations,
   permitted use, and exact purpose without authority elevation. Complete
   D2–D8 mechanics and permanent D6 architecture remain outside the
   decision.
9. EV-071 gives attributable Founder authority for custody to preserve
   identity, provenance, access state, integrity, versions, pack custody,
   and traceability without conferring evidential meaning, approval, or
   Founder authority. Broader information-lifecycle governance remains
   outside the decision.
10. EV-073 demonstrates one complete, validated D3 chain from each RQ
    through evidence, EP-003, S03, finding, Founder disposition, Decision
    Record, and domain closure. It is a dated closed-domain example, not an
    operated full-chain test across creation, transfer, restriction,
    archival, and authorised disposition.
11. EV-074 requires improvements after the Phase 2 milestone to be
    prospective Framework Evolution and prohibits retrospective alteration
    or reinterpretation of closed D1–D3 domains. It does not select
    lifecycle states, transition authority, D5 rules, D6 architecture, or
    Framework Evolution content.

## 4. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The mapped evidence does not itself decide:

- whether a FEF-wide information-lifecycle integrity standard or minimum
  cross-record checkpoint set should be established;
- which creation, maintenance, correction, transfer, archival, restriction,
  withdrawal, or authorised-disposition transitions require approval,
  validation, ownership, entry/exit criteria, or a preserved transition
  record;
- whether and how Draft, Approved, Active, Superseded, Withdrawn, Archived,
  or other lifecycle states should be distinguished;
- what evidence would demonstrate full-chain identity, lineage,
  completeness, accessibility, and reproducibility across custodians or
  systems;
- which future D5 rules should govern applicability, supersession,
  withdrawal, controlled evolution, and transitional treatment; or
- which future D6 structures should administer identifiers, registers,
  checkpoints, and transition records.

These are matters for later Founder judgement and separately governed D5 or
D6 work. This finding selects no answer and makes no recommendation.

## 5. Conclusion

The mapped evidence establishes bounded integrity controls within the
Founder Governance Review and its Evidence Packs: stable identity and
versioning, preserved prior and failed states, attributable correction,
immutable frozen baselines, source and limitation preservation, custody,
controlled reuse, and end-to-end evidence-to-decision traceability. EV-059
also demonstrates one correction that preserved a contradictory prior
claim, and EV-073 demonstrates one complete closed-domain chain.

The same corpus does not establish a FEF-wide end-to-end
information-lifecycle integrity model, general checkpoints and owners,
approved lifecycle states, transition authority, or an operated full-chain
test through authorised disposition. OQ-021 and OQ-022 remain open;
EV-013 and EV-023 are authority-limited; EV-059 remains one
same-capacity correction with a preserved contradiction; EV-069 expressly
defers detailed mechanics to D5; and EV-070, EV-071, EV-073, and EV-074
retain evidence, single-domain, or prospective limits. Broader lifecycle
and administrative treatment therefore requires Founder judgement and
separately governed D5/D6 work.

## 6. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

EV-059 contains the mapped corpus's substantive contradiction: its v1.0
text claimed an unauthorised closure position, while v1.1 corrects that
classification and preserves the prior claim in repository and document
history. The correction supports preserved-history and active-reliance
treatment but also demonstrates that the bounded control environment did
not prevent the original defect. The contradiction remains explicit and is
not resolved into a universal rule.

EV-023 supplies later-chain Evidence Pack controls and EV-069 makes the
immutable-freeze principle authoritative, but neither establishes a
framework-wide lifecycle. EV-013 proposes broader lifecycle terminology,
but its Not Approved status controls. EV-073 supplies a complete D3 chain,
but it is not a full information-handling transition test.

No absence outside EP-004 or outside RQ-031's eleven-item mapping is
claimed.

- EV-013 and EV-023 remain Conditionally Admitted within their exact
  authority and scope limits.

  preserved.

  framework-wide lifecycle mechanics provisional for D5.
- EV-070 and EV-071 establish traceability, reuse, and custody boundaries,
  not complete lifecycle mechanics.
- EV-073 is one domain-specific snapshot.
- EV-074 supplies prospective and non-retrospective boundaries only.
- No D5 lifecycle state, activation model, applicability rule,
  supersession rule, withdrawal rule, D6 architecture, automated workflow,

  validation is non-independent.

full-chain test is mapped, and D5/D6 remain unexamined.
