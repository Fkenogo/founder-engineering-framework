## 1. RACI Key

| Code | Meaning |

| R | Responsible — performs the governed activity |

## 3. Activity Clarifications

### 3.1 Register Administration

The Review Administrator is accountable for the coherent register system. The Review Recorder is responsible for record-entry exactness where recording is required. The Evidence Custodian is responsible for evidence and pack register administration.

### 3.2 Review Question Admission

Admission is an operational scope gate under DG-2. The Review Administrator is accountable and responsible only after Validator input. Admission does not answer the RQ.

### 3.3 Evidence Admissibility

The Evidence Custodian performs the source and custody checks. The Validator is accountable for the validation outcome and participates in the mandatory joint confirmation. Neither role decides the governance conclusion.

### 3.4 Founder Decisions

Only the Founder is responsible and accountable for issuing an FD. Other roles may prepare inputs, record exact wording, validate the record, or remain informed, but cannot select or infer the decision.

### 3.5 Constitutional Candidate Extraction

The Review Analyst prepares candidate extraction, the Review Administrator controls the process, and the Validator tests the candidate criteria. The Founder is consulted where the source decision or scope requires clarification. Candidate extraction creates no constitutional authority.

### 3.6 Review Completion

Operational roles assemble and validate completion evidence. Only the Founder may provide the final Founder completion disposition required by the Charter.

## 4. Combination Rule

Where one individual holds multiple roles, the matrix remains role-based. Every action must still record the capacity used. No combined assignment merges R, A, C, or I authority.

## 5. Current Effect

This matrix assigns responsibility to roles, not individuals. It authorises no review activity without a valid role assignment and the applicable gate.
