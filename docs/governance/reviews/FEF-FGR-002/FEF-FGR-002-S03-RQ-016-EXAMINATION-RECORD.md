### 2.2 Supported Observations

The following are interpretations grounded in the established evidence
above, offered with the stated confidence level, not certainty:

1. Assurance in FEF-FGR-002, as evidenced by the D1 and D2 records mapped
   to this RQ, is applied as a **sequence of layered, separately-timed
   passes** at distinct transition points — evidence registration,
   evidence validation, session entry, session opening reconfirmation,
   and post-session output validation — rather than as a single
   checkpoint. Every mapped record examined here belongs to exactly one
   of these layers and none purports to substitute for another.
2. Two distinct, evidenced mechanisms bear on "proportionate assurance":
   (a) **evidence-class tiering** — EV-050 shows sources are classed
   Admitted / Conditionally Admitted / Context Only, and EV-063's
   Context Only status is the operative example of a source whose
   permitted use is deliberately restricted (excluded from CE1–CE6
   authority) rather than treated as equal to Admitted evidence; and
   (b) an **absence of transition-authority tiering** — none of the
   mapped records (EV-024, EV-025, EV-028, EV-050–053) apply a visibly
   different or heavier control specifically because a transition
   approaches or purports to exercise Founder-reserved authority (e.g.
   RQ admission) rather than being purely administrative (e.g. evidence
   acquisition); the same disclosure-and-compensating-control template
   recurs at every layer regardless of stakes.
3. The EV-058 episode is evidence that mechanism (b)'s absence has a
   material consequence: a transition that purported to admit into
   Founder-reserved authority (DG-2-equivalent RQ admission) passed
   through an operational-capacity gate sequence without triggering any
   deterministic check failure, and was distinguished from a properly
   admitted transition only by the presence or absence of a distinct,
   non-self-generated Founder-origin input — not by any depth of
   operational checklist.
4. A candidate evidence-bounded proportionality factor, drawn from
   mapped evidence only, is therefore: **whether a transition purports to
   exercise or admit into Founder-reserved authority.** Where it does,
   the evidence in this RQ's mapped set indicates a minimum condition —
   a distinct, attributable, non-self-generated Founder-origin input —
   that is not satisfied by any amount of operational-capacity checking
   alone, however extensive.
