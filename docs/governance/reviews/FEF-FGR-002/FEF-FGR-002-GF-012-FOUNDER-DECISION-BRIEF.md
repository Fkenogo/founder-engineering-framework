## 1. Exact Finding

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-012 — Frozen Evidence Baselines Require Versioned Change Control**

> A frozen Evidence Pack is an immutable, versioned examination baseline rather
> than a truth determination. Material additions, removals, replacements,
> admissibility changes, or integrity changes require preservation of the prior
> pack, an attributable successor or Supplemental Pack, impact assessment,
> revalidation, re-freeze, and explicit session linkage before reliance.

Lifecycle state: `Presented — Founder disposition pending`.

## 2. Decision Question

Should the Founder accept the current immutable freeze and versioned-change
principle for FEF governance reviews, and, if so, should supplement and
successor-pack controls remain provisional until operated?

The question separates current review reproducibility from a general D5
framework lifecycle.

## 3. Evidence Basis

EV-005–EV-008; EV-022–EV-023; and EV-026–EV-029. Exact citations remain in
the [S02 Governance Finding Set](FEF-FGR-002-S02-GOVERNANCE-FINDINGS.md).
No evidence outside EP-002 is introduced.

## 4. What Is Established

- A frozen pack is a versioned examination baseline, not a truth determination.
- Frozen content cannot be mutated in place without destroying reproducibility.
- The prior baseline, impact, validation, freeze state, and session linkage
  must remain attributable when material change occurs.
- Initial freeze, hashing, entry verification, and session use were operated
  without post-freeze evidence.

## 5. What Is Not Established

- Successful operation of a Supplemental Pack or successor major version.
- A complete framework-wide lifecycle, supersession, reopening, or archival
  model.
- Independent custody and validation.
- External mutable-source or restricted-source change controls.
- General approval of FEF-EPS-001, which retains a draft-status header.

## 6. Decision Need

The current review already uses frozen-pack controls. A new Founder decision is
potentially required to accept the freeze principle as enduring governance or
to impose conditions. The Founder can accept immutable freeze while retaining
supplement/successor mechanics as provisional pending their first governed use.
