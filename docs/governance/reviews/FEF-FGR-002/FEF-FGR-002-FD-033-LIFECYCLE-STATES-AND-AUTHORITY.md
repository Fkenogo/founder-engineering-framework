## 1. Finding Reference

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-031 — Bounded Draft, Founder-Approval, and Effective-State Examples Are Evidenced, but No Approved FEF-Wide Instrument Lifecycle or Delegated Transition-Authority Model Is Established**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

**Founder Observations**

FEF needs a clear minimum lifecycle vocabulary, but the different control
dimensions must not be collapsed into one status system.

**Discussion**

The Founder considered GF-031 and its authorised lean conditions. No additional
discussion is attributed.

**Disposition**

Accept with Conditions

**Conditions**

Common conditions applying to GF-031 through GF-036:

1. Acceptance confirms the finding, not a proposed solution.
2. Any future FEF control must remain a minimum cross-project foundation.
3. Project-specific lifecycle detail remains under project authority unless a
   universal requirement is demonstrated.
4. Founder-reserved authority cannot be delegated by implication.
5. Existing valid work and historical records must not be retrospectively
   altered without explicit authority.
6. No repository-wide reconciliation, renumbering, reclassification or
   migration programme is authorised.
7. Implementation design requires separately governed follow-up after Founder
   Decision issuance.
8. All eight applicable Open Questions remain open unless separately resolved.

Finding-specific Conditions:

1. Document lifecycle, approval, effectiveness, validation outcome and session
   state must remain distinguishable.
2. Any common vocabulary must be limited to essential cross-project states.
3. Founder approval remains mandatory for Founder-reserved instruments and
   constitutional effects.
4. Administrative transitions may be delegated only through explicit authority.
5. Existing artefacts must not be retrospectively relabelled merely to achieve
   consistency.

**Rationale**

The current practices are useful but inconsistent. A small common core will
improve authority and traceability without forcing every project into one
workflow.

**Follow-up Actions**

Carry the finding into separately authorised lifecycle-control design after
Founder Decision issuance.

## 3. Exact Finding Scope

**Confidence: Moderate to High for the bounded finding; insufficient for a
universal model.** The directly attributable records strongly support the
specific examples and the Founder-confirmation conclusion. The conditional
sources, small number of transition examples, vocabulary variation, and lack
of any delegated example prevent generalisation.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-032 and the nine mapped frozen records. It
does not decide RQ-033 through RQ-037 or any D6/D7 interface.

## 4. Exact Finding Non-Effects
