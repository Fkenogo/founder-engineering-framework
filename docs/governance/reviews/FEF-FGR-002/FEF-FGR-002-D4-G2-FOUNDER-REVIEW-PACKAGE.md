## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> Which classes of governance record, if any, require controlled creation and preservation within FEF, and what minimum characteristics are necessary for each class to remain attributable, understandable, authoritative, and auditable without prescribing an implementation system?

This examination used only the six Evidence Records mapped to RQ-025 in
EP-004 v1.0: EV-005, EV-007, EV-008, EV-013, EV-072, and EV-073. No
unmapped evidence was introduced.

The evidence qualifications remain controlling:

- EV-005 is E2 Admitted within the Founder-approved review Charter only;
- EV-007 is E2 Admitted within the Founder-approved review Plan only;
- EV-008 is E2 Admitted as the review execution-rule baseline only;
- EV-013 is E2 Conditionally Admitted and remains a Draft v0.2,
  Founder Review Required, Not Approved research-specific proposal;
- EV-072 is E2 Conditionally Admitted as a mutable, non-authoritative
  programme index whose source records control; and
- EV-073 is E4 Admitted as one validated D3 operational traceability

## 2. Evidence-Supported Observations

1. EV-005 identifies review-scoped record types through canonical
   identifier patterns for Reviews, Sessions, Evidence Records, Review
   Questions, Governance Findings, Founder Decisions, Validation Findings,
   and Deferred Matters. It requires identifiers to be unique, immutable,
   reserved if withdrawn, status-bearing rather than deleted, and cited
   canonically.
2. EV-005 prescribes type-specific control fields. Its Evidence Record
   controls include identity, source, provenance, dates, version, integrity,
   class, authority and scope, access, precise references, relationships,
   conflicts, limitations, uncertainty, admissibility, validation, and
   retention location. Its required traceability chain links Evidence
   Record, Review Question, Session Record, Governance Finding, Founder
   Decision when required, Open Question disposition, any later candidate
   or domain-standard implication, and separate downstream work.
3. EV-007 identifies the operational artefact classes required for this
   review and requires evidence packs to have identity, manifest,
   Evidence-Record citation, admissibility and limitation treatment,
   version and freeze control, integrity, preserved prior versions, and
   RQ/session mappings. These are review controls, not a general FEF
   records standard.

   iteration uses new versions or linked records, preserves identifiers

   These rules are bounded to review execution.
5. EV-072 shows an operated programme index using controlled path, document
   type, lifecycle status, and governance-effect fields. Because EV-072 is
   mutable and non-authoritative, the listing demonstrates current index
   practice only. It does not establish that any listed class or field is
   required, complete, authoritative, or sufficient.
6. EV-073 shows one operated record that preserves identity, version,
   lifecycle, validation, and end-to-end relationships across RQ,
   Evidence, EP-003, S03, Governance Finding, and Founder Decision. It

   recurring characteristics are visible: unique identity; controlled
   source or location; version, date, status, or history; authority and
   scope; relationships and traceability; integrity or validation;
   limitations and uncertainty; and preservation. The mapped evidence

## 5. Conclusion

FEF-FGR-002's approved review instruments expressly identify multiple
review-scoped record types and prescribe type-specific controls that
preserve identity, authority, integrity, limitations, history, and
traceability. EV-072 and EV-073 show that record categorisation and
end-to-end traceability are also operated in the repository. The mapped
corpus therefore supports the existence of controlled, auditable
review-scoped record practice.

The same corpus does not evidence an approved FEF-wide governance-record
taxonomy or one authoritative minimum characteristic set applying to every
FEF record class. EV-013 contains broader research-specific artefact and
metadata proposals, but it is expressly Not Approved; EV-072 is a
non-authoritative index; and EV-073 is a single domain-specific example.
Whether to establish enduring FEF-wide classes or minimum characteristics
therefore requires Founder judgement and cannot be inferred from current
practice.
