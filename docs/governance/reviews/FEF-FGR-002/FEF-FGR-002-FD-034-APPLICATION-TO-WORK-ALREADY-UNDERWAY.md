## 1. Finding Reference

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-032 — Decision-Specific Commencement and Defect-Correction Treatments Are Evidenced, but No General Transitional-Applicability Rule or Delegated Rule-Selection Model Is Established**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

**Founder Observations**

Governance changes should apply prospectively by default. Valid ongoing work
should not be repeatedly reopened whenever FEF changes.

**Discussion**

The Founder considered GF-032 and its authorised lean conditions. No additional
discussion is attributed.

**Disposition**

Accept with Conditions

**Conditions**

Common conditions applying to GF-031 through GF-036:

1. Acceptance confirms the finding, not a proposed solution.
2. Any future FEF control must remain a minimum cross-project foundation.
3. Project-specific lifecycle detail remains under project authority unless a
   universal requirement is demonstrated.
4. Founder-reserved authority cannot be delegated by implication.
5. Existing valid work and historical records must not be retrospectively
   altered without explicit authority.
6. No repository-wide reconciliation, renumbering, reclassification or
   migration programme is authorised.
7. Implementation design requires separately governed follow-up after Founder
   Decision issuance.
8. All eight applicable Open Questions remain open unless separately resolved.

Finding-specific Conditions:

1. Existing authorised work continues under its recorded governing baseline.
2. New requirements apply to new work and later governed transitions.
3. Retrospective application requires an explicit authorised decision.
4. The governing framework version must remain traceable.
5. Defective, unauthorised or corrupted work may require remediation, but that
   is separate from ordinary prospective transition.

**Rationale**

This protects continuity and prevents framework evolution from destabilising
legitimate project delivery.

**Follow-up Actions**

Define a minimal prospective-application rule through separately governed
design.

## 3. Exact Finding Scope

**Confidence: Moderate to High for the bounded examples; insufficient for a
general transitional model.** The attributable records strongly support the
specific commencement, recovery, correction, and traceability observations.
The absence of an ordinary-amendment example, the open OQ-017, the
non-authoritative draft, and the lack of a delegated example prevent
generalisation.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-033 and the eight mapped frozen records. It
does not decide RQ-034 through RQ-037, D6 administration, or D7 constitutional
transition.
