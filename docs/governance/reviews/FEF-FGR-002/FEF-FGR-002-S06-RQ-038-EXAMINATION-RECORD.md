## 3. Examination Results by Analytical Classification

### 3.1 Directly Established by Evidence

1. All five role and authority documents examined (EV-016 OGRS-001,
   EV-017 OAB-001, EV-018 ICR-001, EV-019 GRM-001, EV-021 RAR-001) state
   their own effective scope as **FEF-FGR-002 only**. OGRS-001 §"Effective
   scope" states "FEF-FGR-002 only"; RAR-001's six assignments are each
   recorded as "OpenAI Codex agent executing FEF-FGR-002-002"; none
   defines, mentions, or bounds a Framework-level or cross-project
   administrative role.
2. EV-080 directly establishes one operated single-document-ownership
   example: Master Programme §1.1 designates the Master Programme itself
   as "the single authoritative source of programme state," and names the
   Founder Dashboard, Review Identity, and Document Manifest as
   "consumers of that state, not independent controllers of it." This
   model is stated and operated entirely among four documents belonging
   to one Founder project's own governance review (FEF-FGR-002), not
   across multiple separate Founder projects.
3. EV-086 directly confirms its own consumer role: "This Dashboard is a
   navigation and executive-view document. It summarises and links to the
   Master Programme; it does not independently maintain, assert, or
   duplicate programme state."
4. EV-072 directly records, in its own header, "**Framework status:**
   Draft — Not Yet Adoptable," and separately states it "registers the
   existence, location, and category of controlled artefacts only" and
   "is not an independent programme-status record." EV-080 separately
   records "Framework release: None" in its Current Programme Position
   table.
5. EV-080 contains exactly one reference to "Founder projects" (plural)
   in the mapped source: the FEF-FEV-001 Framework Evolution Intake
   narrative, describing the intake as "the controlled mechanism through
   which future engineering lessons **from** Founder projects will be
   submitted, evaluated, and prioritised for possible Framework
   Evolution." This describes projects as a future source of lessons
   flowing into the Framework, not as adopters bound by, or holding
   delegated authority under, any Framework-level administrative
   responsibility.
6. RAR-001 directly establishes that operational role assignment is
   explicitly not delegation of Founder authority (OAB-001 §10,
   "Non-Delegation Rule": "No assignment... may be interpreted as
   permission to exercise Founder or constitutional authority").

### 3.2 Reasonably Supported

1. The mapped evidence reasonably supports that, within a single
   governed document set, appropriate stewardship can be achieved by
   designating exactly one document as authoritative and treating the
   rest as consumers — this is the operated pattern EV-080 and EV-086
   jointly demonstrate. The support is bounded to that one demonstrated
   document set (Master Programme, Dashboard, Review Identity, Manifest),
   not to Framework-wide administration generally.
2. The evidence reasonably supports that formal operational-role
   definition (OGRS-001/GRM-001/RAR-001) and Framework-level
   administrative responsibility are, at present, two separate and
   undeveloped questions: the former is fully defined but scoped to one
   review; the latter has no defining instrument in the mapped evidence
   at all.

### 3.3 Unsupported

The mapped evidence does not support:

- any minimum cross-project administrative responsibility currently
  exercised, or proposed to be exercised, at the Framework level — no
  such responsibility is evidenced, because no second adopting Founder
  project yet exists against which one could be evidenced (EV-072's
  "Not Yet Adoptable" status and EV-080's "Framework release: None" are
  the direct basis for this absence);
- any delegation boundary between Framework-level and individual-project
  administrative responsibility, for the same reason;
- a conclusion that the single-document-ownership (Single Source of
  Truth) pattern demonstrated in EV-080/EV-086 generalises beyond the
  one document set it currently governs;
- a conclusion that the FEF-FGR-002 operational role model (Founder,
  Review Administrator, Review Analyst, Review Recorder, Evidence
  Custodian, Validator) is intended to extend to, or govern,
  cross-project Framework administration; and
- any specific ownership or stewardship assignment — none is evidenced,
  and per FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001 §3.1's own exclusion (carried into
  RQ-038's Exclusions field), none is to be assigned by this examination
  either.

### 3.4 Contradictory or Qualifying Matters

No mapped source directly contradicts another. Material qualifications
remain:

- EV-072 is Conditionally Admitted as a mutable, non-authoritative index;
  its "Not Yet Adoptable" header is used here only as the Manifest's own
  current self-description, not as a controlling Framework-wide
  determination;
- EV-080's §1.1 describes the Single Source of Truth model as adopted "as
  a permanent programme-governance architecture principle" — this
  permanence characterisation is noted here only as a qualifying fact
  about the source text; whether that characterisation should itself
  stand, or whether the pattern should generalise, is the distinct
  subject of RQ-043 and is not examined, tested, or relied upon in this
  RQ-038 finding; and
- the absence of any Framework-level administrative instrument is not,
  per Charter §9.3, read here as evidence that none should exist; it is
  recorded as an absence only.

### 3.5 Uncertain

It remains uncertain whether the single-document-ownership pattern
operated within FEF-FGR-002's own control documents would be an
appropriate or sufficient model for Framework-level administration once
other Founder projects adopt the Framework; whether the existing
FEF-FGR-002 role model is intended to extend to Framework-wide
administration or whether a distinct model is intended; and what, if
any, minimum administrative responsibility a Framework-level authority
should hold once cross-project adoption exists.

## 5. Examination Conclusion

The evidence answers RQ-038 only at a bounded finding level. It
establishes that every existing FEF-FGR-002 role, authority, and
responsibility instrument is explicitly scoped to this review only; that
the Framework itself records its own status as "Draft — Not Yet
Adoptable" with "Framework release: None"; and that the only operated
single-document-ownership example (Single Source of Truth) currently
governs four documents within this one review, not multiple Founder
projects. It does not establish, and the evidence does not yet permit
establishing, any minimum cross-project administrative responsibility at
the Framework level or any delegation boundary to individual Founder
projects, because no second adopting project yet exists.

One candidate Governance Finding, FEF-FGR-002-GF-037, records this
bounded positive-and-gap conclusion. No recommendation or Founder
disposition is embedded in it.
