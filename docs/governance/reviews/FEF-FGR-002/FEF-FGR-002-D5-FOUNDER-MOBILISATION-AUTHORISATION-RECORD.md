## 3. Permitted Outcomes

| Outcome | Effect if Selected |
|---|---|
| **Approve** | D5 mobilisation becomes effective with no conditions; D5 Review Question candidate preparation becomes the next separately governed activity |
| **Approve with Conditions** | D5 mobilisation becomes effective subject to the exact conditions recorded; those conditions carry forward as binding on subsequent D5 preparation and execution |
| **Return for Clarification** | D5 mobilisation does not become effective; the Mobilisation Record, Scope and Boundary Assessment, or Dependency and Interface Map is returned for a named clarification before resubmission |
| **Defer** | D5 mobilisation does not become effective at this time; no further D5 preparation proceeds until a later, separately authorised reconsideration |
| **Reject** | D5 mobilisation does not become effective; the prepared package is preserved as historical record and D5 remains Not Started pending a different future approach |

No outcome is selected, recommended, or implied by this prepared record.

## 4. Authorisation Effect (Not Yet Applied)
