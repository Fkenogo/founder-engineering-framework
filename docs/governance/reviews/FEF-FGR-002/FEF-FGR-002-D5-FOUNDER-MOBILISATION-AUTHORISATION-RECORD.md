**Founder Disposition:** APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS

The mobilisation package is approved to proceed to Founder Mobilisation Authorisation. The review concludes that the implementation has correctly preserved the governance boundary between mobilisation planning and substantive review execution. No rework is required before recording the Founder mobilisation disposition.

**Founder Rationale:** The implementation demonstrates good governance discipline. In particular: D1–D4 remain unchanged and closed. D5 correctly remains Not Started. No Review Questions have been created. No Evidence Sources have been admitted. No Evidence Pack has been assembled. No Review Session has been opened. No Governance Findings have been produced. No Founder Decisions have been created. This confirms that mobilisation planning has been completed without inadvertently commencing the review itself. The repository remains internally consistent and the programme state correctly reflects that D5 mobilisation planning has been prepared and is awaiting Founder authorisation.

**Conditions:**

Condition 1: Founder mobilisation approval authorises commencement of the D5 review process only. It does not authorise constitutional changes or governance amendments outside the controlled D5 review.

Condition 2: The identified Agenda/Charter constitutional-boundary ambiguity remains an examination subject within D5. It is not considered resolved by this mobilisation package.

Condition 3: Existing deferred Framework Evolution items (including CE1–CE6, FEC-001, FEF-CCF-001 and FRAS) remain outside D5 unless they are explicitly admitted through the governed D5 evidence process.

Condition 4: The blank Founder Mobilisation Authorisation Record shall remain neutral until the Founder disposition is formally recorded.

**Follow-up:** Proceed with recording the Founder Mobilisation Authorisation (Approve with Conditions), validation of that disposition, and repository synchronisation. No additional mobilisation work is required before that step.
