## 1. Finding Reference

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-039 — A Narrative Dependency Register Exists and Is Operated Across Documents Without Halting Programme Progress, but No Cross-Project Treatment, Release-Readiness Criteria, or Verified Consistency Mechanism Is Evidenced**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

**Disposition**

Accept

**Founder Reflection**

The Framework should continue treating dependency management as a
mechanism for sequencing work rather than restricting progress.

Dependencies should preserve governance integrity while allowing
independent work to proceed wherever constitutional boundaries permit.

Governance should coordinate readiness rather than unnecessarily delay
execution.

No Conditions, additional Discussion, or Follow-up Actions were supplied
by the Founder beyond the Disposition and Founder Reflection recorded
above. The Founder Reflection is explanatory only; it creates no
governance authority, Framework policy, or implementation instruction.

## 3. Exact Finding Scope

**Confidence: High for the bounded observation that a dependency register
exists, is operated, and has coexisted with continued programme progress;
not applicable to any cross-project model or release-readiness standard,
because neither is evidenced to assess; genuinely inconclusive for
whether cross-document consistency is a verified property.** The directly
cited register entries and the directly cited cross-document reference to
FEF-P0-004 strongly support the bounded conclusion. The absence of any
second adopting project and any release-readiness criterion is the direct
and sufficient basis for those two negative findings.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-040 and the two mapped frozen records. It
does not decide RQ-038 or RQ-039 (already answered in GF-037 and GF-038),
RQ-041 through RQ-043, or any D7 interface. It does not dispose of
FEF-P0-004.

## 4. Exact Finding Non-Effects
