## 1. Decision Question

What attributable disposition and enduring treatment applies to
FEF-FGR-002-GF-013?

## 2. Exact Validated Finding

> Every substantive governance output must trace to precise Evidence Records and
> their permitted use through the RQ, session, finding, and any later Founder
> Decision. Cross-domain reuse must preserve source identity, version, class,
> authority, admissibility, limitations, and purpose mapping and must not elevate
> authority, substitute a summary for the controlling source, or conflate
> evidence with assertion or conclusion.

## 3. Exact Founder Disposition and Decision

**Disposition: Accept with Conditions**

Accept mandatory evidence-to-output traceability for substantive FEF governance
outputs.

Traceability shall connect precise Evidence Records through:

- Review Questions;
- sessions;
- Governance Findings;
- later Founder Decisions;
- any subsequent authorised governance or implementation effect.

Controlled reuse shall preserve:

- source identity;
- version;
- evidence class;
- source authority;
- admissibility;
- limitations;
- permitted use;
- exact reuse purpose.

Reuse shall not:

- elevate source authority;
- substitute a summary for the controlling source;
- convert Context Only or conditional evidence into unrestricted evidence;
- conflate evidence, assertion, interpretation, and conclusion.

This disposition does not establish:

- the permanent D6 register architecture;
- a general FEF-P1-002 authority/source hierarchy;
- complete D2–D8 reuse mechanics;
- constitutional extraction rules.

Those remain subject to later governed treatment.

## 4. Decision Treatment

Mandatory evidence-to-output traceability and no-authority-elevation reuse are
accepted within the exact conditions. Permanent register architecture,
framework hierarchy, complete cross-domain mechanics, and constitutional
extraction remain outside the decision.

## 5. Rationale, Conditions, and Limitations

No separate Founder rationale was supplied. The disposition itself supplies
the controlling conditions and exclusions. Current review operation supports
traceability; complete D2–D8 reuse has not yet been operated.
