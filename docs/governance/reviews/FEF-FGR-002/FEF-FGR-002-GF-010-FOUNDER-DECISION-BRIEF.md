## 1. Exact Finding

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-010 — Evidence Class Does Not Automatically Determine Weight**

> Evidence class constrains the kind of proposition a source may support but does
> not automatically determine decisive weight. Contested weight requires an
> explicit rationale addressing exact authority, scope, provenance, relevance,
> temporal state, corroboration, contradiction, and uncertainty; repetition or
> automation cannot substitute for human or Founder judgement.

Lifecycle state: `Presented — Founder disposition pending`.

## 2. Decision Question

Should the Founder accept the principle that evidence class constrains
permitted use but cannot automatically determine decisive weight, while leaving
any detailed hierarchy or conflict-priority model for later evidence and
governance?

The question does not require the Founder to approve a complete weighting
hierarchy.

## 3. Evidence Basis

EV-005–EV-008; EV-012; EV-014; and EV-022–EV-026. Exact citations are
preserved in the [S02 Governance Finding Set](FEF-FGR-002-S02-GOVERNANCE-FINDINGS.md).
No evidence outside EP-002 is introduced.

## 4. What Is Established

- Evidence classes constrain permitted use rather than create an automatic
  ordinal rank.
- Repetition is not corroboration.
- Admitted, Conditionally Admitted, and Context Only evidence may receive
  different reliance treatments.
- A materially contested weighting judgement requires an explicit,
  attributable rationale and cannot be automated.

## 5. What Is Not Established

- A complete evidence hierarchy, scoring model, conflict-priority rule, or
  quantitative corroboration threshold.
- Treatment of a live conflict between equally authoritative sources.
- The relative weight of E3 legal, privacy, security, professional, or
  project-specific evidence; EP-002 contains no E3 source.
- A D3 assurance model.
- Independent validation of the finding.

## 6. Decision Need

Current review controls already prohibit automatic or preference-based
weighting. Founder authority is required to accept that boundary as enduring
governance or to impose conditions.

A bounded disposition can accept the no-automation and explicit-rationale
principle without approving a detailed hierarchy, conflict-priority model, or
quantitative threshold.
