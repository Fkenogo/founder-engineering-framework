## 3. Permitted Disposition Vocabulary

Each item may later receive one of the existing FEF-FGR-002 permitted
treatments:

- Accept;
- Accept with Conditions;
- Return for Additional Evidence;
- Require Independent Revalidation;
- Reject;
- Defer, only with attributable rationale, dependencies, and a reconsideration
  trigger.

This agenda does not add `Approve in Part` or `No Decision Required` as new
lifecycle labels. A bounded treatment may be expressed through exact Founder
conditions without changing the controlled vocabulary.

## 4. Review Boundary

The Founder may change the consideration order. Each finding must retain a
separate attributable disposition because its evidence, conditions, and
later-domain interfaces differ.

## 5. Non-Effects

This agenda selects no option, drafts or allocates no Founder Decision,
modifies no finding or Open Question, creates no candidate or deferral, and
creates no constitutional or downstream authority effect.
