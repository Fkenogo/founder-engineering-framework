## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What governance criteria, authority, and traceability, if any, are required to determine retention, preservation review, archival transfer, and continued accessibility of FEF records without prescribing storage technology, fixed retention periods, or implementation procedures?

This examination used only the nine Evidence Records mapped to RQ-027 in
EP-004 v1.0: EV-005, EV-007, EV-008, EV-012, EV-013, EV-016, EV-023,
EV-069, and EV-071. No unmapped evidence was introduced.

The evidence qualifications remain controlling:

- EV-005, EV-007, and EV-008 are E2 Admitted within the approved review
  Charter, Plan, and execution-rule boundaries;
- EV-012 is E2 Admitted as an Open Questions Register entry that records
  OQ-011 but supplies no answer;
- EV-013 is E2 Conditionally Admitted and remains Draft v0.2, Founder
  Review Required, Not Approved, with research-specific proposals only;
- EV-016 is E2 Admitted as a review-operational role standard only;
- EV-023 is E2 Conditionally Admitted as an Evidence Pack preparation
  baseline with a retained draft header, not as a generally approved
  Framework standard;
- EV-069 is E1 Admitted for its exact frozen-evidence-baseline decision,
  while detailed lifecycle mechanics remain provisional for D5; and
- EV-071 is E1 Admitted for its exact evidence-custody boundary and does
  not decide retention schedules or archival architecture.

## 2. Evidence-Supported Observations

1. EV-005 establishes review-scoped preservation controls: canonical
   identifiers remain immutable and reserved rather than deleted; Evidence
   Records identify a retention location where applicable; conflicts,
   uncertainty, prior states, and exact decisions remain traceable; and
   unavailable repository sources retain location, access route, integrity,
   custodian, and lawful-access information.
2. EV-007 assigns retention and archival examination to D4 while assigning
   status, applicability, supersession, withdrawal, and controlled evolution
   to D5. It also requires evidence-pack updates to preserve prior versions
   rather than overwrite them. This establishes a domain boundary, not a
   substantive retention or archival outcome.
3. EV-008 requires failed versions, preserved prior state, identifiers,
   history, linked records, and traceability to remain available through
   correction, reopening, and iteration. These are review-execution
   controls, not retention schedules or archive-transfer rules.
4. EV-012 records OQ-011: “What retention, archival, legal-hold, and
   deletion rules apply to research records?” Its status remains an open
   policy dependency that blocks final preservation controls. The Open
   Question evidences unresolved scope and authority; it supplies no rule.
5. EV-016 assigns the Evidence Custodian review-scoped responsibility for
   evidence identity, provenance, integrity, preservation, access, Evidence
   Packs, and superseded or supplemental versions. It does not create an
   enduring records archivist, retention authority, or general FEF office.
6. EV-023 defines bounded Evidence Pack controls for preserved copies or
   stable references, retention routes, prior-version continuity, archived
   pack state, successor versions, transfer verification, and continued
   authorised accessibility. Its lifecycle and transfer controls apply to
   Evidence Packs within its preparation-baseline scope only.
7. EV-069 supplies attributable Founder authority for one enduring bounded
   control: a frozen Evidence Pack is an immutable, versioned record of the
   examined evidence baseline and must retain its identity, version,
   manifest, integrity evidence, limitations, and session linkage. Material
   change requires preservation of the prior pack and an attributable,
   revalidated, re-frozen successor or Supplemental Pack before reliance.
   Detailed successor, supersession, reopening, and framework-wide lifecycle
   mechanics remain expressly provisional for D5.
8. EV-071 supplies attributable Founder authority for evidence custody to
   preserve evidence identity, provenance, access state, integrity,
   versions, pack custody, and traceability. It expressly states that the
   decision does not establish retention schedules or archival architecture
   and leaves those subjects for D4.
9. EV-013 proposes research-specific preservation and archival terminology:
   research closure preserves controlled deliverables and supporting
   records, while retention periods, archival formats, deletion authority,
   archival and retention authority, review cadence, and withdrawal remain
   open. Because EV-013 is Not Approved, these statements evidence proposed
   context and unresolved questions only.

## 4. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The mapped evidence does not itself decide:

- whether FEF-wide retention criteria, triggers, durations, or review
  cadence should be established;
- who may set, approve, vary, review, expire, or escalate a retention
  decision or exception;
- what criteria and authority should govern archival transfer, destination
  acceptance, migration, preservation continuity, and loss treatment;

  requirements should apply;
- how any applicable external obligations should be identified and
  reconciled; or

These are matters for later Founder judgement. This finding selects no

## 5. Conclusion

The mapped evidence establishes bounded preservation governance within
FEF-FGR-002 and its Evidence Packs. Identifiers, history, prior states,
access information, traceability, superseded versions, and frozen evidence
baselines are preserved, and material change to a frozen pack requires an
attributable successor path rather than in-place mutation. EV-069 and
EV-071 provide attributable Founder authority for those exact evidence-pack
and evidence-custody boundaries.

The same corpus does not establish FEF-wide retention criteria or schedules,
retention-decision and exception authority, archival-transfer governance,
continued-accessibility standards, recovery controls, or an operated
archive. OQ-011 remains open; EV-013 and EV-023 remain authority-limited;
and EV-069 explicitly leaves detailed lifecycle mechanics provisional for
D5. Broader retention and archival treatment therefore requires Founder
judgement and cannot be inferred from review-specific preservation or
Evidence Pack practice.

## 6. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source substantively contradicts the conclusion. EV-023 contains
an Evidence Pack lifecycle with `Archived`, transfer verification, retention
routes, and successor mechanisms, but its preparation-baseline scope and
retained draft header prevent treating those controls as a generally
approved records model. EV-013 proposes broader research preservation and
archival terminology, but its Not Approved status and express open questions
are controlling.

EV-069 accepts immutable Evidence Pack freeze and attributable change
control while expressly leaving detailed lifecycle mechanics provisional.
EV-071 accepts evidence preservation and custody while expressly excluding
retention schedules and archival architecture. Those qualifications narrow
rather than displace the conclusion.

No absence outside EP-004 or outside RQ-027's nine-item mapping is claimed.

## 7. Limitations and Uncertainty

- The finding is limited to the nine mapped EP-004 v1.0 Evidence Records.
- Most approved controls govern this review or its evidence, not all FEF
  records.
- Repository accessibility does not demonstrate archival durability,
  recovery capability, long-term accessibility, or successful transfer.
- EV-012 records OQ-011 as open and cannot answer it.
- EV-013 and EV-023 remain Conditionally Admitted within their exact limits.
- Legal hold, deletion, disposal, and irreversible restriction remain
  outside RQ-027 and are not decided here.
- No D5 lifecycle state or D6 administrative mechanism is inferred.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.

## 8. Confidence Statement

**Confidence: Moderate.** Review-scoped preservation and immutable frozen
Evidence Pack treatment are directly supported, including by E1 Founder
Decisions. Confidence is bounded because the wider sources are
authority-limited, OQ-011 remains open, no operated archival transfer is
mapped, and no FEF-wide retention or accessibility authority is evidenced.
