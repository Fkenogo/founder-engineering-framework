## 1. Exact Finding

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-013 — Traceability and Reuse Must Preserve Source Authority**

> Every substantive governance output must trace to precise Evidence Records and
> their permitted use through the RQ, session, finding, and any later Founder
> Decision. Cross-domain reuse must preserve source identity, version, class,
> authority, admissibility, limitations, and purpose mapping and must not elevate
> authority, substitute a summary for the controlling source, or conflate
> evidence with assertion or conclusion.

Lifecycle state: `Presented — Founder disposition pending`.

## 2. Decision Question

Should the Founder accept mandatory evidence-to-output traceability and
controlled reuse that preserves source authority, version, admissibility,
limitations, and purpose without authority elevation?

The question does not create a general document-authority hierarchy.

## 3. Evidence Basis

EV-005–EV-008; EV-012; and EV-022–EV-030. Exact citations remain in the
[S02 Governance Finding Set](FEF-FGR-002-S02-GOVERNANCE-FINDINGS.md). No
evidence outside EP-002 is introduced.

## 4. What Is Established

- Substantive outputs can be traced through Evidence Records, RQs, sessions,
  findings, and later Founder Decisions.
- Evidence, assertion, interpretation, and conclusion are distinct.
- Reuse is a new purpose mapping to a controlled source, not a new source.
- Reuse must preserve source identity, version, class, authority,
  admissibility, limitations, and permitted use.
- A summary or repeated citation cannot elevate source authority.

## 5. What Is Not Established

- Complete operation of reuse across D2–D8.
- A permanent D6 register architecture.
- A general authority/source hierarchy under FEF-P1-002.
- Treatment of every restricted or external source.
- Independent validation.
- Constitutional extraction or final-review reconciliation.

OQ-005 remains open.

## 6. Decision Need

Bidirectional traceability is already operated through D1 and S02. Founder
authority is required to accept mandatory enduring traceability or conditions
for cross-domain reuse.

A bounded disposition can accept precise evidence-to-output traceability and
the no-authority-elevation rule while reserving permanent register architecture
and untested future-domain reuse.
