## 1. Purpose and Authority

This record captures the Founder's disposition of each of the six D5
temporary Review Question candidates (D5-RQC-01 through D5-RQC-06),
presented from FEF-FGR-002-D5-G1-FOUNDER-REVIEW-PACKAGE and reviewed
individually and collectively during the D5-G1 Founder Review session held
on 2026-07-29.

The coding agent presented each candidate's exact wording, constitutional
intent, validation summary, and coverage notes without reinterpretation,
and did not recommend, suggest, or otherwise influence any disposition. The
Founder alone determined the disposition of every candidate.

This record does not itself allocate a canonical D5 Review Question
identifier, admit a Review Question, prepare a Review Question Set, qualify
or register evidence, create an Evidence Requirement Catalogue or Evidence
Pack, commence examination, create a Governance Finding, or perform DG-2
Admission. Those remain separately governed actions at a later, separately
authorised gate.
