## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What minimum administrative relationships and consistency controls, if
> any, are necessary among identifiers, registers, and controlled
> documents to preserve governance integrity — administrative coherence,
> as distinct from Single Source of Truth ownership — after D1, D4, and
> D5's controls, without renumbering, migrating, or redesigning existing
> records?

This finding uses only EV-072 (restricted to its frozen-era header and
Scope note), EV-081 (restricted to its structural schema as frozen at
v1.72), EV-087 (restricted to its Scope and Authority Note, present
unchanged since v1.70, within the v1.76 freeze pin), and EV-088
(restricted to §4 and §6), as frozen for RQ-041 in EP-006 v1.0/MAN-001.
No unmapped evidence or later live administrative version was used. No
identifier, register, or document is renumbered, migrated, reconciled,
or redesigned by this finding, and OQ-015 is not decided.

## 2. Finding

The mapped evidence establishes that a minimum administrative
relationship exists and currently operates between at least two
controlled documents and the Master Programme: both the Review Identity
(EV-087) and the Document Manifest (EV-072) carry an explicit, live
textual note declaring the Master Programme the Single Source of Truth
for programme-level state and stating that, where the two differ, the
Master Programme is controlling. This is more than documented intent —
it is the live text presently governing this review's own conduct,
directly relied upon throughout Loops 001–003. Separately, the Review
Question Register (EV-081) is directly established to apply a uniform,
fixed twelve-column schema across all 43 substantive entries recorded at
the time of freeze, providing a structural consistency mechanism
internal to that register. EV-088 shows that this general theme —
residual ambiguity in identifier, register, and document relationships —
was identified before D6 examination began, with the same boundary
language ("does not renumber, migrate, reconcile, or redesign existing
records") now carried into RQ-041's own Exclusions field.

The evidence does not establish more than this. Applying the
documented-intent / administrative-capability / demonstrated-operation /
verified-operation distinction: what exists is a capability (the two
precedence notes, and the register's uniform schema) that has been
operated (in active, current use); what is **not** evidenced is any
**verification** of that capability — no mechanism confirms the
precedence notes continue to be accurate, that the register schema is
maintained without drift, or that either has ever been invoked to
resolve an actual detected divergence. Nor does the evidence establish
whether this two-instance pattern is **required for governance
integrity** or is instead **historically accumulated** from specific
staleness episodes already recorded elsewhere in this review; that
question is recorded as genuinely uncertain, not resolved by inference
in either direction. Throughout, Single Source of Truth (which document
owns a given fact) is kept distinct from Administrative Coherence (how
identifiers, registers, and documents remain structurally consistent
once ownership is settled); the mapped evidence does not present a case
where the two concepts diverge, so this finding cannot test whether the
distinction carries operational consequences beyond the terms in which
RQ-041 itself poses it.

## 3. Evidence Gaps and Unsupported Matters

| Matter | Finding treatment |

| Register-schema-verification procedure, checklist, or assigned role | Not evidenced |
| General, scheduled, or automated cross-document reconciliation mechanism | Not evidenced; only two specific, manually-authored precedence notes exist |

These gaps are part of the answer at finding level; they are not
silently filled by inference, and the inconclusive matter is not
resolved in either direction.

## 4. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source directly contradicts another. Three qualifications
prevent overstatement:

- EV-088's own framing of "identifier, register, and document
  relationships" as an area of "residual ambiguity" at the mobilisation
  planning stage qualifies how much weight the EV-072/EV-087 pattern can
  bear: at planning, this was an unresolved area, not a demonstrated
  existing mechanism, and the mapped evidence does not show that
  assessment formally revisited;
- the consistency between EV-072 and EV-087 (both stating the same
  Master Programme precedence rule) is demonstrated operation, not
  verified operation — no check or reconciliation process is evidenced
  as producing or confirming that consistency; and
- Single Source of Truth and Administrative Coherence must not be
  collapsed into one concept; the mapped evidence shows them working
  together, not in tension, and does not test what happens when they
  diverge.

## 5. Limitations, Uncertainty, and Open Questions

- Confidence is high that an explicit precedence-note pattern exists and
  currently operates in at least two controlled documents (Review
  Identity, Document Manifest), and that the Review Question Register
  applies a uniform structural schema.
- No positive or negative confidence is assigned to whether this pattern
  is required for governance integrity or merely historically
  accumulated — this is recorded as genuinely uncertain, consistent with
  the Founder's direction not to infer either conclusion.
- No positive confidence is assigned to any register-schema-verification
  mechanism, general reconciliation process, or documented minimum
  coherence standard, because none is evidenced to exist at any level.
- No positive or negative confidence is assigned to whether the absence
  of an equivalent precedence note in the Review Question Register
  itself is deliberate, an oversight, or simply untested by divergence —
  this is recorded as genuinely uncertain.
- OQ-015 remains open and unchanged. This finding neither answers nor
  dispositions it, and does not decide any identifier-allocation policy.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and
  validation; validation is non-independent.

## 6. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The evidence does not decide whether a formal register-schema
verification or cross-document reconciliation mechanism should be
established, whether the existing precedence-note pattern should be
extended to other controlled documents, or whether the pattern already
observed is proportionate to the Framework's current minimal-viable
scale. Those remain matters for separately governed Founder judgement or
later authorised work. This finding makes no recommendation and does not
treat additional cross-linking, cataloguing, or mapping as inherently
desirable.

## 7. Scope and Confidence

**Confidence: High for the bounded observation that an explicit,
currently-operating precedence-note pattern exists in at least two
controlled documents and that the Review Question Register applies a
uniform schema; not applicable to any judgement of necessity,
completeness, or proportionality, because none is evidenced to assess;
genuinely inconclusive for whether the pattern is required for
governance integrity or historically accumulated.** The directly quoted
note text in EV-072 and EV-087, and the directly observed uniform schema
in EV-081, strongly support the bounded conclusion. The absence of any
verification, reconciliation, or design-rationale evidence is the direct
and sufficient basis for the negative findings in §3.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-041 and the four mapped frozen records.
It does not decide RQ-038 through RQ-040 (already answered in GF-037,
GF-038, and GF-039), RQ-042 or RQ-043, or OQ-015. It does not renumber,
migrate, reconcile, or redesign any existing identifier, register, or
document.

## 8. Non-Effects

This finding does not adopt or recommend a register-schema verification
mechanism, cross-document reconciliation process, or extension of the
precedence-note pattern to additional documents. It does not modify
frozen evidence or evidence treatment, decide OQ-015, renumber, migrate,
reconcile, or redesign any existing record, examine RQ-042 or RQ-043,
issue a Founder disposition or Founder Decision, close S06, or commence
D7. It remains Presented and pending Founder disposition.
