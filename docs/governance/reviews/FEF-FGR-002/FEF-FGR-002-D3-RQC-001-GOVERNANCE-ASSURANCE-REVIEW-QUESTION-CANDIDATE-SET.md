## 1. Purpose and Authority

This record prepares the smallest complete and separately examinable set of
neutral D3 Review Question candidates supported by the bounded scope in
[FEF-FGR-002-D3-MOB-001](FEF-FGR-002-D3-MOB-001-GOVERNANCE-ASSURANCE-MOBILISATION-RECORD.md).

Preparation is governed by the Founder-approved
[Charter](../FEF-FGRC-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-CHARTER.md),
[Agenda](../FEF-FGRA-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-AGENDA.md), and
[Plan](../FEF-FGRP-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-PLAN.md), the controlled
[Execution Rules](../FEF-FGRER-001-REVIEW-EXECUTION-RULES.md), and
[FEF-RQS-001](../FEF-RQS-001-REVIEW-QUESTION-SPECIFICATION.md).

The temporary references `D3-RQC-01` through `D3-RQC-09` are drafting aids
only. They are not canonical RQ identifiers, do not instantiate canonical RQ
lifecycle entries, and are not entered in the Review Question Register.

## 2. Preparation Method

The Review Analyst:

1. treated the ten mobilisation themes as coverage inputs rather than a target
   question count;
2. separated issues that could require distinct evidence or Founder
   dispositions;
3. combined scope with proportionality because both determine what assurance
   must precede reliance;
4. kept residual-risk treatment separate from validation outcomes because a
   validation verdict cannot itself accept reserved risk;
5. kept continuity separate from tool-assisted validation because the former
   concerns later reliance and revalidation while the latter concerns method
   boundaries; and
6. combined assurance traceability with closure and downstream reliance because
   the attributable chain is the control that makes those transitions
   auditable.

No candidate states a preferred answer, threshold, assurance model, final
vocabulary, or implementation design.

## 5. Proportionality and Overlap Assessment

Nine candidates are proportionate because:

- themes 1 and 2 are one indivisible assurance-coverage question;
- themes 5 and 6 remain separate because validation consequences and reserved
  risk acceptance may require different findings and Founder dispositions;
- theme 8 remains separate from theme 3 because initial independence and later
  continuity/revalidation are independently answerable;
- theme 10 remains separate from theme 7 because end-to-end assurance
  traceability is broader than exact Founder Decision input and record
  assurance; and
- no candidate exists merely to mirror a theme number.

No merge or removal is proposed. The separately labelled Validator pass may
return any candidate for revision before canonical identifier allocation or
DG-2 admission.
