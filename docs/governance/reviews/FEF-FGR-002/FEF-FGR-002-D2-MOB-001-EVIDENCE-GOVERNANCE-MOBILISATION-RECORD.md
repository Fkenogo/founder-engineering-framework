## 4. Bounded D2 Scope

### 4.1 Included

D2 may examine governance of:

- evidence identity and unique source reference;
- provenance, acquisition route, issuer, custodian, and source authority;
- evidence classes, hierarchy, weighting, and permitted use;
- source version, observed state, temporal applicability, and integrity;
- admissibility, conditional admission, rejection, and context-only treatment;
- relevance, sufficiency, completeness, and material limitations;
- contradictory, inconsistent, superseding, and corroborating evidence;
- evidence gaps, uncertainty, absence, and unavailable evidence;
- Evidence Record validation within the Charter evidence layer;
- evidence freeze, supplements, successor packs, and change control;
- controlled reuse of evidence across domains without silent authority expansion;
- traceability from evidence through RQs, sessions, findings, and decisions;
- transparency, explainability, and separation of evidence from assertion;
- protected or restricted evidence treatment within applicable access constraints;
- preservation and retention of evidence chains sufficient to support governance traceability;
- operational evidence responsibilities already assigned to the Evidence Custodian, Validator, Administrator, Analyst, and Recorder.

### 4.2 Exclusions

D2 does not:

- design the overall validation, approval, activation, exception, or risk-acceptance model governed by D3 — Governance Assurance;
- decide records architecture, archival schedules, deletion, organisational knowledge, or information lifecycle governed by D4 — Records and Information Governance;
- decide framework-wide status, supersession, withdrawal, applicability, or evolution governed by D5 — Governance Lifecycle and Evolution;
- redesign identifiers, taxonomies, roadmaps, or administrative structures governed by D6 — Framework Administration;
- extract constitutional principles or decide the Constitution/standards boundary governed by D7;
- close or finally disposition Open Questions governed through D8;
- amend FEF-RGS-000, approve FEF-P1-002, commence Engineering Discovery, or create constitutional effect.
