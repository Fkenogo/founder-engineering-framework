## 1. Purpose and Authority

This record captures the Founder's disposition of each of the six D6
temporary Review Question candidates (D6-RQC-01 through D6-RQC-06),
prepared in [FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001-FRAMEWORK-ADMINISTRATION-REVIEW-QUESTION-CANDIDATE-SET.md)
and validated Pass with Conditions in
[FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQCVR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-VALIDATION-REPORT.md), together
with five Founder Observations arising from the same Candidate Review that
are not tied to any single candidate.

The coding agent presented each candidate's exact wording, validated
treatment, and coverage notes without reinterpretation, and did not
recommend, suggest, or otherwise influence any disposition, amendment
instruction, rationale, or observation. The Founder alone determined the
disposition of every candidate and supplied every amendment instruction,
rationale, and observation reproduced below.

This record does not itself: draft final neutral Review Question wording
incorporating any Founder amendment instruction; allocate a canonical D6
Review Question identifier; admit a Review Question; qualify or register
evidence; commence examination; create a Governance Finding; create a
Founder Decision beyond this Candidate Review disposition; or perform DG-2
Admission. Where the Founder supplied a complete replacement question
(D6-RQC-01, D6-RQC-06), that exact text is reproduced as the Founder's
amendment instruction. Where the Founder supplied a refocusing instruction
rather than complete replacement wording (D6-RQC-02 through D6-RQC-05),
that instruction is reproduced exactly and is not completed, extended, or
converted into finished neutral question wording by this record — doing so
would exceed the presentation-and-recording-only facilitation capacity
disclosed above. Final neutral wording for every amended candidate is
prepared, and re-presented for Founder confirmation if required by
methodology, at the separately authorised D6 Admission Package (DG-2
preparation) gate.

FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001 itself is not edited by this record and remains
byte-identical to its validated v1.0, consistent with the
FEF-FGR-002-D3-G1 / FEF-FGR-002-D5-G1 precedent of preserving the original
candidate-preparation artefact and recording disposition only in a
separate record.

## 2. Candidate-by-Candidate Dispositions

### D6-RQC-01 — Administrative Ownership and Stewardship

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Candidate wording (FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001 §3.1) | What minimum ownership or stewardship responsibility, if any, should exist for cross-project Framework-wide administrative artefacts — including programme state, controlled registers, and controlled documents — and who should hold it? |
| Founder Disposition | **Confirm with Amendment** |
| Amendment (Founder's exact replacement wording) | What minimum cross-project administrative responsibilities, if any, should be exercised at the Framework level, and which responsibilities should remain delegated to individual Founder projects to preserve appropriate ownership and stewardship? |
| Founder Rationale (exact) | The Founder confirms that the administrative question is appropriate for D6. However, the Framework should examine the appropriate allocation of administrative responsibilities between Framework-level governance and individual Founder projects rather than implying a single framework-wide owner. The examination should preserve the philosophy that Framework-level administration exists only where genuine cross-project governance requires it. |
| Follow-up Actions | Final neutral wording (already supplied in full by the Founder) to be carried forward unchanged into the D6 Admission Package; not itself admitted or allocated a canonical identifier by this record. |

### D6-RQC-02 — Maintenance and Current-State Integrity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Candidate wording (FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001 §3.2) | What minimum maintenance and current-state reporting responsibility, if any, is needed to keep programme and governance control records current, mutually intelligible, and synchronised, and how should that synchronisation be verified going forward? |
| Founder Disposition | **Confirm with Amendment** |

## 3. Founder Observations (Collective, Exact)

The following five observations were supplied by the Founder during the
same Candidate Review as guidance arising from the review, not as
dispositions of any individual candidate. Each is reproduced exactly,
without interpretation, amendment, or supplementation.

<!-- BEGIN EXACT FOUNDER OBSERVATION 1 -->
Minimum Viable Administration has consistently emerged as the preferred administrative philosophy.

The objective of Framework Administration is to preserve governance integrity using the minimum administration necessary rather than increasing administrative controls.
<!-- END EXACT FOUNDER OBSERVATION 1 -->

<!-- BEGIN EXACT FOUNDER OBSERVATION 2 -->
Governance should preserve readiness and sequencing rather than constrain progress.

Administrative controls should exist to determine whether work is genuinely ready to proceed.

They should not become barriers to progress.
<!-- END EXACT FOUNDER OBSERVATION 2 -->

<!-- BEGIN EXACT FOUNDER OBSERVATION 3 -->
Framework Administration should distinguish clearly between:

- Single Source of Truth (ownership)
- Administrative Coherence (structural place)

These are related but distinct administrative concepts.
<!-- END EXACT FOUNDER OBSERVATION 3 -->

<!-- BEGIN EXACT FOUNDER OBSERVATION 4 -->
The Framework should remain lightweight and adaptable across Founder projects while preserving baseline trust, governance integrity, and project autonomy.

Common governance should standardise outcomes rather than unnecessarily standardising project administration.
<!-- END EXACT FOUNDER OBSERVATION 4 -->

<!-- BEGIN EXACT FOUNDER OBSERVATION 5 -->
Operational experience should inform the Framework through governed maturation rather than immediate adoption.

Potential governance improvements arising from Founder projects should mature through an administrative evaluation process before any consideration of becoming Framework practice or constitutional principle.
<!-- END EXACT FOUNDER OBSERVATION 5 -->
