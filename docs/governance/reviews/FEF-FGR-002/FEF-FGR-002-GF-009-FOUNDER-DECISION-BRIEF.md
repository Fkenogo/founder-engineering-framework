## 1. Exact Finding

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-009 — Evidence Qualification Controls Permitted Reliance**

> Evidence may support an FEF governance conclusion only when an attributable
> qualification record establishes its identity, provenance, acquisition route,
> issuer or source authority, version or temporal state, integrity, relevance,
> access state, limitations, and permitted use. Registration or pack inclusion
> does not itself establish admissibility, truth, or unrestricted reliance.

Lifecycle state: `Presented — Founder disposition pending`.

## 2. Decision Question

Should the Founder accept, conditionally accept, return, require independent
revalidation of, reject, or validly defer the evidence-qualification and
permitted-reliance controls as an enduring minimum baseline for FEF governance
reviews?

The question must distinguish the controls already operating within
FEF-FGR-002 from any enduring framework-wide minimum and from external or
protected evidence policy that is not yet established.

## 4. What Is Established

- The current review defines and operates attributable evidence identity,
  provenance, authority, version, integrity, relevance, limitation, and
  permitted-use controls.
- Registration or pack inclusion is distinct from admissibility, truth, and
  unrestricted reliance.
- Conditionally Admitted and Context Only treatments are operationally
  distinguishable and their limits can be preserved.
- Missing provenance cannot be replaced through reconstruction.

## 5. What Is Not Established

- A permanent mandatory-versus-conditional field catalogue for every FEF
  evidence type.
- Sufficiency of the current controls for every external, mutable,
  confidential, restricted, or project-controlled source.
- External legal, privacy, security, or professional requirements; EP-002 has
  no E3 evidence.
- A general document-authority hierarchy under FEF-P1-002.
- Independent validation of the current model.
- D4 access, retention, archival, deletion, or broader records-lifecycle policy.

FEF-RQS-001 and FEF-EPS-001 remain conditionally admitted preparation controls,
and FEF-FGR-001 remains Context Only.

## 6. Decision Need

The existing controls remain operative for FEF-FGR-002 under their source
authority without a new decision. Founder authority is required to accept them
as an enduring minimum baseline, impose framework-wide conditions, accept
residual risk, or determine later treatment.

A bounded disposition is possible: the Founder may accept the qualification
and permitted-reliance principle while expressly excluding a final external,
protected-source, or mandatory-field policy.
