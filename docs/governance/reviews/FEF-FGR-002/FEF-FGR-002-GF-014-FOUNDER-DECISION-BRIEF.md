## 1. Exact Finding

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-014 — Evidence Custody Is Operational and Does Not Decide Meaning**

> Evidence custody is an operational responsibility for preserving evidence
> identity, provenance, access state, integrity, versions, pack custody, and
> traceability. It does not determine evidential meaning or exercise Founder
> authority. Combined custody, analysis, recording, and validation must remain
> capacity-labelled, disclosed as non-independent, supported by deterministic
> checks and escalation, and subject to later independent revalidation where
> required.

Lifecycle state: `Presented — Founder disposition pending`.

## 2. Decision Question

Should the Founder accept the operational boundary that Evidence Custody
preserves evidence but does not decide evidential meaning or exercise Founder
authority, while retaining independence, handover, restricted evidence,
retention, and archival matters for D3 and D4?

## 4. What Is Established

- Custody preserves identity, provenance, access state, integrity, versions,
  pack custody, and traceability.
- Custody, tool access, authorship, or validation does not confer evidential
  meaning or Founder authority.
- Combined operational capacities require explicit labels, non-independence
  disclosure, deterministic checks, and escalation.
- The current review operates role boundaries, assignments, hashing, registers,
  pack controls, and traceability.

## 5. What Is Not Established

- Independent assurance in the current same-agent arrangement.
- Restricted-evidence handling.
- Role handover, successor custody, or durable organisational continuity.
- External preservation duties; EP-002 contains no E3 evidence.
- D4 retention schedules, archival architecture, deletion, legal hold, or
  broader information lifecycle.
- A permanent D3 assurance model.

OQ-010 and OQ-011 remain open.

## 6. Decision Need

The current operational boundary already functions under the role and
assignment controls. Founder authority is potentially required to accept it as
an enduring governance boundary, impose conditions, or accept residual risk.

A bounded disposition can accept the custody-versus-meaning and
custody-versus-Founder-authority boundary while retaining independence,
handover, restricted-source, retention, archival, and continuity questions for
D3 and D4.
