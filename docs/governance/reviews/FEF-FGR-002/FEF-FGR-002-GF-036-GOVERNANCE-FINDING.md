## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> How should pre-FEF-FGR-002 governance material — including exploratory
> drafts, legacy scheduled-work identifiers, and incomplete historical
> evidence records — be classified under any future lifecycle model, without
> retrospectively validating or invalidating them?

This finding uses only EV-012, EV-014, EV-078, EV-080, EV-084, and EV-085 as
frozen for RQ-037 in EP-005 v2.0. EV-014 remains Context Only; EV-080 and
EV-085 remain bounded to their historical acquisition objects. No unmapped
evidence or later substituted file was used.

## 2. Finding

The mapped evidence establishes bounded labels, practices, and gaps:

1. EV-012 records OQ-013 and OQ-016 as unresolved questions; it supplies no
   legacy-classification answer.
2. EV-014 identifies itself as `Incomplete — Missing Source Evidence`, limits
   its governance effect to preserving metadata and evidence gaps, and forbids
   reconstruction of unsupported content. It remains Context Only.
3. EV-078 records a specific six-artefact correction/recovery quarantine that
   preserves material for possible future authorised reuse while expressly
   creating no quality judgement, evidence admission, registration, or
   constitutional effect.
4. EV-080 records four scheduled-work identifiers as a legacy plan or legacy
   planning items, pending or not authorised to commence, at its governed
   RQ-037 acquisition. It states no repository-wide classification rule.
5. EV-084 labels itself `Exploratory Draft — Non-Authoritative` and requires
   future governed review and approval before constitutional use. It defines
   no universal exploratory-draft category.
6. EV-085 supplies only GF-001's already-dispositioned conclusion that no
   complete constitutional hierarchy is evidenced. GF-001 and FD-002 are not
   reopened, extended, or reweighed.

Together these sources establish several item-specific status labels and
non-effects, one correction/recovery preservation practice, and unresolved
authority and disposition questions. They do not establish a universal
legacy-material taxonomy, general classification-versus-validation rule,
authority or reliance consequence, retention/archival control, repository
inventory, or migration mechanism.

## 4. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source directly imposes a conflicting general rule. The decisive
limitations are heterogeneous scope and absent reconciliation: EV-014 is a
Context Only evidence-gap record, EV-078 is a recovery quarantine, EV-080 is a
historical programme classification of four identifiers, EV-084 is one
document's self-label, EV-085 supplies fixed GF-001 text, and EV-012 preserves
open questions. These cases cannot be combined into a complete lifecycle or
legacy-classification model.

## 5. Limitations, Uncertainty, and Open Questions

- Confidence is high that each source carries the specific status, non-effect,
  or open-question content stated above.
- Confidence is insufficient for any general taxonomy, classification test,
  authority or reliance consequence, owner, register, migration, or retention
  rule.
- EV-014 remains Context Only and cannot carry substantive authority.
- EV-078 remains a six-artefact correction/recovery example, not an ordinary
  lifecycle transition or universal preservation rule.
- EV-080 and EV-085 are acquisition-bounded; later administrative bytes do not
  replace their evidence states.
- OQ-013 and OQ-016 remain open, unchanged, and undispositioned. Their linkage
  is preserved without resolution.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.

## 6. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The evidence does not decide whether a general legacy classification should
exist; which labels, criteria, authority, ownership, register, migration, or
retention treatment it would use; what effect classification would have on
reliance; or how OQ-013 and OQ-016 should be resolved. These remain matters for
later separately governed Founder judgement. This finding proposes no taxonomy
or migration and makes no recommendation.

## 7. Scope and Confidence

**Confidence: High for the item-specific labels, non-effects, and recovery
facts; insufficient for a general classification, reliance, or migration
rule.** Exact acquisition objects support the bounded observations. Their
different purposes, the Context Only limitation, and the absence of a general
retention or authority rule prevent generalisation.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-037 and the six mapped frozen records. It
does not classify or change a legacy artefact, reopen GF-001, decide authority
or validity, prepare the Founder Review Package, close S05/D5, or commence
D6/D7.

## 8. Non-Effects

This finding does not adopt, recommend, or apply a legacy taxonomy,
classification, inventory, migration, relabelling, numbering, retention,
archival, validity, authority, effectiveness, or reliance model; validate or
invalidate a legacy instrument; reopen GF-001 or FD-002; resolve OQ-013 or
OQ-016; modify frozen evidence or evidence treatment; alter GF-031 through
GF-035; issue a Founder disposition or Founder Decision; close S05 or D5;
prepare a Founder Review Package; or commence D6 or D7. It remains Presented
and pending Founder disposition.
