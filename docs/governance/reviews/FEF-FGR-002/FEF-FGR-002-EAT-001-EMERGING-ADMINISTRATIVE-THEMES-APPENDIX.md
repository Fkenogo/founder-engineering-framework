## 1. Purpose and Boundary

This appendix exists **solely so that the five Founder Observations
recorded below remain discoverable** when D6 wording is finalised at the
D6 Admission Package gate, when D7 (Constitutional and Standards Boundary)
is undertaken, and at any eventual constitutional synthesis. It is created
at the Founder's own explicit recommendation, recorded in
FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-FDR-001 §9.

This appendix is **not**:

- a Framework principle;
- a governance standard;
- an administrative practice;
- constitutional content or a Constitutional Candidate;
- a Deferred Matter under FEF-FGR-002-DEFERRED-MATTER-REGISTER.md;
- a source of implementation authority; or
- evidence of any kind for any Review Question.

Nothing in this appendix may be cited as authority for a decision. Each
observation's only standing is as decision context — the Founder's own
words, preserved unaltered, pending whatever future disposition the
Founder Governance Review lifecycle gives them. An item remains listed
here until a separately governed activity (a D6, D7, or later Founder
decision) formally dispositions it; this appendix is administratively
updated to reflect that disposition when it occurs, but such an update is
itself informational only and creates no authority.

### EAT-1 — Minimum Viable Administration as Preferred Philosophy

> Minimum Viable Administration has consistently emerged as the preferred administrative philosophy.
>
> The objective of Framework Administration is to preserve governance integrity using the minimum administration necessary rather than increasing administrative controls.

### EAT-2 — Governance Preserves Readiness Rather Than Constraining Progress

> Governance should preserve readiness and sequencing rather than constrain progress.
>
> Administrative controls should exist to determine whether work is genuinely ready to proceed.
>
> They should not become barriers to progress.
