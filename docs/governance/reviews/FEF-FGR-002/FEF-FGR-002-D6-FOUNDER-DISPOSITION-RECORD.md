## 1. Purpose and Authority

This record captures the Founder's disposition of the D6 Founder Review
Package ([FEF-FGR-002-D6-FRP-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-FOUNDER-REVIEW-PACKAGE.md)),
including its five-item Founder Decision Agenda and one recorded Founder
Observation.

The coding agent presented the package's Executive Summary, Administrative
Philosophy, Examination Themes, Administrative Architecture Review,
Administrative Boundaries, Future Administrative Standards, and Decision
Agenda exactly as validated, without reinterpretation, and did not
recommend, suggest, or otherwise influence any disposition. The Founder
alone determined the disposition of every agenda item and the content of
the recorded observation.

This record does not itself allocate a canonical D6 Review Question
identifier, admit a Review Question, prepare a Review Question Candidate
Set, qualify or register evidence, create an Evidence Pack, commence a
session or examination, create a Governance Finding, issue a Founder
Decision under DG-5, or perform DG-2 Admission. Those remain separately
governed actions, now authorised to commence for the Review Question
Candidate Set only (Item 5), at a later, separately governed activity.

**Overall Disposition**

Approve with Minor Administrative Observation

The Founder confirms that the D6 Founder Review Package has achieved its intended purpose as a decision-support document.

The package appropriately remains within mobilisation and examination-planning boundaries, introduces no Review Questions, evidence, sessions, findings, recommendations, or implementation authority, and preserves the separation between Framework Administration, Framework Evolution, Constitutional Governance, and programme administration.

The package is therefore approved as the authorised basis for preparation of the D6 Review Question Candidate Set.

**Decision Agenda**

Item 1 — D6 Scope

Disposition: Confirm

The Founder confirms that D6 shall remain strictly limited to the examination of residual cross-project Framework Administration.

D6 shall not redesign the Framework, amend constitutional content, perform Framework Evolution, or implement governance changes.

Item 2 — Examination Themes

Disposition: Confirm

The five proposed examination themes are confirmed as an appropriate administrative basis for D6.

These themes are sufficient to guide Review Question preparation without prejudging examination outcomes.

Item 3 — Administrative Architecture

Disposition: Confirm

The Founder confirms the presented administrative architecture.

The Master Programme shall remain the single authoritative source of programme state.

Supporting programme documents shall perform only their own defined administrative purposes and shall not independently determine or duplicate current programme state.

Item 4 — Administrative Boundaries

Disposition: Confirm

The proposed examination boundaries are confirmed.

D6 shall continue to distinguish clearly between:

- Framework Administration
- Governance Methodology
- Constitutional Governance
- Framework Evolution
- Programme Administration

No implementation authority is created by examination outcomes.

Item 5 — Commencement of Review Question Candidate Preparation

Disposition: Confirm

Preparation of the D6 Review Question Candidate Set is authorised.

This authorisation permits only preparation of candidate Review Questions in accordance with the approved D6 Examination Architecture and the established Founder Governance Review methodology.

It does not admit Review Questions, commence examination, create evidence requirements, establish sessions, issue findings, or create implementation authority.

**Founder Observation**

During review it became evident that the recently adopted Single Source of Truth architecture represents a significant improvement to programme governance.

The Master Programme now provides the single authoritative answer to programme position, while the Dashboard, Review Identity, Document Manifest, and related programme documents operate as consumers of that authoritative state.

This architectural separation substantially reduces synchronisation risk and should be preserved throughout future Framework development.

This observation records an operational lesson only.

It does not create constitutional authority, amend Framework architecture, or initiate Framework Evolution.

**Follow-up**

Proceed with preparation of the D6 Review Question Candidate Set as the next separately governed activity.

All existing mobilisation conditions remain in force.

No other programme activity is authorised by this disposition.
