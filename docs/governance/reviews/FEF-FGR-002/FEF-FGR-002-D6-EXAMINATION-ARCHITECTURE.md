## 0. Purpose of This Document

This document is the engineering blueprint that will guide every later D6
Review Question, evidence requirement, and Governance Finding. It defines
**how** Domain D6 will be examined. It does not itself examine D6.

It is prepared under, and is bound by, the eight Founder conditions recorded
in [FEF-FGR-002-D6-FMAR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-FOUNDER-MOBILISATION-AUTHORISATION-RECORD.md)
§2. Section 11 of this document confirms each condition's exact incorporation.

This document does **not**:

- draft, allocate, admit, or answer a Review Question;
- identify, register, qualify, admit, or mobilise evidence;
- create or open a session;
- commence D6 examination;
- create a Governance Finding, Constitutional Candidate, Deferred Matter, or
  review-scoped Founder Decision;
- resolve an Open Question;
- perform constitutional redesign or create constitutional content;
- perform Framework Evolution;
- commence D7, D8, or Final Assembly; or
- create implementation authority.

## 1. Purpose of D6 — Framework Administration

Per [FEF-FGRC-001](../FEF-FGRC-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-CHARTER.md), D6 —
Framework Administration examines **"Identifiers, taxonomy, classifications,
roadmap relationships, simplicity, and administrative controls"** and must
**"not redesign programme architecture implicitly."**

Per [FEF-FGRA-001](../FEF-FGRA-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-AGENDA.md) §6.6,
D6 exists because "identifiers, classifications, taxonomies, registers, and
roadmap relationships make governance usable, but excessive administration
can become a substitute for judgment." D6 carries **Medium** strategic
priority — lower than D1–D5's Critical/High ratings — reflecting that it
enables consistent operation only after the fundamental controls established
by D1–D5 are already known and closed.

Under Post-D5 Founder Condition 1 and the validated
[FEF-FGR-002-D6-MPP-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-MOBILISATION-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md) §1,
D6 is further narrowed to examine only **residual cross-project Framework
Administration** — matters not already settled by D1 (authority), D4
(records and custodianship), or D5 (lifecycle and versioning) — after
removing anything D1–D5 already closed. D6's purpose in this examination
architecture is therefore:

> To determine the minimum administrative controls needed to keep the
> Founder Engineering Framework's identifiers, registers, roadmap and
> work-package relationships, and cross-project documentation usable,
> traceable, and proportionate — without redesigning programme architecture,
> without repeating D1/D4/D5, and without creating new governance authority.

## 2. Constitutional Boundaries

D6 sits below D1 (authority), D4 (records), and D5 (lifecycle) and above no
domain except itself; it is bounded above by D7 (constitutional and
standards boundary) and cross-cut by D8 (Open Question Resolution). Its
constitutional boundaries are:

1. **No implicit programme-architecture redesign** (Charter §D6 boundary).
   D6 examines existing identifier, taxonomy, register, and roadmap
   relationships; it does not design a new programme model.
2. **No constitutional content or redesign** (Founder Condition 2). D6 may
   record constitutional implications it discovers but must route them to D7
   or later constitutional consolidation, not resolve them itself.
3. **No Framework Evolution activity** (Founder Condition 3; Charter's
   Framework Evolution boundary). D6 may record framework-improvement
   observations but must not activate, disposition, or implement them.
4. **No implementation authority** (Founder Condition 4). Every D6 output
   remains a recommendation or finding pending separate governed
   implementation authority, exactly as for D1–D5.
5. **No merging of governance categories** (Founder Condition 5). Programme
   administration, governance methodology, constitutional governance, and
   Framework Evolution are examined as explicitly distinct categories
   throughout D6; see Section 5.
6. **No lifecycle bypass** (Founder Condition 6; FEF-FGRER-001 DG-1 through
   DG-7). D6 must use the same Mobilise → Prepare RQ Candidates → Founder
   Review → DG-2 Admit → Evidence Mobilisation → DG-3 Freeze → DG-4 Session
   Entry → Examine → Session Exit → Founder Review → DG-5 Decision Issuance
   → DG-6 Domain Exit sequence used by D1–D5, without omission or
   compression.
7. **No authority over D7 or D8** (Founder Condition 7). D6 outputs may be
   read by D7/D8 as inputs; D6 itself does not commence, constrain, or
   pre-decide either domain.
8. **Operational-maturity boundary** (Founder Condition 8). Because the
   Framework is now Operational, D6 examination should be proportionate and
   should not itself become an occasion for unnecessary Framework change.

## 3. Administrative Capabilities Intended to Be Examined

Consistent with [FEF-FGR-002-D6-MPP-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-MOBILISATION-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md)
§2 and [FEF-FGRA-001](../FEF-FGRA-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-AGENDA.md) §6.6,
D6 examination is architected around seven residual administrative
capability areas:

| Capability Area | What Is Examined |
|---|---|
| Ownership and stewardship | Minimum cross-project responsibility for framework-wide records whose maintenance crosses Founder projects |
| Maintenance and current-state integrity | Minimum responsibilities for keeping programme and governance control records current, mutually intelligible, and free of the kind of drift corrected in the Post-D5 prerequisite |
| Roadmap, release, and dependency administration | Cross-project roadmap, work-package, release, and dependency relationships, including the blocked FEF-P0-004 interface |
| Identifier, register, and document relationships | Residual identifier, register, classification, and document-relationship consistency not already settled by D1, D4, or D5 |
| Simplicity, reporting, and tailoring | Minimum reporting needed across projects versus what remains project-specific; avoiding excess roles, states, registers, and controls |
| Single-source-of-truth programme-control architecture | Whether the Master-Programme-authoritative / consumer-document model adopted in this task operates correctly across the Dashboard, Review Identity, and Manifest, and whether any further administrative control is needed to sustain it |
| Minimum viable administration | Whether any examined control can be simplified or removed without loss of traceability or authority |

## 4. Explicit Exclusions

D6 examination must not:

- reopen, reinterpret, or repeat D1–D5 findings, decisions, conditions, or
  non-effects;
- implement FD-026 through FD-038 or convert any accepted direction into an
  operating control;
- create a universal lifecycle, versioning, transition, exception, or legacy
  model (that boundary belongs to D5 and is already closed);
- determine constitutional placement, draft constitutional text, or create
  extraction candidates (that belongs to D7);
- resolve an Open Question, perform D8 final synthesis, or create the final
  Open Question Traceability Matrix (that belongs to D8, after D7);
- activate, evaluate, disposition, or implement Framework Evolution,
  FEF-FEV-001-FEC-001, FEF-CCF-001, FAP-001, or CE1–CE6;
- create project-specific repository, roadmap, delivery, or release rules
  binding on individual Founder projects;
- create Constitutional Candidates or Deferred Matters during architecture
  preparation (this document); or
- commence final assembly, consolidation, RGS amendment, or implementation.

## 5. Relationship With Adjacent Governance Categories

Founder Condition 5 requires these four categories to remain explicit and
non-interchangeable throughout D6. This architecture defines the boundary
each way:

| Category | Relationship to D6 | D6 Must Not |
|---|---|---|
| **Programme Administration** (Master Programme, Dashboard, Manifest, work-package register) | D6 examines the *governance rules that should apply to* programme-administration records (ownership, currency, minimum control); it may find that the Master-Programme-authoritative model adopted in this task needs a named custodian or maintenance rule | Directly rewrite Master Programme content itself as part of examination; any resulting rule is a recommendation pending separate implementation |
| **Governance Methodology** (FEF-FGRC-001/FGRA-001/FGRP-001/FGRER-001, DG-1–DG-7) | D6 examines whether administrative identifier/register conventions used *by* the methodology are consistent and minimal; it operates strictly *within* the existing methodology (Founder Condition 6) | Amend the Charter, Agenda, Plan, or Execution Rules, or alter DG-gate sequencing |
| **Constitutional Governance** (future Constitution, FEF-RGS-000 constitutional content, D7 candidates) | D6 may record that an administrative matter has constitutional implications | Decide, draft, or extract constitutional content; that is D7's reserved function (Founder Condition 2) |
| **Framework Evolution** (FEF-FEV-001 intake, CE1–CE6, FAP-001) | D6 may record that an administrative matter resembles a framework-improvement candidate | Activate, evaluate, disposition, submit on the Framework's behalf, or implement any Framework Evolution candidate (Founder Condition 3) |

Every D6 Review Question, Governance Finding, and Founder Decision must
identify which of these four categories it belongs to and must not merge
categories, consistent with Founder Condition 5.

| Closed D5 lifecycle decisions | FD-033–FD-038 and their exact conditions/non-effects | Do not design or implement accepted directions |

## 7. Expected Categories of Review Questions

D6 Review Questions, when later drafted and admitted, are expected to fall
into exactly the five provisional question themes already disclosed in
[FEF-FGR-002-D6-MPP-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-MOBILISATION-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md) §5,
restated here as architectural RQ categories (not RQ wording):

1. **Ownership RQs** — minimum cross-project framework-record ownership responsibilities;
2. **Maintenance/currency RQs** — minimum maintenance and current-state reporting responsibilities, including whether the Master-Programme-authoritative model requires a standing reconciliation rule;
3. **Roadmap/dependency RQs** — roadmap, release, work-package, and dependency administration boundaries;
4. **Identifier/register RQs** — residual identifier, register, classification, and document-relationship consistency; and
5. **Proportionality RQs** — proportionate tailoring between the common FEF foundation and individual project authority, including minimum viable administration.

No RQ identifier, wording, evidence need, lifecycle state, or admission
effect is created by this categorisation. Formal RQ drafting remains a
separate, later, Founder-gated activity following this blueprint.

## 8. Expected Categories of Governance Findings

Per [FEF-FGRA-001](../FEF-FGRA-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-AGENDA.md) §6.6
"Expected output classes," adapted to D6's narrowed residual scope, D6
Governance Findings are expected to fall into these categories:

| Finding Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Administrative consistency findings | Whether identifiers, registers, or classifications are applied consistently across the repository |
| Duplication/ambiguity/complexity findings | Whether any administrative control (including programme-status duplication of the kind corrected in Section 12 below) is redundant, ambiguous, or disproportionate |
| Ownership/stewardship gap findings | Whether a genuine cross-project administrative responsibility remains undefined after D1/D4/D5 |
| Category-boundary findings | Whether an administrative matter has constitutional, methodological, or Framework Evolution implications requiring routing rather than resolution |
| Proportionality findings | Whether a control is minimum-viable or should be simplified or removed |

Every Governance Finding, when produced, must state its finding category and
must not itself resolve a constitutional, methodological, or Framework
Evolution matter (Section 5).

## 9. Interfaces With D7 — Constitutional and Standards Boundary

Per [FEF-FGRA-001](../FEF-FGRA-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-AGENDA.md) §6.6
"Downstream dependency created": **"D7 needs clear administrative and
domain boundaries to distinguish constitutional principles from operating
mechanisms."** D6's interface with D7 is therefore:

- D6 produces validated administrative-boundary outputs that D7 may later use
  to distinguish enduring constitutional principles from domain-specific
  administrative mechanism;
- any constitutional implication D6 discovers is **recorded only**, per
  Founder Condition 2, and routed to D7 as a candidate input — D6 does not
  draft constitutional text, determine placement, or create extraction
  candidates itself;
- D7 remains separate and Uncommenced; this architecture creates no D7
  authority, schedule, or precondition beyond "D6 validated outputs exist
  when D7 later commences," consistent with [FEF-FGRP-001](../FEF-FGRP-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-PLAN.md)
  §6 (D7 depends on "Validated D1–D6 outputs").

## 10. Interfaces With D8 — Open Question Resolution

Per [FEF-FGRA-001](../FEF-FGRA-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-AGENDA.md) §6.8,
D8 performs continuous cross-cutting mapping throughout D1–D7 with final
synthesis after D7. D6's interface with D8 is therefore:

- D6 may examine only the **residual administrative traceability and
  maintenance-responsibility aspects** of OQ-014, OQ-016, and OQ-021, exactly
  as bounded in [FEF-FGR-002-D6-MPP-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-MOBILISATION-PLANNING-PACKAGE.md)
  §8; it does not resolve any Open Question;
- FEF-P0-004 remains Blocked under OQ-016 throughout D6; D6 may examine only
  the roadmap/dependency-administration consequences of that blocked state,
  not its disposition;
- all 23 baseline Open Questions remain Open and are not silently closed by
  any D6 output;
- the final Open Question Traceability Matrix does not yet exist and is not
  a D6 output; it remains a D8-or-later output, after D7.

## 12. Confirmation — No Implementation Authority Created

This document, and any future D6 recommendation, Governance Finding, or
Founder Decision prepared under this architecture:

- does not implement, activate, or make effective any administrative
  control, register rule, identifier convention, or programme-architecture
  change;
- does not bind the Master Programme, Dashboard, Review Identity, or
  Manifest beyond what those documents' own governing authority (Section 5's
  "Programme Administration" row) separately authorises;
- remains, in every case, a **recommendation or finding pending separate,
  attributable, Founder-gated implementation authority**, exactly as for
  every D1–D5 output; and
- does not, by its own preparation, alter the current programme position,
  which remains authoritative only in the Master Programme (FEF-MP-001) per
  the single-source-of-truth Authority Model recorded there.
