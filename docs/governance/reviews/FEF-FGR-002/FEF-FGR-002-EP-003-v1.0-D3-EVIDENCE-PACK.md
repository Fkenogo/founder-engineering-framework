## 3. Scope Statement

**Purpose.** This pack assembles the evidence required for future
examination of RQ-016 through RQ-024 (D3 — Governance Assurance). It does
not perform that examination.

**Included evidence subjects:** controlling review instruments and their
evidence-admissibility rules; operational role, authority, and independence
controls; the operated D1 and D2 validation/session/closure record set (as
worked examples of assurance practice); the D3 quarantine-and-recovery
incident; the D3 admission-readiness-checkpoint correction; the D3 Founder
Review Package and Disposition Record; the D3 DG-2 admission record; the D3
Governance Evolution Review and FAP-001 (context only); and Founder
Decisions FD-010 through FD-016 as direct attributable evidence.

**Exclusions.** The six quarantined D3 artefacts (`FEF-FGR-002-D3-RQVA-001`,
`-ERM-001`, `-CEIQR-001`, `-ERRM-001`, `-EMVR-001`, `-RQS-001`) are excluded
as substantive evidence; only the fact of their quarantine (via EV-058) is
in scope. CE1–CE6 recommendations are excluded as governance authority —
EV-063/064 are Context Only. No project-specific, external, or
non-repository material is included. No source not individually qualified
in FEF-FGR-002-D3-EQR-002 is included.

**Known gaps.**

1. No operated dissent, challenge, rebuttal, or contrary-Founder-view
   record exists in the reviewed D1/D2 history (affects RQ-018). This gap
   is preserved as an evidence limitation, not converted into proof of
   either the presence or the suppression of dissent, and does not exclude
   RQ-018 from future examination.
2. EV-058 and EV-059 each contain a preserved, unresolved contradiction
   (Section 7).

**Intended use.** This pack, once frozen, is the sole permitted evidence
baseline for a future D3 session, in the same manner FEF-FGR-002-EP-001 and
FEF-FGR-002-EP-002 were the sole baselines for S01 and S02. It may not be
used before a session-entry gate (DG-4) validates its currency, and no
session currently exists.

## 7. Conflict and Limitation Log

### 7.1 EV-058 — Quarantine manifest contradiction

EV-058 documents that six artefacts (`FEF-FGR-002-D3-RQVA-001`, `-ERM-001`,
`-CEIQR-001`, `-ERRM-001`, `-EMVR-001`, `-RQS-001`) asserted DG-2 admission
and Evidence Record registration that directly contradicts the active
repository state (which shows those identifiers voided and unused). Both
the claim and its contradiction are preserved as part of the evidentiary
record. **This contradiction is not resolved by pack inclusion or freeze.**
Pack inclusion does not validate the incorrect claims made by the
quarantined material; it preserves them as a documented, examinable
failure case.

### 7.2 EV-059 — Admission-readiness checkpoint contradiction

EV-059 (`FEF-FGR-002-D3-C1-001`) contains, within one document, its own
v1.0 claim ("D3 — Governance Assurance is formally closed") and its v1.1
correction (D3 active, not closed). Both remain visible in the source
document. **This contradiction is not resolved by pack inclusion or
freeze.** The pack records the administrative fact that a correction was
made and the reasons given for it (Domain Exit Gate criteria unmet); it
does not adjudicate what the contradiction means for D3's substantive
assurance conclusions — that is reserved for future examination.

### 7.3 RQ-018 — Absence of dissent evidence

No GF, FD, or session record across D1 or D2 documents a contrary Founder
view, a returned finding, or an escalated disagreement. Per FEF-FGRC-001
§9.3, this absence is recorded as a limitation and is **not** treated as
proof that dissent was permitted, prevented, or irrelevant. EV-058 is the
only item mapped to RQ-018, and it evidences a detection-and-recovery
episode, not a dissent episode; the underlying gap therefore persists
inside this pack.

### 7.4 FD evidence vs. surrounding process

EV-065 through EV-071 (FD-010–016) evidence what the Founder decided. They
are not evidence, and must not be read as evidence, that the validation
process around each decision was adequate — that is separately evidenced
by EV-050–EV-057 and remains unexamined.

### 7.5 Non-independent validation

Every validation record in this pack (D1, D2, and D3 alike) discloses a
non-independent combination. This pack's own validation (Section 9) is
likewise non-independent. This is a carried-forward limitation, not a
defect introduced by this pack.
