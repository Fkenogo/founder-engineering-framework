## 1. Purpose

This assessment confirms whether D5, as already defined by the Charter,
Agenda, and Plan, remains within reusable cross-project engineering
governance and does not silently expand into adjacent domains or
Framework Evolution territory. It is an assessment of the domain's
*definition*, not a substantive review of any D5 content, because no D5
content yet exists.

## 2. Assessment

| Test | Assessment | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Remains within reusable cross-project engineering governance | **Yes** | D5's defined subject — status, versioning, applicability, supersession, withdrawal, controlled evolution, transitional treatment of governance instruments — is exactly the kind of common engineering-governance concern the FEF exists to standardise; it does not touch any individual project's product, repository, or delivery architecture |
| Avoids project-specific architecture or product governance | **Yes** | Nothing in the Charter/Agenda/Plan definition of D5 references any Founder project's product architecture, repository layout, or delivery governance; FRAS (repository architecture) is explicitly excluded (§4.2 of the Mobilisation Record) |
| Does not duplicate D1–D4 | **Yes, with a noted adjacency** | D1 (authority), D2 (evidence), D3 (assurance), and D4 (records) each examine a distinct governance concern; D5 examines *change over time* to governance instruments, which is a materially different question from any of the four. The adjacency is that D5 will necessarily reference D1–D4 outcomes as its primary inputs (per the Plan) — this is a dependency, not a duplication, and the Mobilisation Record's exclusions (§4.2) explicitly bar D5 from reopening or reinterpreting D1–D4 |
| Does not absorb Framework Evolution work | **Yes** | FEF-FEV-001 (the intake mechanism), FEF-FEV-001-FEC-001 (the registered candidate), and FEF-CCF-001 are all explicitly excluded from D5's substantive scope in the Mobilisation Record §4.2; D5 examines the framework's *own* lifecycle governance, not the intake or evaluation of specific improvement candidates |
| Does not prematurely resolve CE1–CE6 | **Yes** | FEF-GER-D3-001's six candidate enhancements remain explicitly deferred "to Domain D5 or later constitutional consolidation" per the Master Programme; this mobilisation-planning task does not examine, evaluate, or disposition any of CE1–CE6 — it only notes that D5, once substantively active, is one of the two places those candidates were told they would eventually be considered. Whether and how D5 later engages with CE1–CE6 is future D5 substantive-review work, not decided here |
| Does not implement FRAS | **Yes** | FRAS (Founder Repository Architecture Standard) is explicitly excluded in the Mobilisation Record §4.2; it is unrelated to D5's lifecycle-and-evolution subject matter |
| Does not perform constitutional consolidation | **Yes** | D5's Charter-defined boundary is "does not activate a lifecycle model without decision"; constitutional consolidation is reserved to D7 (Constitutional and Standards Boundary) per the Charter's domain table, and this Mobilisation Record excludes any D7 activity |

## 3. Noted Ambiguity — Recorded, Not Resolved

The Agenda (§6.5) lists "Constitutional Candidates concerning controlled
evolution" as one of D5's expected output classes, while the Charter (§6)
separately assigns constitutional-candidate extraction to D7. This is an
**existing overlap in the controlling instruments themselves**, not
something this task introduces or resolves. It means D5 may, in its future
substantive review, identify a candidate that is *about* controlled
evolution and yet require D7-style constitutional-extraction treatment.
This assessment records the ambiguity explicitly, per the task's
instruction to record uncertainty rather than resolve it by inference. No
disposition is made here; a future D5 Review Question examination would
need to navigate this boundary carefully, and DG-6 domain exit for D5 would
need to confirm no constitutional content was created without D7 involvement.

## 4. Conclusion

D5, as defined by the existing controlling instruments, remains within
reusable cross-project engineering governance, is properly bounded away
from project-specific architecture, Framework Evolution, FRAS, and
constitutional consolidation, and does not duplicate D1–D4 substantively
(only depends on their outcomes as inputs, which is expected and approved
sequencing). One pre-existing ambiguity between the Agenda and Charter
regarding constitutional-candidate output is recorded for future D5/D7
attention and is not resolved by this assessment.

## 5. Non-Effects

This assessment does not amend the Charter, Agenda, or Plan; does not
resolve the Agenda/Charter constitutional-candidate ambiguity; does not
commence D5 substantive review; and does not disposition CE1–CE6, FRAS,
FEF-FEV-001-FEC-001, or FEF-CCF-001.
