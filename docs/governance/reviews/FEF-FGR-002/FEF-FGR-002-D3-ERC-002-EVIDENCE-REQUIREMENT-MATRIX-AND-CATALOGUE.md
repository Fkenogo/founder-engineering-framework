## 1. Purpose

This record derives the minimum evidence needed for a defensible future
examination of the nine admitted D3 Review Questions, and catalogues
candidate sources located by repository and Git-history search. It does not
qualify or admit evidence (that is FEF-FGR-002-D3-EQR-002), does not answer
a Review Question, and does not perform Governance Assurance.

registered as EV-065 through EV-071 (see FEF-FGR-002-D3-EQR-002 for
