## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What minimum cross-project administrative responsibilities, if any,
> should be exercised at the Framework level, and which responsibilities
> should remain delegated to individual Founder projects to preserve
> appropriate ownership and stewardship?

This finding uses only EV-016, EV-017, EV-018, EV-019, EV-021, EV-072,
EV-080, and EV-086 as frozen for RQ-038 in EP-006 v1.0/MAN-001. EV-072
remains Conditionally Admitted. EV-072, EV-080, and EV-086 are used only
at their frozen D6 acquisition state. No unmapped evidence or later live
administrative version was used.

## 2. Finding

The mapped evidence establishes that every currently defined role,
authority, and responsibility instrument in this repository — Operational
Governance Roles (EV-016), Operational Authority Boundary (EV-017),
Independence and Conflict Rules (EV-018), Governance Responsibility
Matrix (EV-019), and the Role Assignment Register (EV-021) — is explicitly
and exclusively scoped to FEF-FGR-002, this Founder Governance Review.
None defines, mentions, or bounds a Framework-level or cross-project
administrative role of any kind.

Separately, the evidence establishes one operated example of
single-document administrative ownership: the Master Programme (EV-080)
designates itself the sole authoritative source of programme state and
names the Founder Dashboard (EV-086), Review Identity, and Document
Manifest (EV-072) as consumers, not independent controllers, of that
state — a pattern EV-086 independently confirms of itself ("does not
independently maintain, assert, or duplicate programme state"). This
pattern is operated entirely among four documents belonging to this one
Founder project's own governance review.

Both EV-072 and EV-080 record, in their own current text, that the
Framework itself is not yet adopted by any other project: EV-072's header
states "Framework status: Draft — Not Yet Adoptable," and EV-080 records
"Framework release: None." The only reference in the mapped evidence to
multiple "Founder projects" (plural) describes them as a future source of
engineering lessons submitted into the Framework Evolution intake
mechanism — not as adopters holding, or bound by, any Framework-level
administrative responsibility.

The mapped evidence therefore supports a bounded conclusion: within this
one review, administrative ownership is achieved by scoping every role
instrument to the review itself and by operating a single-document
ownership pattern among its own control documents. It does not establish
that any minimum administrative responsibility currently is, or has been
proposed to be, exercised at a Framework level spanning multiple Founder
projects, nor any boundary for what should instead remain delegated to
individual projects — because no second adopting project yet exists
against which either could be evidenced.

These gaps are part of the answer at finding level; they are not silently

## 4. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source directly contradicts another. Two qualifications prevent
overstatement:

- EV-072 is Conditionally Admitted as a mutable, non-authoritative index;
  its "Not Yet Adoptable" self-description is treated here only as the
  Manifest's own current statement, not as an independently controlling
  Framework-wide determination; and
- EV-080's §1.1 characterises the Single Source of Truth model as adopted
  "as a permanent programme-governance architecture principle." This
  finding notes that characterisation as a qualifying fact about the
  source text only; it neither tests nor relies on whether that
  permanence characterisation, or the pattern's generalisation to
  Framework-wide administration, is itself correct — that question
  belongs to RQ-043 and is not decided here.

## 5. Limitations, Uncertainty, and Open Questions

- Confidence is high that every currently defined role and authority
  instrument is scoped to FEF-FGR-002 only, and that the Framework's own
  current-state records show no other adopting project.
- Confidence is high that the Single Source of Truth pattern is
  accurately described as operated among four specific documents within
  this one review.
- No positive confidence is assigned to any Framework-level cross-project
  administrative model, minimum responsibility, or delegation boundary,
  because the mapped evidence does not establish one and none yet exists
  in operated practice.
- OQ-021 remains open and unchanged (partial interface only — reporting
  and maintenance responsibility, not governance-chain approval
  authority). This finding neither answers nor dispositions it.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.

## 6. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The evidence does not decide whether, when, or how a Framework-level
administrative responsibility should be established once other Founder
projects adopt the Framework; what minimum responsibilities such an
authority should hold; which responsibilities should instead remain with
individual projects; or whether the Single Source of Truth pattern
demonstrated within FEF-FGR-002 should inform any future Framework-level
design. Those remain matters for separately governed Founder judgement or
later authorised work. This finding makes no recommendation.

## 7. Scope and Confidence

**Confidence: High for the bounded observation that no Framework-level
cross-project administrative instrument currently exists; not applicable
to any cross-project model, because none is evidenced to assess.** The
directly attributable scope statements in every role instrument, and the
Framework's own "Not Yet Adoptable" / "Framework release: None" status
records, strongly support the bounded conclusion. The absence of any
second adopting project is the direct and sufficient reason no
cross-project responsibility or delegation boundary can yet be evidenced.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-038 and the eight mapped frozen records.
It does not decide RQ-039 through RQ-043 or any D7 interface.

## 8. Non-Effects

This finding does not adopt or recommend a cross-project administrative
model, ownership assignment, delegation boundary, or role design. It does
not modify frozen evidence or evidence treatment, resolve OQ-021, examine
RQ-039 through RQ-043, issue a Founder disposition or Founder Decision,
close S06, or commence D7. It remains Presented and pending Founder
disposition.
