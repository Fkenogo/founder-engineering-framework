## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What information classification, access authority, confidentiality, privacy, and security governance, if any, are required for FEF records so that availability and restriction remain attributable, proportionate, reviewable, and consistent with governance transparency without selecting technical controls?

This examination used only the nine Evidence Records mapped to RQ-028 in
EP-004 v1.0: EV-005, EV-007, EV-008, EV-012, EV-013, EV-016, EV-023,
EV-066, and EV-071. No unmapped evidence was introduced.

The evidence qualifications remain controlling:

- EV-005, EV-007, and EV-008 are E2 Admitted within the approved review
  Charter, Plan, and execution-rule boundaries;
- EV-012 is E2 Admitted as an Open Questions Register entry that records
  OQ-010 but supplies no answer;
- EV-013 is E2 Conditionally Admitted and remains Draft v0.2, Founder
  Review Required, Not Approved, with research-specific proposals only;
- EV-016 is E2 Admitted as a review-operational role standard only;
- EV-023 is E2 Conditionally Admitted as an Evidence Pack preparation
  baseline with a retained draft header, not as a generally approved
  Framework standard;
- EV-066 is E1 Admitted for its exact evidence-qualification and
  permitted-reliance decision, while external, protected, restricted,
  privacy, security, legal, professional, and project-specific source
  policy remains outside its scope; and
- EV-071 is E1 Admitted for its exact evidence-custody boundary; it
  preserves access state but supplies no general restricted-evidence policy.

## 2. Evidence-Supported Observations

1. EV-005 requires review evidence to record accessibility, access
   limitations, confidentiality or access classification, and permitted use
   consistent with confidentiality, legal, security, and repository
   boundaries. An unavailable source cannot be used without recording the
   access limitation. These are review-evidence controls, not a general
   information-classification scheme.
2. EV-007 places access within D4 and requires access limitations,
   unavailable evidence, dependencies, risks, and conflicts to remain
   visible. Potential external legal, security, privacy, professional, or
   technical inputs are evidence dependencies; their absence must not be
   silently resolved through assumption.
3. EV-008 provides a stop boundary when an unresolved confidentiality,
   legal, security, or privacy prohibition exists. It supports explicit
   exception, escalation, and stop treatment within review execution, not a
   substantive security, privacy, or access policy.
4. EV-012 records OQ-010: “What confidentiality, privacy, security, and
   access classifications apply to FEF research evidence?” Its status
   remains an open policy dependency that blocks sensitive research
   execution. The Open Question supplies no classification or authority.
5. EV-016 assigns the Evidence Custodian review-scoped responsibility for
   preserving access treatment and handling confidentiality and access
   within Evidence Packs. The role may control evidence custody and block
   unsafe material pending disposition, but cannot determine evidential
   meaning, governance outcomes, access entitlement, or Founder authority.
6. EV-023 defines bounded Evidence Pack controls: record access
   classification; separate restricted source copies from unrestricted
   metadata where needed; minimise unnecessary sensitive reproduction;
   preserve lawful access constraints; record access or validation of
   restricted material where required; identify redaction and its effect on
   reliance; and keep restriction visible. These are Evidence Pack
   preparation-baseline controls only.
7. EV-066 supplies an attributable enduring minimum for FEF governance
   reviews: evidence may support a conclusion only where its qualification
   records access state, limitations, and permitted use alongside identity,
   provenance, authority, integrity, and relevance. Registration or pack
   inclusion does not create unrestricted reliance. The decision expressly
   excludes external, protected, restricted, project-specific, legal,
   privacy, security, and professional policy.
8. EV-071 supplies an attributable custody boundary: evidence custody
   preserves access state, while custody, repository access, tool access,
   authorship, recording, or validation does not confer evidential,
   approval, or Founder authority. Restricted-evidence policy remains
   outside the decision, and no restricted evidence is evidenced as
   operated in the mapped corpus.
9. EV-013 proposes research-specific principles that transparency does not
   override security, privacy, confidentiality, ownership, or access
   restrictions; calls for recorded access authority, constraints,
   sensitivity, confidentiality, transformations, redaction, and access
   events; and preserves open classification-specific controls. Because
   EV-013 is Not Approved, these are proposed context only.

## 4. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The mapped evidence does not itself decide:

- whether FEF-wide information classifications, levels, criteria, markings,
  ownership, or review cycles should be established;
- who may request, approve, deny, review, revoke, expire, except, or
  escalate access to particular record classes;
- what confidentiality, privacy, disclosure, redaction, transparency, and
  security governance should apply;
- what access, disclosure, exception, assessment, audit, or incident records
  should be required;
- how applicable external legal, regulatory, contractual, privacy, security,
  or professional obligations should be identified and reconciled; or
- which later D6 administrative structures should implement any
  Founder-approved D4 treatment.

These are matters for later Founder judgement. This finding selects no
answer and makes no recommendation.

## 5. Conclusion

The mapped evidence establishes bounded access governance for
FEF-FGR-002's evidence: access state, restrictions, limitations, permitted
use, unavailable-source treatment, redaction effects, and reliance
boundaries must remain attributable and visible. EV-066 provides
attributable Founder authority for access state, limitations, and permitted
use as evidence-qualification requirements, while EV-071 confirms that
repository or custody access does not confer evidential, approval, or
Founder authority.

The same corpus does not establish a FEF-wide information-classification
scheme, access-authority lifecycle, confidentiality or privacy policy,
disclosure or transparency model, security governance, legal-compliance
framework, or access-control implementation. OQ-010 remains open; EV-013
and EV-023 remain authority-limited; EV-066 and EV-071 expressly exclude
the broader policy; and no operated restricted-evidence case is mapped.
Broader treatment therefore requires Founder judgement and cannot be
inferred from review-evidence controls or repository access.

## 6. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source substantively contradicts the conclusion. EV-023 contains
restriction, redaction, sensitive-content minimisation, access-recording,
and visibility controls, but its Evidence Pack scope and retained draft
header prevent treating those controls as a generally approved model.
EV-013 proposes broader transparency, access, confidentiality, privacy, and
security treatment, but its Not Approved status and open control questions
are controlling.

EV-066 accepts access state, limitations, and permitted use as enduring
evidence-qualification fields while expressly excluding the broader source
and policy domains. EV-071 accepts access-state preservation while
expressly leaving restricted-evidence policy outside D2. Those
qualifications narrow rather than displace the conclusion.

No absence outside EP-004 or outside RQ-028's nine-item mapping is claimed.

## 7. Limitations and Uncertainty

- The finding is limited to the nine mapped EP-004 v1.0 Evidence Records.
- Most approved controls govern this review or its evidence, not all FEF
  records.
- Repository accessibility does not prove entitlement, authorised
  disclosure, confidentiality treatment, privacy compliance, or security
  control effectiveness.
- EV-012 records OQ-010 as open and cannot answer it.
- EV-013 and EV-023 remain Conditionally Admitted within their exact limits.
- EV-066 and EV-071 do not supply external or restricted-evidence policy.
- No technical control, security architecture, privacy implementation,
  access-control system, or legal determination is selected.
- No D6 administrative structure is inferred.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.
