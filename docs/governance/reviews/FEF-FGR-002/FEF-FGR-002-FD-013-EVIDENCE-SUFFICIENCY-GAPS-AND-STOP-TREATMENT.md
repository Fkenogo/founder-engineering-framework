## 1. Decision Question

What attributable disposition and enduring treatment applies to
FEF-FGR-002-GF-011?

## 2. Exact Validated Finding

> Evidence sufficiency is specific to the Review Question and proposition. Before
> an examination path proceeds, the record must disclose coverage, contrary or
> superseding evidence, material limitations, uncertainty, unavailable evidence,
> and gaps, and must state whether the path may proceed, proceed conditionally,
> or must stop. Missing evidence may never be replaced by assumption.

## 3. Exact Founder Disposition and Decision

**Disposition: Accept with Conditions**

Accept an enduring qualitative requirement that every substantive Review
Question examination record:

- evidence coverage;
- contrary or superseding evidence;
- material limitations;
- uncertainty;
- unavailable evidence;
- material gaps;
- one explicit treatment:
  - proceed;
  - proceed conditionally;
  - stop.

Missing evidence shall not be replaced by assumption, reconstruction,
preference, or unsupported inference.

This disposition does not create:

- universal numerical sufficiency thresholds;
- quantitative corroboration thresholds;
- external legal or compliance completeness requirements;
- a general D3 residual-risk model.

Those subjects remain for later governed treatment.

## 4. Decision Treatment

The qualitative recording and proceed/conditional-proceed/stop requirements are
accepted. Quantitative thresholds, external completeness rules, and residual
risk treatment remain outside this decision.

## 5. Rationale, Conditions, and Limitations

No separate Founder rationale was supplied. The disposition itself supplies
