## 1. Finding Reference

**FEF-FGR-002-GF-041 — Simplicity and Proportionality Were Named as Review-Design Intent, and One Document Demonstrates Minimal Reporting in Actual Operation, but No Cross-Project Minimum-Administration Standard Is Evidenced**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

**Disposition**

Accept

**Founder Reflection**

Administrative simplicity should remain an outcome of sound governance
design rather than an objective pursued in isolation.

The Framework should encourage proportional governance, applying only the
level of administration necessary to preserve integrity, traceability, and
trust while allowing individual Founder projects to adopt additional
controls where their operational context requires them.

No Conditions, additional Discussion, or Follow-up Actions were supplied
by the Founder beyond the Disposition and Founder Reflection recorded
above. The Founder Reflection is explanatory only; it creates no
governance authority, Framework policy, or implementation instruction.

## 3. Exact Finding Scope

**Confidence: High for the bounded observation that proportionality and
simplicity were named as review-design intent and that one document
demonstrates minimal reporting in actual current operation; not
applicable to any cross-project minimum-administration standard, because
none is evidenced to assess and no second adopting project exists;
genuinely inconclusive for whether the Simplicity principle is operative
or aspirational, and whether the demonstrated pattern is a cross-project
template.** The directly quoted text in EV-006 and EV-086 strongly
supports the bounded conclusion. The absence of any adopted standard or
second adopting project is the direct and sufficient basis for the
negative findings in §3.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-042 and the two mapped frozen records.
It does not decide RQ-038 through RQ-041 (already answered in GF-037,
GF-038, GF-039, and GF-040), or RQ-043.

## 4. Exact Finding Non-Effects
