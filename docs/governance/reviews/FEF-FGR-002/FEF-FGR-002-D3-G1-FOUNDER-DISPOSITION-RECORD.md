## 1. Purpose and Authority

This record captures the Founder's disposition of each of the nine D3
temporary Review Question candidates (D3-RQC-01 through D3-RQC-09),
presented one at a time from FEF-FGR-002-D3-G1-FOUNDER-REVIEW-PACKAGE
during the D3-G1 Founder Review session held on 2026-07-25.

The coding agent presented each candidate's exact wording, constitutional
intent, validation summary, and coverage notes without reinterpretation,
and did not recommend, suggest, or otherwise influence any disposition. The
Founder alone determined the disposition of every candidate.

This record does not itself allocate a canonical D3 Review Question
identifier, admit a Review Question, update the Review Question Register or
any other governance register, create an Evidence Record, or perform DG-2
Admission. Those remain separately governed actions at the next gate.
