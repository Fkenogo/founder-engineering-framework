## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What governance-chain transitions — amendment, supersession, or
> withdrawal — require explicit Founder or delegated approval, who owns
> the resulting transition record, and how is a superseded or withdrawn
> instrument preserved rather than deleted?

This finding uses only EV-005, EV-017, EV-074, EV-078, EV-079, and EV-082 as
frozen for RQ-035 in EP-005 v2.0. All six remain bounded to their exact
qualification and permitted uses. No unmapped evidence or later substituted
file was used.

## 2. Finding

The mapped evidence establishes bounded controls and practices:

1. EV-017 places Founder Decision Authority with the Founder only and states
   that it cannot be exercised by implication, operational assignment, or role
   combination. It does not say that every amendment, supersession, or
   withdrawal is necessarily an FD.
2. EV-005 requires issued identifiers to remain unique and immutable, not be
   renumbered, remain reserved if withdrawn, and carry a status rather than
   being deleted. This is an identifier/record-namespace rule, not a universal
   substantive-content preservation rule.
3. EV-078 and EV-079 demonstrate traceable correction/recovery. Unsupported
   D3 artefacts were quarantined and preserved outside active reliance; a
   defective closure claim was expressly corrected and superseded for active
   reliance while history remained discoverable.
4. EV-074 and EV-082 distinguish candidate registration from approval or
   effectiveness. The examples create no present framework effect and reserve
   later activation to separately governed Founder authority.

These controls support a bounded practice of explicit authority, traceable
active-reliance changes, and historical preservation in correction/recovery
cases. They do not establish a general governance-chain model mapping each
ordinary amendment, supersession, or withdrawal to an approval capacity; any
delegated authority; ordinary transition mechanics; transition-record
ownership; or a universal preserve-not-delete rule for substantive
instruments.

## 4. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source directly imposes a transition rule that contradicts another.
The decisive limitations are scope and absence. EV-017 controls Founder
Decision Authority but does not classify every transition; EV-005 controls
identifiers and record namespace; EV-078 and EV-079 concern correction or
recovery rather than ordinary change; and EV-074/EV-082 concern pre-decision
candidates. The D3 recovery records' own broader lessons remain bounded
analysis, not approved lifecycle governance. These qualifications prevent the
examples from being assembled into a complete model.

## 5. Limitations, Uncertainty, and OQ-021

- Confidence is high that the mapped acquisition objects accurately establish
  the bounded authority, identifier, recovery, correction, and candidate-state
  examples.
- Confidence is moderate that those examples share a traceability and
  preservation tendency; no mapped source approves that tendency as a general
  rule.
- No positive confidence is assigned to a transition-by-transition approval
  map, delegated authority, ordinary mechanics, record ownership, or universal
  preservation scope.
- OQ-021 remains open, unchanged, and undispositioned. Its linkage is
  clarified by this finding, but the question is not resolved.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.

## 6. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The evidence does not decide which ordinary transitions require Founder or
validly delegated approval; whether any delegation should exist; what
amendment, supersession, or withdrawal mechanics should apply; who should own
their records; or what substantive material should be preserved. These remain
matters for later separately governed Founder judgement. This finding proposes
no model and makes no recommendation.

## 7. Scope and Confidence

**Confidence: High for the bounded operated controls and examples;
insufficient for a general transition model.** The exact historical records
support the stated Founder-authority, identifier, quarantine, correction, and
candidate-state observations. Their scope limits, the absence of an ordinary
amendment example, and open OQ-021 prevent generalisation.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-035 and the six mapped frozen records. It does
not decide RQ-036 or RQ-037, D6 administration, D7 constitutional effect, D4
retention policy, or Framework Evolution.

## 8. Non-Effects

This finding does not adopt, recommend, or apply an amendment, supersession,
withdrawal, delegated-authority, record-ownership, or preservation model;
reinterpret or reopen the D3 quarantine; convert correction into ordinary
amendment; convert Evidence Record retirement into instrument withdrawal;
convert candidate registration into approval or effectiveness; assign a role
or owner; modify or delete an instrument; resolve OQ-021; modify frozen
evidence or evidence treatment; alter GF-031 through GF-033; issue a Founder
disposition or Founder Decision; examine RQ-036 or RQ-037; close S05 or D5; or
commence D6 or D7. It remains Presented and pending Founder disposition.
