## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What governance process should determine how operational lessons
> arising from Founder projects mature into Framework administrative
> practices, and does the recently adopted Single Source of Truth
> architecture satisfy those criteria?

This finding uses only EV-080 (restricted to §1.1 Programme Authority
Model), EV-086 (restricted to its "Role of This Document" framing),
EV-087 (restricted to its Scope and Authority Note), and EV-089
(restricted to the D6-RQC-06 disposition entry and Founder Observation
5 with its exact constraint), as frozen for RQ-043 in EP-006
v1.0/MAN-001. No unmapped evidence or later live administrative version
was used. This finding does not itself adopt, generalise, or implement
Single Source of Truth beyond its current, already Founder-confirmed
application, and does not convert Founder Observation 5, or any other
Founder Observation, into Framework policy.

## 2. Finding

The mapped evidence establishes that the Founder has twice stated,
consistently, that operational lessons should mature through an
evidence-based process before adoption: in the exact rationale
underlying RQ-043's own origin ("The Framework should not immediately
adopt operational lessons. Operational experience should mature through
an evidence-based administrative process before any consideration of
constitutional adoption") and, as non-authoritative decision context,
in Founder Observation 5 ("Operational experience should inform the
Framework through governed maturation rather than immediate adoption").
The evidence also directly establishes the Founder's exact instruction
that Single Source of Truth is to be treated as "the first examined
example rather than the predetermined subject."

The evidence does not establish more than this. No defined governance
process — steps, roles, criteria, triggers, or decision points — for
maturing operational lessons into Framework administrative practices is
evidenced; only the stated intent that one should exist is present. No
explicit criteria exist in any mapped source against which Single
Source of Truth's, or any other pattern's, "satisfaction" of a
maturation process could be tested, so the second part of RQ-043 cannot
be answered on the mapped evidence. The evidence further shows,
factually, that Single Source of Truth's own adoption is recorded as a
direct Founder recommendation ("Adopted 2026-08-04, on Founder
recommendation, as a permanent programme-governance architecture
principle") without the mapped evidence documenting an intervening,
separately evidenced maturation procedure. This is recorded as a
neutral qualifying observation only: whether direct Founder
recommendation itself satisfies the rationale's own terms, or whether a
distinct maturation process is intended and simply does not yet exist,
is genuinely inconclusive and is not resolved by this examination in
either direction. Consistent with the anti-inference discipline applied
throughout D6, the demonstrated operation of Single Source of Truth
within FEF-FGR-002's own internal documents (EV-080, EV-086, EV-087) is
not treated as evidence of Framework-wide administrative capability or
of any tested cross-project operational-lesson maturation, since no
second adopting Founder project currently exists.

## 3. Evidence Gaps and Unsupported Matters

| Matter | Finding treatment |
|---|---|
| Defined governance process (steps, roles, criteria, triggers) for maturing operational lessons | Not evidenced; only stated intent that one should exist |
| Explicit criteria for testing whether a pattern has "matured" adequately | Not evidenced |
| Documented multi-step maturation procedure preceding Single Source of Truth's own adoption | Not evidenced; adoption recorded as direct Founder recommendation |
| Cross-project (multi-Founder-project) evidence of operational-lesson maturation | Not evidenced; no second adopting Founder project exists |

These gaps are part of the answer at finding level; they are not
silently filled by inference.

## 4. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

No mapped source directly contradicts another. Two qualifications
prevent overstatement:

- a textual tension exists between the Founder's stated rationale/
  Observation 5 and Single Source of Truth's own direct-recommendation
  adoption path; this is recorded as a neutral factual observation, not
  as a judgement that the adoption was improper or that any specific
  process should now be created; and
- Single Source of Truth's demonstrated operation within FEF-FGR-002's
  own internal documents must not be read as proof of Framework-wide
  capability or cross-project applicability.

## 5. Limitations, Uncertainty, and Open Questions

- Confidence is high that the Founder has stated, consistently and
  twice, an intent that operational lessons mature through an
  evidence-based process before adoption, and that Single Source of
  Truth is to be treated as the first examined example rather than the
  predetermined subject.
- No positive or negative confidence is assigned to whether direct
  Founder recommendation itself satisfies that stated intent — this is
  recorded as genuinely uncertain.
- No positive or negative confidence is assigned to whether Single
  Source of Truth is intended for retrospective evaluation against a
  future-defined process — this is recorded as genuinely uncertain.
- No positive confidence is assigned to the existence of any defined
  maturation process or testable criteria, because neither is evidenced
  to exist at any level.
- No Open Question maps directly to RQ-043. This finding does not
  create one.
- The five Founder Observations recorded in EV-089, including
  Observation 5, remain non-authoritative decision context only; this
  finding does not convert any of them into Framework policy.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and
  validation; validation is non-independent.

## 6. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The evidence does not decide what governance process, if any, should be
established to determine how operational lessons mature into Framework
administrative practices, nor whether Single Source of Truth's existing,
directly-recommended adoption should be treated as having already
satisfied such a process, as exempt from it, or as a pending candidate
for future retrospective evaluation. Those remain matters for separately
governed Founder judgement or later authorised work. This finding makes
no recommendation.

## 7. Scope and Confidence

**Confidence: High for the bounded observation that the Founder has
stated a consistent intent favouring evidence-based maturation and that
Single Source of Truth is designated the first examined example; not
applicable to any defined maturation process or testable criteria,
because neither is evidenced to exist; genuinely inconclusive for
whether Single Source of Truth's direct-recommendation adoption itself
satisfies the Founder's stated rationale.** The directly quoted rationale
and Observation 5 text in EV-089, and the directly quoted adoption
statement in EV-080, strongly support the bounded conclusion. The
absence of any defined process or criteria is the direct and sufficient
basis for the negative findings in §3.

The finding is scoped only to RQ-043 and the four mapped frozen records.
It does not decide RQ-038 through RQ-042 (already answered in GF-037
through GF-041), and does not decide D7's future maturation treatment of
constitutional documents.
