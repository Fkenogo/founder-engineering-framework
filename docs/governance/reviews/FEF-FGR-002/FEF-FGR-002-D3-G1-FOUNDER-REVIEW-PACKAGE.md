## Purpose

This package assembles the nine validated D3 temporary Review Question
candidates for Founder review at gate D3-G1. It reproduces, without
reinterpretation, the exact candidate wording from
FEF-FGR-002-D3-RQC-001 and the exact validation findings from
FEF-FGR-002-D3-RQC-VALIDATION-REPORT, and adds blank sections for the
Founder to record observations, discussion, disposition, rationale, and
follow-up actions.

This package does not perform the Founder Review. It does not recommend a
disposition for any candidate. It does not allocate a canonical Review
Question identifier, admit a Review Question, update a governance register,
create an Evidence Record, or create a Founder Decision.

Disposition options available to the Founder for each candidate are:

- **Accept**
- **Accept with Amendment**
- **Merge**
- **Split**
- **Reject**
- **Defer**

## Set-Level Context (from FEF-FGR-002-D3-RQC-001 and its validation)

- Nine candidates were prepared against ten provisional D3 mobilisation
  themes; no candidate exists merely to mirror a theme number, and no merge
  or removal was proposed at the validation pass.
- Every candidate treats D1 and D2 Founder Decisions as dependencies, not as
  questions to reopen; identifies D4–D8 only as bounded interfaces; and
  preserves the exact wording and open status of all 23 Open Questions.
- The Validator pass (FEF-FGR-002-D3-RQCVR-001) recorded **Pass with
  Conditions** at both candidate level and set level. The six set-level
  conditions in that report apply to every candidate below and are not
  repeated per candidate:
  1. the temporary references remain drafting aids only, not admitted or
     canonical RQs;
  2. the next gate must allocate collision-safe canonical identifiers only
     after confirming complete FEF-RQS-001 mandatory-field, evidence-need,
     dependency, and traceability treatment;
  3. the canonical Review Question Register must remain unchanged until that
     gate validly admits the questions;
  4. preliminary evidence classes must not be treated as registered,
     admitted, sufficient, or weighted evidence;
  5. the disclosed non-independent validation condition remains visible —
     the validation is not independent assurance;
  6. any material wording, purpose, domain, OQ mapping, or dependency change
     requires a new preparation pass and revalidation before admission.

## D3-RQC-01

### Candidate Reference

D3-RQC-01

### Candidate Wording

> Which governance records, transitions, and outputs require assurance across FEF-FGR-002, and by what evidence-bounded factors, if any, can proportionate assurance and minimum conditions for later reliance be distinguished?

### Constitutional Intent

D3 cannot evaluate whether assurance coverage is complete or proportionate
until the governance records, transitions, and outputs subject to assurance
are bounded, without assuming a single uniform level of assurance applies
everywhere. This candidate asks what must be covered and what
evidence-bounded factors could distinguish proportionate assurance, rather
than proposing a coverage model or threshold.

### Validation Summary

Validator result: **Retain.** Assessed as neutral and singular — assurance
coverage and proportionality form one indivisible reliance issue. D3 scope:
Pass. D1/D2 preservation: Pass. Open Question treatment: OQ-004, OQ-007, and
OQ-021 partial only, wording and status unchanged. Evidence-addressable:
Pass. Overlap treatment: bounded from the detailed controls examined in
D3-RQC-02 through D3-RQC-09, to prevent this candidate from absorbing every
later candidate.

### Coverage Notes

- **Dependencies:** D1 — bounded operational validation authority, distinct
  lifecycle transitions, reserved Founder authority. D2 — reliance begins
  only after evidence qualification and explicit sufficiency/gap treatment.
- **Cross-domain interfaces:** D4 records the resulting assurance evidence;
  D5 later governs final lifecycle states; D6 later governs permanent
  administration.
- **Exclusions:** no D5 lifecycle model, D6 register architecture, approval
  rule, quantitative threshold, project testing standard, or constitutional
  treatment.
- **Overlap risk:** could absorb every later candidate if "assurance" is
  read too broadly; bounded to coverage and proportionality factors, not the
  content of each individual assurance control.
