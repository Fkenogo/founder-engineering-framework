## 1. RQ and Evidence Boundary

### Exact Review Question

> What governance responsibilities, authority boundaries, and traceability, if any, are required for registers and records custodianship so that creation, maintenance, correction, access, handover, and accountability remain attributable without allowing custody to determine substantive meaning or Founder authority?

This examination used only the ten Evidence Records mapped to RQ-026 in
EP-004 v1.0: EV-005, EV-008, EV-016, EV-017, EV-019, EV-020, EV-021,
EV-059, EV-071, and EV-073. No unmapped evidence was introduced.

The evidence qualifications remain controlling:

- EV-005 and EV-008 are E2 Admitted within the approved review Charter and
  review execution-rule boundaries;
- EV-016, EV-017, EV-019, EV-020, and EV-021 are E2 Admitted as
  FEF-FGR-002 operational role, authority, responsibility, procedure, and
  assignment records, not enduring FEF-wide offices or controls;
- EV-059 is E2/E4 Admitted with its v1.0/v1.1 contradiction preserved and
  is limited to one same-capacity correction example;
- EV-071 is E1 Admitted as the attributable Founder Decision governing
  evidence custody only; and
- EV-073 is E4 Admitted as one validated D3 operational traceability
  example, not a universal model or current programme-state authority.

## 2. Evidence-Supported Observations

1. EV-005 separates the Review Administrator, Review Analyst, Review
   Recorder, Evidence Custodian, Validator, and Founder functions. Role
   combination does not combine their authority, and Founder authority
   cannot be delegated by implication.
2. EV-016 assigns review-scoped operational responsibilities by function:
   the Review Administrator coordinates registers and gates; the Review
   Recorder prepares attributable records, maintains history, corrects
   recording errors transparently, and links records to sources; the
   Evidence Custodian controls evidence identity, provenance, integrity,
   access, preservation, and pack custody; and the Validator tests
   structure, links, completeness, lifecycle, boundaries, and readiness.
   Each role is expressly barred from deciding matters outside its
   authority.
3. EV-017 separates operational administration, governance analysis,
   validation, Founder decision authority, and constitutional authority.
   Register control, record production, and custody may create or maintain
   operational records but cannot decide governance, infer Founder intent,
   or confer Founder authority.
4. EV-019 allocates review responsibility and accountability at activity
   level. The Review Administrator is accountable for the coherent register
   system; the Review Recorder is responsible for record-entry exactness;
   the Evidence Custodian is responsible for evidence and pack register
   administration; and the Validator is accountable for validation
   outcomes. The matrix states that operational accountability is not
   Founder decision authority.
5. EV-020 supplies a review-scoped assignment and handover procedure. It
   requires attributable authority, recorded assignment state, conflicts,
   limitations, controls, validation, linked predecessor/successor records,
   preserved audit history, access treatment, and defined reassignment,
   vacancy, succession, and handover information. It is a prescribed
   procedure; the mapped evidence contains no operated handover,
   reassignment, vacancy, or succession instance.
6. EV-021 shows six effective assignments and discloses that the same
   acting capacity combines the Administrator, Analyst, Recorder,
   Custodian, and Validator roles. Capacity labels, sequential passes,
   hashes, source comparison, and other compensating controls are recorded.
   The assignment snapshot demonstrates attributable allocation but not
   independent operation or a completed handover.
7. EV-071 supplies the controlling E1 boundary for evidence custody:
   custody preserves identity, provenance, access state, integrity,
   versions, Evidence Pack custody, and traceability, but custody,
   repository or tool access, authorship, recording, or validation does not
   confer authority to determine evidential meaning, approve governance, or
   exercise Founder authority. Its scope is evidence custody, not every
   FEF register or record class.
8. EV-059 provides one operated correction example. Version 1.0 incorrectly
   declared D3 closed and authorised a non-governed next stage; version 1.1
   explicitly corrected those claims, retained the original claims in
   repository history, stated the controlling active position, and
   identified the conflicting registers and gates. This demonstrates a
   traceable correction with history preserved. Because the same acting
   capacity produced and corrected the record, it does not evidence
   independent correction or establish general correction authority.
9. EV-073 demonstrates one end-to-end D3 chain connecting Review Question,
   evidence, Evidence Pack, session, Governance Finding, Founder
   disposition, Founder Decision, and domain treatment. It is evidence of
   operated attribution and linkage in D3 only.

## 3. Preserved EV-059 Contradiction

EV-059's internal contradiction remains explicit and unresolved:

- version 1.0 declared that D3 was formally closed and that the programme
  was authorised to commence a stage labelled D3-E1;
- version 1.1 states that the labels were not authorised programme-stage
  names, D3 remained active, DG-6 had not been invoked, and evidence
  mobilisation had not commenced; and
- version 1.1 preserves version 1.0's claims in repository history while
  superseding them for active reliance.

This examination relies only on the attributable fact and traceability of
that correction. It does not decide whether in-place correction, quarantine,
reopening, escalation, or another treatment should apply generally, and it
does not treat later correction as proof that the original control
environment was sufficient.

## 5. Matters Requiring Founder Judgement

The mapped evidence does not itself decide:

- whether the review-scoped division of register administration, recording,
  custody, validation, and decision authority should become an enduring
  FEF-wide responsibility model;
- which roles should own or maintain particular register and record classes;
- what correction, challenge, approval, and escalation authorities should
  apply across those classes;
- what handover, succession, access-removal, and independent-control
  requirements should apply generally; or
- whether the current combined-capacity arrangement is acceptable for
  later enduring register governance.

These are matters for later Founder judgement. This finding selects no
answer and makes no recommendation.

## 6. Conclusion

The mapped evidence establishes a clear FEF-FGR-002 operational separation:
register administration, record-entry exactness, evidence custody,
validation, and Founder decision authority are allocated to distinguishable
roles, with traceability and capacity-labelling controls. EV-071 supplies an
attributable Founder boundary that custody preserves the evidence chain but
does not decide meaning, approve governance, or confer Founder authority.
EV-059 and EV-073 show bounded operated examples of transparent correction
and end-to-end traceability.

The same corpus does not establish an approved FEF-wide register-ownership
or records-custodianship model, a general correction-authority rule,
independently operated control, or an operated handover. EV-059 remains a
contradictory same-capacity correction example, and EV-073 remains one D3
example. Enduring ownership, correction, accountability, independence, and
handover treatment therefore require Founder judgement and cannot be
inferred from review-scoped roles or repeated practice.

## 7. Contrary and Qualifying Evidence

EV-059 is the material contrary and qualifying record. Its corrected
version demonstrates transparent correction and retained history, while
its superseded version evidences that the same control environment first
produced an inaccurate closure and authority claim. Both sides are
preserved. The correction is not treated as erasing the defect, proving
independent assurance, or establishing a general correction procedure.

EV-021's effective assignments demonstrate attributable current role
coverage but simultaneously disclose combined capacities. EV-020 prescribes
handover controls without demonstrating an operated handover. EV-073
demonstrates traceability once without establishing universal sufficiency.
These qualifications narrow rather than displace the conclusion.

No absence outside EP-004 or outside RQ-026's ten-item mapping is claimed.

## 8. Limitations and Uncertainty

- The finding is limited to the ten mapped EP-004 v1.0 Evidence Records.
- The role, responsibility, assignment, and procedure sources are scoped to
  FEF-FGR-002.
- EV-071 is authoritative only for evidence custody.
- Access is examined only as a custodial and assignment responsibility;
  confidentiality, privacy, security, and technical access governance remain
  outside RQ-026.
- OQ-002, OQ-021, and OQ-023 remain open and unchanged; their mappings are
  not dispositions.
- No D6 administrative structure, taxonomy, or identifier policy is
  inferred.
- The same acting capacity performed analysis, recording, and validation;
  validation is non-independent.

## 9. Confidence Statement

**Confidence: Moderate.** The review-scoped responsibility separation and
the evidence-custody authority boundary are directly supported, including
by the E1 Founder Decision in EV-071. Confidence is bounded because the
operated correction and traceability examples are single cases, current
assignments combine roles, and no operated handover or FEF-wide ownership
model is mapped.
