## 1. Purpose and Authority

This package drafts the final neutral wording for the four D6 Review
Question candidates the Founder Confirmed **with Amendment** by way of a
refocusing instruction rather than complete replacement text — D6-RQC-02,
D6-RQC-03, D6-RQC-04, and D6-RQC-05 — and demonstrates, for each, that the
drafted wording remains strictly within the boundary the Founder set.

Inputs to this package are, unmodified:

- the validated D6 Candidate Set,
  [FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001-FRAMEWORK-ADMINISTRATION-REVIEW-QUESTION-CANDIDATE-SET.md)
  v1.0, validated Pass with Conditions in
  [FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQCVR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-VALIDATION-REPORT.md);
- the Founder Candidate Review Disposition Record,
  [FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-FDR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-FOUNDER-CANDIDATE-REVIEW-DISPOSITION-RECORD.md),
  validated Pass in
  [FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-FDVR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-FOUNDER-CANDIDATE-REVIEW-DISPOSITION-VALIDATION-REPORT.md),
  which supplies every amendment instruction and rationale reproduced in §3
  below, exactly as recorded;
- the eight D6 mobilisation conditions,
  [FEF-FGR-002-D6-FMAR-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-FOUNDER-MOBILISATION-AUTHORISATION-RECORD.md)
  §2; and
- the validated engineering blueprint,
  [FEF-FGR-002-D6-EA-001](FEF-FGR-002-D6-EXAMINATION-ARCHITECTURE.md).

This package stops at the **pre-admission drafting boundary**. It does not:

FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001 and FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-FDR-001 are not edited by

## 2. Drafting Method

For each of the four candidates requiring drafting, the Review Analyst:

1. took the Founder's amendment instruction and rationale from
   FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-FDR-001 §2 as the sole source of required change —
   no additional scope, theme, or emphasis not present in the Founder's own
   words was introduced;
2. identified every discrete element of the instruction (a required
   substitution, a required addition, a required omission, or a required
   framing constraint) and traced each into the drafted wording, recorded
   in the per-candidate Boundary Compliance table below;
3. preserved, unless the amendment instruction itself required a change,
   every non-wording element of the original candidate recorded in
   FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001 §3 — primary theme linkage, D1/D4/D5 dependency
   treatment, Open Question interface, explicit exclusions, and
   preliminary evidence classes;
4. re-applied the neutrality tests already used to validate
   FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001 (FEF-RQS-001; FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQCVR-001 §5): the
   drafted wording asks what is needed or should exist rather than
   asserting a preferred answer, uses "if any" wherever the baseline does
   not already establish that some control is mandatory, and remains
   evidence-addressable and singular; and
5. did not draft, alter, or re-present D6-RQC-01 or D6-RQC-06, whose
   wording the Founder already supplied in full and which are carried
   forward unchanged in §4.

No drafted wording expands scope beyond FEF-FGR-002-D6-MPP-001 §2, and no
drafted wording is influenced by the coding agent's own preference — every
substantive word choice is traceable to either the original candidate
wording (FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001) or the Founder's exact amendment
instruction (FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-FDR-001).

## 3. Finalised Wording, Boundary Demonstration, and Carried-Forward Metadata

### 3.1 D6-RQC-02 — Maintenance and Current-State Integrity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Original candidate wording | What minimum maintenance and current-state reporting responsibility, if any, is needed to keep programme and governance control records current, mutually intelligible, and synchronised, and how should that synchronisation be verified going forward? |
| Founder amendment instruction (exact) | Refine the wording so the examination determines: minimum maintenance responsibility; minimum synchronisation responsibility; and minimum verification responsibility, if any, consistent with the philosophy of Minimum Viable Administration. Avoid wording that presumes a standing verification mechanism. |
| Founder rationale (exact) | The Framework should determine the minimum administration necessary to preserve governance integrity rather than assume increasing verification is the appropriate administrative response. |
| **Drafted final wording** | **What minimum maintenance responsibility, minimum synchronisation responsibility, and minimum verification responsibility, if any, are needed to keep programme and governance control records current, mutually intelligible, and synchronised, consistent with the philosophy of Minimum Viable Administration?** |

**Boundary Compliance**

| Founder Instruction Element | Present in Drafted Wording |
|---|---|
| "the examination determines: minimum maintenance responsibility" | "What minimum maintenance responsibility ... are needed" |
| "minimum synchronisation responsibility" | "minimum synchronisation responsibility ... are needed" |
| "minimum verification responsibility, if any" | "minimum verification responsibility, if any" — verification is now one co-equal, optional item rather than an assumed follow-on step |
| "consistent with the philosophy of Minimum Viable Administration" | "consistent with the philosophy of Minimum Viable Administration" — reproduced verbatim |
| "Avoid wording that presumes a standing verification mechanism" | The original clause "how should that synchronisation be verified going forward" (which presumes verification occurs and asks only how) is removed; verification is instead posed as a "responsibility, if any," symmetrical with maintenance and synchronisation, presuming nothing |

**Neutrality check:** the drafted wording does not assert that maintenance,
synchronisation, or verification responsibility is required; "if any"
governs verification specifically and the whole clause is bounded by
"minimum." No implementation, standard, or ownership assignment is
proposed.

**Carried forward unchanged from FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001 §3.2:** primary
theme (Theme 2); D1/D4/D5 dependency treatment; OQ-014 partial interface
(administrative traceability/maintenance fields only; substantive OQ-014
resolution remains a D8 matter); explicit exclusion that no maintenance
procedure, verification tool, or responsibility is adopted by the
candidate itself, and that the Single Source of Truth model itself is not
redesigned; preliminary evidence requirement classes.

### 3.3 D6-RQC-04 — Administrative Coherence

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Original candidate wording | What residual identifier, register, and document-relationship consistency issues, if any, remain after D1, D4, and D5's controls, and what minimum identifier-governance, register-state, and document-lifecycle/discoverability practice would address them without renumbering, migrating, or redesigning existing records? |
| Founder amendment instruction (exact) | Refocus the wording toward administrative coherence rather than relational mapping. The examination should determine the minimum administrative relationships and consistency controls necessary to preserve governance integrity. |
| Founder rationale (exact) | The Framework should avoid over-cataloguing administrative relationships. The distinction between Single Source of Truth (ownership) and Administrative Coherence (structural place) should remain explicit. |
| **Drafted final wording** | **What minimum administrative relationships and consistency controls, if any, are necessary among identifiers, registers, and controlled documents to preserve governance integrity — administrative coherence, as distinct from Single Source of Truth ownership — after D1, D4, and D5's controls, without renumbering, migrating, or redesigning existing records?** |

**Boundary Compliance**

| Founder Instruction Element | Present in Drafted Wording |
|---|---|
| "Refocus the wording toward administrative coherence rather than relational mapping" | The original's issue-cataloguing frame ("What residual ... consistency issues ... remain") is replaced with a necessity frame ("What minimum ... relationships and consistency controls ... are necessary ... to preserve governance integrity") |
| "determine the minimum administrative relationships and consistency controls necessary to preserve governance integrity" | Reproduced near-verbatim: "minimum administrative relationships and consistency controls, if any, are necessary ... to preserve governance integrity" |
| Rationale — "avoid over-cataloguing administrative relationships; relationships should exist only where they demonstrably preserve governance integrity" | The wording asks only what is *necessary*, not what issues or relationships exist to be catalogued |
| Rationale — "distinction between Single Source of Truth (ownership) and Administrative Coherence (structural place) should remain explicit" | Named explicitly: "administrative coherence, as distinct from Single Source of Truth ownership" |

**Neutrality check:** the drafted wording does not assert that any
administrative relationship or consistency control is necessary; "if any"
governs the entire clause. The pre-existing exclusion — "without
renumbering, migrating, or redesigning existing records" — is preserved
unchanged, since the Founder's instruction addressed the examination's
focus, not this exclusion, and FEF-RCR-001's completed navigation audit

**Carried forward unchanged from FEF-FGR-002-D6-RQC-001 §3.4:** D1/D4/D5
dependency treatment; OQ-015 direct interface (administrative-consistency
question only; the permanent work-package identifier policy itself remains

overlap with D6-RQC-01 on ownership versus identifier custody — is resolved
by the Founder's own instruction: the wording now names the Single Source
of Truth / Administrative Coherence distinction explicitly, so ownership
(D6-RQC-01) and structural coherence (D6-RQC-04) remain separately
examinable by construction, not merely by drafting convention.

| "governance efficiency" | "... and governance efficiency" |
