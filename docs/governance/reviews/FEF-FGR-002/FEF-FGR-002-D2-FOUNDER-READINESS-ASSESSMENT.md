## 4. Decisions That May Be Bounded Now

The evidence supports Founder consideration of:

- GF-009’s qualification and permitted-reliance principle, with external and
  protected-source policy reserved;
- GF-010’s no-automation and attributable-rationale boundary, without a full
  hierarchy;
- GF-011’s qualitative coverage/gap/conflict/stop requirement, without numeric
  thresholds;
- GF-012’s immutable freeze principle, with supplements and successors
  provisional;
- GF-013’s precise traceability and no-authority-elevation rule, without a
  permanent register architecture;
- GF-014’s custody-versus-meaning and custody-versus-Founder-authority
  boundary, with D3/D4 subjects reserved.

“May be bounded now” is a maturity assessment, not a recommended disposition.

## 5. Matters Unsuitable for D2 Decision

- a complete evidence weighting hierarchy or conflict-priority model;
- quantitative universal sufficiency thresholds;
- legal, privacy, security, professional, or project-specific evidence policy;
- restricted-source control details not operated in EP-002;
- permanent supplement or successor mechanics before governed operation;
- permanent D6 register architecture or FEF-P1-002 source hierarchy;
- independent assurance thresholds and residual-risk model;
- retention schedules, archival architecture, deletion, legal hold, and
  information lifecycle;
- constitutional wording or candidate approval.

## 6. Risk of Deciding Now

The principal risk is over-generalisation: an unconditioned disposition could
be read beyond the evidence into untested external, protected, successor,
cross-domain, continuity, assurance, or D4 contexts.

That risk can be managed only by the Founder’s exact scope and conditions. This
assessment does not supply those conditions or accept the risk.

## 7. Risk of Delaying

Delay would leave the exact D2 findings undispositioned and keep the enduring
status of qualification, weighting, sufficiency, freeze, traceability, and
custody controls unresolved. Current FEF-FGR-002 operations remain governed by
their existing sources, but later-domain reuse and eventual consolidation would
lack attributable D2 Founder input.

## 8. Cross-Domain and Candidate Dependencies

- D3: independent assurance, challenge, conflict validation, and residual risk.
- D4: access, preservation, handover, retention, archival, deletion, and
  information lifecycle.
- D5: framework-wide versioning, supersession, and reopening.
- D6: permanent identifiers and register architecture.
- D7: constitutional-candidate testing after attributable disposition.
- D8: OQ-005, OQ-006, OQ-010, OQ-011, and OQ-018.

No Constitutional Candidate exists. An accepted finding might later supply an
input to D7; it would not automatically satisfy the Charter’s ten candidate
tests.

## 9. Independent Validation Condition

The same Codex agent prepared and validated the S02 findings and this package.
This is not independent assurance. The Founder may disposition a finding,
condition reliance, return it for evidence, require independent revalidation,
reject it, or validly defer it. This assessment does not select among those
options.

## 10. Verdict

**Ready with Conditions.**

All six findings are sufficiently mature for Founder consideration within their
exact evidence-bounded scope. GF-012 and GF-014 require particular caution
because important change-path and continuity controls remain unoperated.
GF-009, GF-010, GF-011, and GF-013 are ready only for bounded disposition; none
supports unrestricted framework-wide implementation beyond its recorded
limitations.

## 11. Non-Effects

This assessment creates no disposition, Founder Decision, identifier,
candidate, deferral, OQ change, constitutional effect, FEF-P1-002 approval,
RGS amendment, or Engineering Discovery authority.
