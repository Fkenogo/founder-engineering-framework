## 1. Method

Every candidate source in FEF-FGR-002-D3-ERC-002 §4 (new) and every reused
source class was checked against the ten mandatory admissibility tests in
FEF-FGRC-001 §9.2 (identity, provenance, integrity, authority, relevance,
temporal applicability, accessibility, conflict treatment, uncertainty,
permitted use). Existence in the repository or the Document Manifest is not
treated as qualification; each source's evidence class and admissibility
result are independently recorded below.

This qualification pass and the requirement derivation in
FEF-FGR-002-D3-ERC-002 were both prepared by the same combined operational
capacity. This is **not independent assurance**. Capacity labelling,
sequential passes, exact-source comparison, and this explicit disclosure are
the compensating controls, consistent with every prior validation record in
this repository. No claim of independence is made anywhere in this record.

## 5. Contradictory Evidence Summary

Per FEF-FGRC-001 §9.2 test 8 and §9.3 ("shall not hide conflicting
evidence"), the following contradictions are explicitly preserved in the
qualified evidence base rather than resolved or hidden:

1. **EV-058:** the quarantined material's claims (admission, evidence
   registration) directly contradict the active repository state. Both the
   claim and its contradiction are part of the qualified record.
2. **EV-059:** FEF-FGR-002-D3-C1-001 v1.0's closure claim directly
   contradicts its own v1.1 correction. Both remain visible in the same
   document.

Neither contradiction is resolved by this qualification pass. Resolving
what they mean for D3's substantive assurance conclusions is examination
work, reserved for a later, separately governed gate.
