## 1. Method and Boundaries

Each admitted Review Question was examined against only the Evidence Records
listed for that RQ in
[FEF-FGR-002-EP-002 v1.0](FEF-FGR-002-EP-002-v1.0-D2-EVIDENCE-PACK.md).
Evidence class, admissibility, authority, and limitations were preserved.

The record separates:

- **established evidence** — an attributable provision or operated record;
- **interpretation** — an evidence-bounded governance implication;
- **risk** — a consequence supported by the recorded limitation or control;
- **possible treatment** — an option for later Founder consideration;
- **uncertainty** — a matter the pack does not establish.

FEF-RQS-001 and FEF-EPS-001 are used conditionally as approved Plan
preparation controls, not as generally approved Framework standards.
FEF-FGR-001 is used only as Context Only evidence of the historical gap. No
external knowledge, assumption, post-freeze source, or reconstructed historical
content was used.

## 2. RQ-009 — Evidence Identity, Provenance, Authority, and Integrity

### 2.1 Exact RQ

**FEF-FGR-002-RQ-009:** What minimum controls are required to establish the
identity, provenance, acquisition route, issuer or source authority, version,
temporal applicability, and integrity of evidence used by FEF governance
reviews?

### 2.2 Evidence Examined

EV-005 Sections 9–10; EV-006 Sections 4 and 6.2; EV-007 Sections 9–10;
EV-008 Sections 5, 9, and 12; EV-012 OQ-005; EV-014; EV-022 Sections 4,
7, 11–13; EV-023 Sections 5, 8, 10, and 12; EV-024 Sections 1–4; EV-025
Sections 1–6; EV-026 Sections 1, 3, 7–8; EV-027 Sections 2–6; EV-031
Sections 1–9.

### 2.3 Evidence Established

- EV-005 requires unique identity, origin and custody, version or date,
  integrity indication, source authority and limits, relevance, temporal
  applicability, accessibility, conflict treatment, uncertainty, and permitted
  use before evidence may support a GF.
- EV-023 specifies a pack manifest containing Evidence Record identity, stable
  source reference, version, evidence class, admissibility, access, and
  integrity data, with SHA-256 where technically possible.
- EV-024 and EV-025 demonstrate operated registration of unique identifiers,
  repository paths, source authority, provenance, acquisition date, version or
  observed state, class, admissibility, digest, and RQ mapping.
- EV-026 and EV-027 demonstrate an ordered, hashed, frozen pack and a separate
  recalculation pass, subject to the disclosed non-independent role limitation.
- EV-031 demonstrates why source identity and attributable provenance are
  necessary: extensive search activity could prove non-recovery but could not
  make unsupported historical content authoritative.

### 2.4 Evidence Not Established

  from conditional provenance fields.
- It does not establish controls for every mutable, non-file, restricted,

  FEF-P1-002.

### 2.5 Corroboration and Conflict

EV-005, EV-007, EV-008, and conditionally admitted EV-022/EV-023 align on
identity, provenance, version, authority, integrity, and traceability. EV-024
through EV-027 provide operated corroboration. No contradictory source is
registered. EV-014 and EV-031 corroborate the risk of absent provenance but do
not supply historical governance content.

### 2.6 Limitations and Gaps

- OQ-005 remains open.
- FEF-RQS-001 and FEF-EPS-001 retain draft-status headers.
- Validation is non-independent.
- No E3 or project-specific evidence tests external-source handling.

### 2.7 Evidence-Bounded Analysis

**Risk:** Without an attributable qualification record, later review outputs
cannot distinguish source replacement, stale state, unsupported authority, or
unrecoverable provenance.

**Possible treatment:** Preserve these controls as the minimum review-evidence
qualification baseline while leaving the permanent mandatory/conditional field
catalogue to Founder disposition and later authorised governance.

**Founder judgement:** Required to make any enduring mandatory-field rule.

### 2.8 Provisional Treatment

## 3. RQ-010 — Evidence Classes, Weighting, Corroboration, and Challenge

### 3.1 Exact RQ

**FEF-FGR-002-RQ-010:** How should evidence classes, authority limitations,
corroboration, contradiction, and challenge affect the weight permitted for a
source without converting repetition or preference into authority?

### 3.2 Evidence Examined

EV-005 Section 9; EV-006 Sections 4 and 6.2; EV-007 Sections 6, 8–10;
EV-008 Sections 2, 5, and 9; EV-012 OQ-006; EV-014; EV-022 Sections 7 and
11; EV-023 Sections 2, 5, 8, and 15; EV-024 Sections 2–4; EV-025 Sections
1–6; EV-026 Sections 3–6.

### 3.3 Evidence Established

- EV-005 defines E1–E5 and C1 by source type and permitted use, not by an
  automatic numerical rank.
- E1 may establish attributable Founder intent within scope; E2 establishes
  controlled recorded state within authority; E3 establishes external
  constraint or recognised practice; E4 supports technical implications; E5
  advises; C1 cannot support a conclusion without corroboration.
- EV-005 prohibits treating repetition as corroboration and requires contrary
  or superseding evidence to remain visible.
- EV-023 states that a pack does not elevate source authority and requires
  admitted, rejected, conditional, and context-only treatment to remain
  distinguishable.
- EV-024 through EV-026 demonstrate different reliance treatments for
  controlled records, conditional preparation/index records, and Context Only
  history.

### 3.4 Evidence Not Established

- No approved ordinal weighting algorithm or automatic hierarchy exists.
- The pack contains no competing E1 or E3 sources from which to derive a rule
  for resolving equally authoritative conflict.
- It does not establish when corroboration is quantitatively sufficient.
- It does not create the D3 assurance or risk-acceptance model.

### 3.5 Corroboration and Conflict

The Charter class definitions and operated D1 admissibility treatments
corroborate class-sensitive reliance. The preparation specifications align but
remain conditionally admitted. No registered evidence contradicts the rule
that source authority and scope constrain use. The absence of an explicit
weighting rule is a gap, not contrary evidence.

### 3.6 Limitations and Gaps

- OQ-006 remains open.
- No E3 evidence is present.
- No live contested-source example was admitted.
- Human or Founder judgement remains necessary where source authority,
  relevance, and conflict cannot be reconciled deterministically.

### 3.7 Evidence-Bounded Analysis

**Interpretation:** Evidence class determines the kind of proposition a source
may support, but does not by itself determine decisive weight. Weight must also
account for exact authority, scope, provenance, relevance, temporal state,
corroboration, contradiction, and uncertainty.

**Risk:** Automatic class ranking could elevate stale or out-of-scope evidence,
convert repetition into authority, or automate reserved judgement.

**Possible treatment:** Use class as a permitted-use boundary and require an
explicit, attributable rationale when evidential weight is contested.

**Founder judgement:** Required for any enduring evidence hierarchy or
conflict-priority rule.

### 3.8 Provisional Treatment

**Partially answerable.** Permitted-use boundaries and the prohibition on
automatic authority are supported; an enduring weighting hierarchy remains
unresolved.

## 4. RQ-011 — Evidence Admissibility and Permitted Use

### 4.1 Exact RQ

**FEF-FGR-002-RQ-011:** What tests and recorded dispositions should govern
admission, conditional admission, context-only use, rejection, access-pending
treatment, supersession, and permitted reliance on protected or restricted
evidence?

### 4.2 Evidence Examined

EV-005 Sections 9–10; EV-006 Sections 4 and 6.2; EV-007 Sections 8–10;
EV-008 Sections 5, 9, and 10; EV-012 OQ-005, OQ-010, and OQ-018; EV-017

Sections 1–7; EV-026 Sections 3–6; EV-027 Sections 3–7; EV-028 Sections
1–5.

### 4.3 Evidence Established

- EV-005 supplies ten mandatory admissibility tests and requires rejection,
  conditional admission, or context-only treatment when a test fails.
- EV-023 supplies six explicit dispositions: Admitted, Conditionally Admitted,
  Context Only, Rejected, Access Pending, and Superseded; conditional admission
  must state permitted reliance.
- EV-016 through EV-019 and EV-021 separate custody, validation, analysis, and
  Founder authority. Custody and validation may constrain use but cannot decide
  evidential meaning or governance outcomes.
- EV-024 through EV-028 demonstrate registration, conditional/context
  restrictions, pack validation, integrity checks, and entry-gate enforcement.

### 4.4 Evidence Not Established

- The pack does not establish legal, privacy, security, confidentiality, or
  project-specific access classifications.
- No restricted source or Access Pending item was operated in EP-002.
- No source was rejected or superseded in the operated examples.
- It does not establish external-authority competence for disputed protected
  evidence.

### 4.5 Corroboration and Conflict

EV-005 and conditionally admitted EV-023 align on tests and dispositions.
EV-024 through EV-028 corroborate operated enforcement. No contradiction is
registered. The absence of restricted-source operation limits generalisation.

### 4.6 Limitations and Gaps

- OQ-005, OQ-010, and OQ-018 remain open.
- No E3 evidence is present.
- Current custody and validation are non-independent.
- Protected-source treatment beyond metadata and escalation is not tested.

### 4.7 Evidence-Bounded Analysis

**Interpretation:** Admissibility is a recorded gate that constrains permitted
reliance; it is not a truth determination. A source must retain its exact
disposition, rationale, access limitation, and authority boundary throughout
later use.

**Risk:** Treating registration as admission, Context Only as authoritative, or
conditional evidence as unqualified would silently expand source authority.

**Possible treatment:** Maintain the present tests and controlled dispositions,
require an explicit rationale and permitted-use statement, and escalate
material access or authority ambiguity.

**Founder judgement:** Required for enduring mandatory evidence requirements
and any external/protected evidence policy.

### 4.8 Provisional Treatment

**Answer supported with limitations.** The admission and permitted-use control
model is evidenced and operated; external and protected-evidence policy remains
outside the present evidence.

### 5.1 Exact RQ

material limitations, contradiction, uncertainty, absence, and unavailable
evidence be assessed and disclosed before a governance question may be
examined?

### 5.2 Evidence Examined

EV-005 Sections 9–10; EV-006 Sections 4, 6.2, and 11; EV-007 Sections 8–10;
EV-008 Sections 5, 8–10, and 12; EV-012 OQ-006 and OQ-018; EV-014; EV-022

3–5; EV-025 Sections 1–7; EV-026 Sections 4–7; EV-027 Sections 3–8; EV-031
Sections 2–9.

### 5.3 Evidence Established

- EV-005 requires relevance, conflict, uncertainty, temporal state, access, and
  permitted-use treatment before reliance.
- EV-008 prohibits validation from creating missing evidence or resolving
  contradiction by preference, and provides return, block, escalation, and
  reopening controls.
- EV-022 permits an RQ to proceed with an explicit gap only when entry
  validation permits it; a missing source cannot be converted into an answer.
- EV-023 requires conflicts, gaps, unavailable material, conditional evidence,
  and known contrary evidence to remain visible.
- EV-025, EV-027, and EV-031 demonstrate limited reliance, recorded
  non-recovery, and controlled proceed-with-condition treatment.

### 5.4 Evidence Not Established

- No universal quantitative sufficiency threshold is approved.
- The pack does not determine every RQ-specific minimum evidence class.
- No material contradiction between two authoritative sources was operated.
- No E3 source determines whether external completeness obligations are met.

### 5.5 Corroboration and Conflict

The Charter, Execution Rules, and conditional preparation specifications align
on explicit limitations, no assumptions, and stop/return/escalation. EV-031
demonstrates that an evidence gap can support a limited finding about
non-recovery while not supporting missing substantive content. No contrary
rule is registered.

### 5.6 Limitations and Gaps

- OQ-006 and OQ-018 remain open.
- No E3 or project-specific evidence is present.
- The operated examples test absence and limitation, not competing external
  obligations.
- Validation is non-independent.

### 5.7 Evidence-Bounded Analysis

**Interpretation:** Sufficiency is RQ- and proposition-specific. A gap may be
non-blocking only when the remaining evidence supports a bounded proposition,
the limitation is explicit, and no missing source could reasonably reverse
that proposition. Otherwise the affected path must pause, return, or block.

**Risk:** A universal completeness assertion or assumption-filled gap would
make uncertainty invisible and could contaminate later findings and decisions.

**Possible treatment:** Require every examination to state coverage, contrary
evidence, limitations, unavailable evidence, and a reasoned proceed,
conditional-proceed, or block treatment.

**Founder judgement:** Required for enduring sufficiency policy and any
acceptance of material residual risk.

### 5.8 Provisional Treatment

**Answer supported with limitations.** The qualitative decision model is
supported; universal thresholds and external completeness requirements are not.

## 6. RQ-013 — Evidence Freeze, Versioning, and Change Control

### 6.1 Exact RQ

**FEF-FGR-002-RQ-013:** What controls should govern evidence-pack assembly,
versioning, freeze, integrity verification, supersession, supplements,
successor packs, and post-freeze change?

### 6.2 Evidence Examined

EV-005 Sections 9–10; EV-006 Sections 4 and 6.2; EV-007 Sections 8–10;
EV-008 Sections 3, 5–7, and 12; EV-022 Sections 11–13; EV-023 Sections 2–13
and 15; EV-026 Sections 1–8; EV-027 Sections 1–8; EV-028 Sections 1–5;
EV-029 Sections 1–6.

### 6.3 Evidence Established

- EV-007 defines baseline, domain, session, supplemental, and decision-extract
  pack layers and requires identity, version, manifest, limitations, freeze,
  integrity, and RQ/session mapping.
- EV-008 requires frozen packs to remain immutable; new material must use a
  successor or Supplemental Pack and may trigger pause or reopening.
- EV-023 defines Candidate through Archived states, `1.0` first freeze, no
  in-place mutation, major versioning for material evidence change, supplement
  controls, SHA-256 verification points, and post-change impact assessment.
- EV-026 through EV-029 demonstrate a frozen pack, exact digest, separate entry
  validation, and bounded session use without post-freeze evidence.

### 6.4 Evidence Not Established

- The conditional preparation controls are not generally approved Framework
  lifecycle standards.
- No successor major version or Supplemental Pack has yet been operated.
- The evidence does not define the D5 framework-wide lifecycle model.

### 6.5 Corroboration and Conflict

EV-007, EV-008, and conditionally admitted EV-023 align on immutable freeze,
versioned change, impact assessment, and revalidation. EV-026 through EV-029
corroborate initial freeze and use. No contrary evidence is registered.

### 6.6 Limitations and Gaps

- FEF-EPS-001 retains a draft-status header.
- Successor and supplement controls are specified but not yet exercised.
- Non-independent custody/validation remains a limitation.
- Framework-wide lifecycle authority remains D5.

### 6.7 Evidence-Bounded Analysis

**Interpretation:** A frozen pack is a stable examination baseline, not an
assertion of truth. Material change must preserve the prior pack, identify the
trigger and changed items, reassess admissibility and impact, revalidate, and
re-freeze before reliance.

**Risk:** In-place mutation or informal post-freeze evidence would destroy
reproducibility and make the evidence-to-session chain unverifiable.

**Possible treatment:** Retain the operated freeze controls for review
execution and later test supplement/successor operation before generalising
beyond the review.

**Founder judgement:** Potentially required to make this an enduring
framework-wide rule; not required for current review operation.

### 6.8 Provisional Treatment

**Answer supported with limitations.** Freeze and change controls are
specified and initial freeze is operated; successor/supplement operation and
D5 generalisation remain untested.

1–16; EV-030 Sections 1–4; EV-031 Sections 1–9.

### 8.3 Evidence Established

- EV-005 separates Evidence Custodian, Analyst, Recorder, Validator, and
  Founder authority.
- EV-016 assigns evidence identity, provenance, integrity, admissibility
  records, preservation, access, registers, pack assembly/freeze, version
  preservation, and gap reporting to the Evidence Custodian, while prohibiting

- EV-017 through EV-019 separate administration, analysis, validation, and

### 8.4 Evidence Not Established

- The pack does not define D4 retention schedules, archival architecture,
  deletion, legal hold, or organisation-wide information lifecycle.
- No restricted evidence was handled.
- No operational handover, vacancy, or successor-custodian event was tested.
- No E3 evidence defines external preservation or confidentiality duties.

### 8.5 Corroboration and Conflict

The Charter role model, operational role documents, RACI, assignment register,

review by D1 decision evidence referenced through EV-030.

### 8.6 Limitations and Gaps

- Validation remains non-independent.
- OQ-010 and OQ-011 remain open.
- No E3 or project-specific evidence is present.
- Historical evidence remains unavailable.
- D4 controls are outside D2.

### 8.7 Evidence-Bounded Analysis

**Interpretation:** Evidence custody is an operational accountability for
preserving identity, provenance, access state, integrity, versions, and
traceability. It must remain separate from evidential interpretation and
Founder judgement even where one individual holds multiple labelled
capacities.

**Risk:** Combined custody, analysis, and validation increases undetected error
and self-validation risk. Missing handover and continuity evidence leaves the
durability of the role model untested.

**Possible treatment:** Retain explicit role boundaries, capacity labels,
deterministic checks, conflict disclosure, escalation, and later independent
revalidation objectives. Carry retention and archival design into D4 without
creating a D2 policy.

**Founder judgement:** Potentially required for enduring custody and
independence requirements; no D4 decision should be made here.

### 8.8 Provisional Treatment

**Partially answerable.** Current operational responsibility and minimum
evidence-chain preservation are supported; restricted evidence, handover,
external duties, and D4 lifecycle controls are not.
