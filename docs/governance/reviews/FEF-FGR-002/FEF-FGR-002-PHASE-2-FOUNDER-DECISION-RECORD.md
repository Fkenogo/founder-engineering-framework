## 1. Decision Question

> **Should the Founder Governance Review methodology demonstrated through Phase 2 continue unchanged for the remaining review domains?**

## 2. Exact Founder Record

The following record reproduces the Founder-supplied wording without
interpretation, amendment, or supplementation.

<!-- BEGIN EXACT FOUNDER RECORD -->
**Founder Disposition**
Approve with Conditions
**Founder Conditions**
The Founder Governance Review methodology demonstrated through Phase 2 is approved for continued use in the remaining review domains.
The methodology shall continue under the existing governance instruments, authority boundaries, decision gates, validation controls, traceability requirements, and protected-state controls.
Any improvements identified after this milestone shall be treated as prospective framework evolution and shall not retrospectively alter or reinterpret the closed D1–D3 domains.
A future framework work item shall be established to evaluate a **Context Continuity Framework (FEF-CCF-001)**, including Engineering Context Snapshots and related continuity artefacts. This future work is outside the scope of this decision and shall follow the Framework Evolution process.
Commencement of D4 remains subject to its own separately governed Founder authorisation and entry sequence.
**Founder Rationale**
The demonstrated methodology has shown sufficient maturity, repeatability, governance control, traceability, and repository integrity across D1, ORC-001, D2, and D3 to continue into the remaining review domains. Future enhancements should be managed through controlled Framework Evolution rather than by modifying or reinterpreting the validated methodology already demonstrated.
**Founder Follow-up**
Record and validate this Founder disposition.
Synchronise programme records.
Register **FEF-CCF-001 — Context Continuity Framework** as a future Framework Evolution candidate in the Master Programme.
Prepare, but do not commence, D4 pending separate Founder authorisation.
<!-- END EXACT FOUNDER RECORD -->
