## 1. Purpose

This package presents the validated FEF-PAR-001 Programme Architecture
Review for Founder consideration. It is a reading and options package,
not a disposition workbook: PAR-001 is a strategic architectural
assessment, not a set of Governance Findings requiring individual
Accept/Reject treatment. No disposition is solicited by this package.

## 2. Executive Summary

After six formally Closed governance domains (D1–D6), 43 Review
Questions, 42 Governance Findings, and 44 Founder Decisions, the
Framework Engineering Framework has produced a complete, byte-verifiable
governance audit trail — entirely within the scope of a single project's
own self-governance. Every governance activity examined across all six
domains has evidence-bounded limits on cross-project applicability: no
governance activity in D1–D6 has ever been operated by, or tested
against, a second Founder project. Zero of the programme's 23 baseline
Open Questions have been closed. Both the Constitutional Candidate and
Deferred Matter Registers remain at zero entries. The full evidence base
and its citations are in FEF-PAR-001 §§1–7; four neutral Founder Options
are presented in §8, none recommended.

## 4. Founder Options (Reproduced for Reference)

No option is recommended, ranked, or preferred. Each is reproduced
exactly as it appears in FEF-PAR-001 §8.

### Option A — Proceed to D7 unchanged

D7's own Charter-defined scope is unaffected by anything in this
review. D6 has already produced boundary language relevant to D7's
constitutional/operating-mechanism distinction, though it explicitly
declined to resolve it, and zero Constitutional Candidates currently
exist to seed D7's examination.

### Option B — Proceed to D7 following targeted programme refinement

FEF-PAR-001 §7 identifies specific, evidence-bounded candidates for
targeted refinement (the four-document synchronisation burden; the
RQ-to-GF chain convention; decision-recording format) without
specifying how, or whether, any should be changed.

### Option C — Pause D7 and conduct a Framework simplification initiative

FEF-PAR-001 §3.6, §3.8, and §7 together document a directly observed,
repeated administrative-synchronisation cost and a 370-file/44-decision
administrative ratio, both stated without further interpretation.

### Option D — Freeze FEF as an Operational Baseline and prioritise broader application across Founder projects before further Framework development

FEF-PAR-001 §6 documents that no governance activity in D1–D6 has ever
been operated across more than one Founder project, and that every
cross-project conclusion reached in D6 is bounded by that same absence
of evidence.
