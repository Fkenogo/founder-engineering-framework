## 1. Purpose

Provide a comparative strategic view of the eight Charter domains for later Review Plan preparation.

Expected session counts are indicative effort ranges only. They are not session assignments, identifiers, schedules, commitments, or authority to conduct a session.

## 2. Priority and Planning Scales

| Scale | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Critical | Minimum governance baseline would be unsafe or structurally incomplete without the domain |
| High | Material effect on governance reliability or downstream readiness |
| Medium | Required for coherent operation after higher-priority outcomes are known |
| Low | Deferrable without undermining the minimum baseline |
| Complexity — High | Multiple authority, evidence, or cross-domain relationships require careful decomposition |
| Complexity — Medium | Bounded subject with material cross-references |
| Risk — High | Poor treatment could invalidate authority, evidence, or downstream governance |
| Risk — Medium | Poor treatment would reduce consistency or usability but is less likely to invalidate the baseline alone |

|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|

## 4. Indicative Effort Interpretation

The ranges indicate relative examination effort only:

- they do not establish a total session count;
- a future Review Plan may combine or divide domain examination;
- no domain may be omitted merely to fit the ranges;
- D8 mapping should operate across the review even if final synthesis is bounded;
- iteration may return a later-domain issue to an earlier domain without renumbering or scheduling authority from this Matrix;
- the approved Review Plan must justify material variance.

## 5. Priority Observations

1. D1 and D2 are Critical entry foundations.
2. D7 is Critical but dependency-late; its priority does not justify premature examination.
3. D3–D5 carry High risk because assurance, records, and lifecycle ambiguity can undermine otherwise sound decisions.
4. D6 is Medium because administration should implement established governance needs.
5. D8 is High and cross-cutting; final disposition depends on all substantive domains.
6. No domain is Low because all eight are required by the Charter.

## 6. Matrix Non-Effects

This Matrix does not:

- assign a review identifier;
- create a Review Plan;
- create or schedule a session;
- instantiate a Review Question or Governance Issue;
- create a GF, FD, or Constitutional Candidate;
- dispose an Open Question;
- authorise review execution.
