# FEF Draft Foundational Principles

**Framework status:** Draft — Not Yet Adoptable  
**Status:** Exploratory Draft — Non-Authoritative

1. Governance before engineering.
2. Product before technology.
3. Evidence before opinion.
4. Authority must be explicit.
5. AI is a governed collaborator, never the approval authority.
6. Knowledge is an engineering asset.
7. Every governed implementation should leave verifiable evidence.
8. Historical records preserve what happened but do not become current authority.
9. Projects adopt framework versions; they do not silently inherit changing rules.
10. Deviations are permitted only through explicit governance.
11. Improvements that benefit multiple projects should be evaluated for inclusion in FEF.
12. Framework change must not silently alter adopted project obligations.
13. Product-specific constitutions remain product-specific.
14. FEF should reduce repeated work without creating unnecessary bureaucracy.
15. A project is not administratively complete until its decisions, implementation, validation, and history are sufficiently preserved.

These principles require governed review and approval before constitutional use. No approval is recorded.
