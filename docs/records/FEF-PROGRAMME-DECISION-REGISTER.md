# FEF Programme Decision Register

**Register identifier:** FEF-PDP-001
**Register name:** Programme Decision Register
**Pilot classification:** Programme Governance Pilot Artefact
**Status:** Current pilot register
**Authority boundary:** Programme-management decisions only
**Standard effect:** None — this register is not a governance standard

## 1. Purpose

This register preserves decisions that govern administration and execution of the FEF programme. It provides traceability for programme sequence, work-package policy, dashboard policy, programme control, dependencies, milestones, and pilot operation.

Recording a decision does not expand its authority. Each entry is limited to its cited source and recorded effect.

## 2. Inclusion Boundary

The register may contain decisions concerning:

- programme sequencing;
- work-package administration and control;
- dashboard and reporting policy;
- dependency and milestone control;
- programme health and validation;
- programme change control;
- operation and evaluation of the Programme Governance pilot.

## 3. Explicit Exclusions

This register must not be used to decide or approve:

- constitutional decisions or constitutional authority;
- Framework Governance principles or standards;
- research or engineering standards;
- product or project decisions;
- technical engineering methods;
- standard approval, activation, adoption, or effective dates.

Cross-boundary dependencies may be referenced, but their substantive decisions must remain in the appropriate governed record.

## 6. Change Control

An entry may be added only when supported by a cited authorised source. Corrections must preserve the previous wording or treatment in repository history. Programme decisions must not be merged with constitutional, framework-governance, engineering, research-standard, or product decisions.
