# FEF-FRCD-001 — D1 Governance Authority Preparation Package

**Status:** Prepared Operational Outline — Review Not Commenced  
**Domain:** D1 — Governance Authority  
**Domain priority:** Critical  
**Review Questions created:** 0  
**Evidence assembled:** 0  
**Sessions created:** 0

## 1. Purpose

Prepare the operational boundary for examining D1 — Governance Authority after the Founder authorises commencement and all entry dependencies pass.

This package does not examine D1, create Review Questions, collect or admit evidence, assemble an Evidence Pack, or schedule a session.

## 2. Intended Objectives

When validly opened, D1 should enable the review to determine:

- the source and limits of Founder authority within FEF;
- the relationship between Founder authority and any future constitutional authority;
- the conditions and limits of delegated governance;
- matters reserved to the Founder;
- transitional authority before constitutional controls exist;
- the separation of governance authority, review authority, execution responsibility, and validation responsibility;
- how authority claims must be evidenced, attributed, and traced;
- which authority questions must be resolved before dependent domains proceed.

These are intended objectives, not findings or decisions.

## 3. Expected Review Question Themes

Future Review Questions may address themes including:

- Founder authority source and scope;
- reserved authority;
- delegation creation, limits, accountability, expiry, and withdrawal;
- constitutional authority boundary;
- transitional governance authority;
- approval versus activation authority;
- authority for exceptions and risk acceptance;
- review commissioning and commencement authority;
- separation of execution, validation, recommendation, and decision roles;
- authority conflicts and escalation;
- evidence required to substantiate an authority claim;
- applicability of decisions across FEF artefact classes.

These themes are planning categories only. They are not Review Questions and carry no identifier, wording, admission, or disposition.

## 4. Expected Evidence Classes

The D1 evidence search may consider the following classes after commencement:

| Evidence Class | Intended Relevance | Current Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Attributable Founder decision records | Direct evidence of reserved or exercised Founder authority | Identify prospectively; not assembled |
| Approved governance instruments | Evidence of existing review controls and authority boundaries | Known governing class; not packaged here |
| Programme decision and architectural review records | Evidence of prior authority exercise within bounded contexts | Candidate class only |
| Controlled standards and draft standards | Evidence of authority assumptions requiring review | Candidate class only |
| Registers and lifecycle records | Evidence of how authority is currently represented | Candidate class only |
| Validation and review reports | Evidence of assurance roles and gate treatment | Candidate class only |
| Repository history and provenance | Evidence of document evolution and attribution | Candidate class only |
| External duties or controlling obligations | Evidence of authority constraints where applicable | Unidentified; admissibility required |
| Explicit evidence gaps and conflicting records | Evidence of uncertainty or unresolved authority | Must be preserved, not inferred |

Listing an evidence class does not admit a source or establish its weight.

## 7. D1 Completion Criteria

D1 may later be considered complete only when:

- every admitted D1 RQ has a validated disposition or explicit controlled deferral;
- every material claim is traceable to admissible evidence or an explicit evidence gap;
- all D1 Governance Findings have passed their lifecycle and validation;
- all Founder decisions arising from D1 are attributable and linked;
- constitutional candidates, if any, are separately identified without premature extraction;
- affected Open Questions have controlled dispositions without silent closure;
- conflicts, minority interpretations, assumptions, and uncertainty are preserved;
- downstream dependency impacts are recorded;
- D1 completion validation passes;
- the Founder confirms the domain disposition where required by the Charter and Plan.

## 8. Validation Expectations

Future D1 validation should confirm:

- authority conclusions remain within D1 scope;
- evidence provenance and admissibility are complete;
- RQ → evidence → GF → FD traceability is intact;
- Founder-only authority is not delegated by implication;
- execution roles are not treated as decision authority;
- historical evidence gaps are not reconstructed;
- FEF-FGR-001 is used only as evidence of the historical gap;
- no constitutional wording is adopted through a GF or candidate;
- no Open Question is closed without its authorised disposition;
- no downstream standard is amended during D1 examination.

## 9. Risks and Controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Premature constitutional drafting | Keep constitutional extraction outside D1 examination until validated decisions exist |
| Founder intent inferred from programme history | Require attributable evidence or record an evidence gap |
| Role descriptions create authority | Separate descriptive execution responsibility from granted authority |
| D1 expands into all governance domains | Enforce admitted RQ scope and defer dependent matters |
| Existing drafts are treated as approved authority | Verify document status and attributable approval evidence |
| Historical review content is reconstructed | Preserve FEF-FGR-001 boundary and prohibit inference |
| Decision fatigue | Sequence RQs and group evidence without merging distinct decisions |
