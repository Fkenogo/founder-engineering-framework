# FEF-FRCD-001 — Initial Review Question Preparation Authority

**Status:** Prepared Conditional Authority — Not Effective  
**Authority trigger:** Founder approval of FEF-FRCD-001  
**Review Questions drafted:** 0  
**Review Questions admitted:** 0

## 1. Purpose

Define the limited authority and controls for drafting the first Review Questions after review commencement.

This document does not currently grant effective drafting authority and does not instantiate or draft a Review Question.

## 2. Conditional Authority

After all conditions in Section 3 are satisfied, authorised review administration may:

- prepare candidate Review Question wording for D1 — Governance Authority;
- map candidate RQs to D1 themes, source Open Questions, intended evidence classes, and decision needs;
- submit candidate RQs for validation;
- admit only those candidate RQs that pass the applicable controls.

This preparation authority is administrative and analytical. It does not grant Founder decision authority, permit answers to be predetermined, or permit an RQ to bypass admission.

## 3. Conditions Precedent

RQ drafting may begin only after:

1. the Founder approves FEF-FRCD-001;
2. the approval is recorded;
3. the review identifier is assigned;
4. the Review Question Register is instantiated and validated;
5. FEF-RQS-001 is confirmed as the applicable specification;
6. D1 is confirmed as the first domain;
7. the drafting role and validator are recorded;
8. no applicable stop condition exists.

## 4. Preparation Rules

Every candidate RQ must:

- use the assigned review identifier and canonical RQ schema;
- express one decision-relevant governance question;
- use neutral wording;
- state its primary domain;
- identify its source or trigger;
- identify any related existing Open Question without modifying it;
- define scope and exclusions;
- identify the decision need;
- identify expected evidence classes without treating them as admitted;
- preserve uncertainty and competing interpretations;
- avoid embedding a conclusion, recommendation, GF, FD, or constitutional clause;
- remain individually traceable.

Candidate RQs must not be merged merely to reduce count when they require distinct Founder decisions or evidence bases.

## 5. Admission Rules

A candidate RQ may be admitted only when:

- its identifier is unique and schema-conformant;
- all mandatory fields are complete;
- it is within D1 scope;
- its wording is answerable through evidence and Founder review;
- its source and decision need are explicit;
- dependencies and conflicts are recorded;
- it does not duplicate an admitted RQ without a controlled relationship;
- it does not silently close or rewrite an Open Question;
- required pre-admission validation passes;
- admission authority is recorded.

Admission places an RQ within review scope. It does not answer the RQ or approve any resulting governance.

## 6. Validation Rules

Validation must occur at the stages required by [FEF-RQS-001](../../../governance/reviews/FEF-RQS-001-REVIEW-QUESTION-SPECIFICATION.md):

### 6.1 Candidate Validation

- canonical structure;
- neutral wording;
- domain fit;
- source traceability;
- non-duplication;
- decision relevance;
- no hidden conclusion.

### 6.2 Pre-Admission Validation

- unique identifier;
- mandatory fields complete;
- scope and exclusions clear;
- evidence needs identified;
- dependencies recorded;
- Open Question relationship preserved;
- admission authority identified.

### 6.3 Pre-Session Validation

- admitted lifecycle state;
- evidence links resolve;
- applicable Evidence Pack is frozen;
- conflicts and evidence gaps are visible;
- session scope matches the RQ.

### 6.4 Post-Session Validation

- discussion and evidence references are preserved;
- any proposed GF is separately controlled;
- the RQ disposition is explicit;
- deferral or reopening is justified;
- no Founder decision is inferred.

## 7. Traceability Minimum

The future RQ chain must support:

```text
Source / Open Question
        ↓
Review Question
        ↓
Evidence Records
        ↓
Evidence Pack
        ↓
Session Record
        ↓
Governance Finding
        ↓
Founder Decision
        ↓
Constitutional Candidate or Deferred Matter
```

Later nodes may be absent, but missing links must never be invented.

## 8. Stop and Escalation Conditions

RQ preparation must stop or escalate when:

- commencement approval is absent or ambiguous;
- the review identifier is unassigned;
- a required register is unavailable;
- wording requires an unevidenced Founder-intent assumption;
- the question crosses the approved review scope;
- a material duplicate or conflict cannot be resolved procedurally;
- a constitutional conclusion is embedded in the question;
- an Open Question would be silently changed;
- independence or validation roles are unresolved.

## 9. Current Non-Effects

This prepared authority:

- is not yet effective;
- creates no RQ identifier;
- drafts no RQ;
- admits no RQ;
- changes no Open Question;
- creates no evidence, GF, FD, or Constitutional Candidate.
