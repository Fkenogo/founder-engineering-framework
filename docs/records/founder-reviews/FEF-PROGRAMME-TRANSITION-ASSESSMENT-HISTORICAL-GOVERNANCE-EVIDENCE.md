## 4. Required Sequencing if Option C Is Approved

At a high level, the conditional sequence would be:

1. record an attributable Founder selection of Option C;
2. update the Master Programme to reflect that decision before authorising or starting new work;
3. establish a separately authorised and bounded Founder Constitutional Review;
4. conduct the review without importing unsupported historical GF or FD content;
5. record and validate new Governance Findings and Founder Decisions contemporaneously;
6. confirm that every required Open Question is mapped without treating mapping as automatic closure;
7. reassess and authorise constitutional consolidation;
8. retain RGS amendment as a separate downstream activity;
9. retain Engineering Discovery behind its applicable approved standards.

No step in this sequence is authorised by this assessment.

## 5. Dependency Treatment

### 5.1 Historical Dependency

The unavailable Session 1–6 source package should cease to be a forward dependency only if the Founder explicitly approves Option C. Its absence must remain visible in FEF-FGR-001, WPK-001B.5 records, and the Recovery Report.

### 5.2 New Dependency

The new forward dependency would be a completed, attributable, and validated Founder review record. This assessment does not assign that review an identifier, work-package structure, owner, dates, or approval state.

### 5.3 Downstream Protection

FEF-WPK-001C, FEF-WPK-001D, FEF-RGS-000, FEF-RDS-001, and Engineering Discovery must retain their separate authority and lifecycle boundaries. Approval of Option C would authorise a transition decision only unless the Founder’s decision expressly includes additional bounded authority.

## 7. Programme Transition Recommendation

If the Founder approves Option C, apply only the minimum programme changes needed to:

- record the Founder’s attributable decision;
- retire the unrecoverable historical source package as a forward dependency;

Until that decision is recorded, the current programme state remains unchanged.
