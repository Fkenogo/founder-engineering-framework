## 1. Decision Purpose

This instrument records the single attributable Founder decision that commenced the new Founder Governance Review.

The decision authorises controlled mobilisation. It does not represent the review as already in session and does not waive any operational entry control.

## 2. Authority Basis

The decision is prepared under:

- [FEF-FGRC-001 — Founder Governance Review Charter](../../governance/reviews/FEF-FGRC-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-CHARTER.md);
- [FEF-FGRA-001 — Founder Governance Review Agenda](../../governance/reviews/FEF-FGRA-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-AGENDA.md);
- [FEF-FGRP-001 — Founder Governance Review Plan](../../governance/reviews/FEF-FGRP-001-FOUNDER-GOVERNANCE-REVIEW-PLAN.md);
- FD-2026-07-24-006, which reserves commencement to a distinct attributable Founder decision;
- FD-2026-07-24-008, which establishes D1 — Governance Authority as the first domain.

The Charter, Agenda, and Plan are Founder approved. Their approval did not commence the review.

## 3. Founder Decision

### 3.1 Commencement Authorisation

The Founder authorises commencement of the new Founder Governance Review in accordance with the governing instruments identified in Section 5.

Commencement is attributable to the approval recorded in Section 12. No earlier drafting, preparation, approval, milestone, or programme transition is treated as commencement.

### 3.2 Commencement Authority

The Founder is the sole commencement authority.

Approval of this instrument exercises that reserved authority. Operational preparation and review administration may proceed within the approved Charter, Agenda, Plan, specifications, templates, and execution rules. This does not delegate Founder decision authority or constitutional authority.

### 3.3 Commencement Scope

The decision authorises only:

1. assignment of the review identifier proposed through the controlled identifier-assignment procedure;
2. instantiation of the minimum execution registers defined by the approved instantiation plan;
3. controlled drafting and admission processing of Review Questions;
4. controlled identification, registration, and preparation of admissible evidence;
5. preparation and freezing of Evidence Packs;
6. preparation and controlled creation of review sessions;
7. examination of D1 — Governance Authority as the first domain;
8. creation of review outputs only through their approved lifecycle and authority gates;
9. validation, reopening, escalation, and completion activity governed by FEF-FGRP-001 and FEF-FGRER-001.

The decision grants no authority outside this scope.

## 4. First Review Domain

The first review domain shall be:

> **D1 — Governance Authority**

D1 may be prepared and examined only after assignment of the review identifier, instantiation of required registers, satisfaction of the applicable entry gates, and validation of the first admitted Review Questions and Evidence Pack.

Subsequent domains shall follow the approved dependency sequence unless a later attributable Founder decision authorises a controlled deviation.

## 6. Commencement Conditions

The approving Founder disposition does not waive operational entry controls. Before substantive review examination begins:

- the approved review identifier must be assigned and collision-checked;
- required registers must be instantiated and validated;
- the D1 preparation package must be confirmed against the approved instruments;
- Review Questions must be drafted, validated, and admitted;
- relevant evidence must be registered and pass admissibility review;
- the first Evidence Pack must be frozen and integrity-verified;
- the session entry gate must pass;
- unresolved blocking dependencies must be escalated.

## 7. Review Non-Effects

This decision does not:

- predetermine any Review Question;
- admit any Review Question automatically;
- deem any evidence admissible;
- approve an Evidence Pack;
- create a Governance Finding;
- create or predetermine a review-scoped Founder Decision;
- create or adopt a Constitutional Candidate;
- close or modify an Open Question;
- amend, approve, activate, or supersede the Constitution;
- amend, approve, activate, or supersede FEF-RGS-000;
- reconstruct FEF-FGR-001;
- authorise Engineering Discovery;
- waive any validation, evidence, traceability, quality, or approval gate;
- authorise work outside the Founder Governance Review.
