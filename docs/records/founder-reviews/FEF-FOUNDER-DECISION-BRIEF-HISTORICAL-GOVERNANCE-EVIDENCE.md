# FEF Founder Decision Brief — Historical Governance Evidence

**Programme:** Founder Engineering Framework  
**Decision subject:** Transition after unrecoverable historical governance evidence  
**Preparation date:** 2026-07-24  
**Document status:** Founder Decision Brief — Founder Review Required  
**Decision status:** No Founder decision recorded  
**Authority effect:** None — this brief presents options and does not authorise programme or governance change  
**Package type:** Decision package, not an implementation package

## 1. Purpose

Present the Founder with a controlled decision on how the FEF programme should proceed after the evidence required to complete FEF-WPK-001B.5 could not be recovered.

This brief:

- records the established evidence position;
- explains the governance and programme consequences;
- presents three available options;
- recommends an option for Founder consideration;
- does not make, imply, or implement the Founder’s decision.

## 2. Background

FEF-WPK-001B.5 was authorised to preserve the completed Founder Governance Review as an authoritative repository record. Its intended record, FEF-FGR-001, was expected to contain:

- six Founder Governance Review sessions;
- GF-001–GF-036;
- FD-001–FD-033;
- decision rationales and relationships;
- constitutional observations;
- transitional decisions;
- deferred matters;
- Open Question references.

The repository did not contain the source transcripts, exports, or equivalent attributable records required to populate those fields. In accordance with the work-package evidence boundary, FEF-FGR-001 was retained as an evidence-gap record and no missing content was reconstructed.

## 6. Why the Dependency Is Considered Unrecoverable

The dependency is considered unrecoverable for programme-decision purposes because:

1. both the repository/Git audit and the separately authorised external recovery are complete;
2. every identified and accessible evidence source tied to the programme was examined;
3. the only FEF-specific external archive located predates the review;
4. session histories contain later instructions and evidence-gap discussion, not the missing review record;
5. no source supports any substantive GF or FD wording;
6. no source provides Founder-approval provenance for a Session 1–6 package;
7. repeating an unsupported reconstruction would violate the governing evidence boundary;
8. the Founder has directed that recovery is finished and no further searches are to be attempted.

“Unrecoverable” does not mean the historical review did not occur. It means the evidence required to make that review an attributable, authoritative governance baseline cannot be recovered from the completed search.

## 7. Governance Implications

- The historical Founder Governance Review cannot be used as authoritative evidence for GF-001–GF-036 or FD-001–FD-033.
- FEF-FGR-001 must remain a historical evidence-gap record unless an attributable source is independently supplied later.
- The missing decisions cannot be treated as constitutional principles, governance rules, approvals, or accepted risk.
- The 23 FEF-RGS-000 Open Questions remain open.
- FEF-RGS-000 remains Draft v0.2 and is not amended, approved, activated, adopted, or made authoritative by this decision package.
- A fresh authoritative baseline requires a new attributable Founder review rather than reconstruction of the lost review.

## 8. Programme Implications

- FEF-WPK-001B.5 cannot reach its original acceptance criteria from recovered historical evidence.
- FEF-WPK-001B.5A is complete and should remain preserved as the recovery record.
- FEF-WPK-001C cannot execute against the missing GF/FD source set.
- FEF-WPK-001D remains downstream and cannot commence.
- Engineering Asset Discovery remains downstream of approved research and discovery governance.
- The Master Programme requires a Founder decision before the critical path can be changed.

Detailed conditional effects are recorded in the [Programme Transition Assessment](FEF-PROGRAMME-TRANSITION-ASSESSMENT-HISTORICAL-GOVERNANCE-EVIDENCE.md).

## 9. Founder Options

### Option A — Continue Treating FEF-WPK-001B.5 as Permanently Blocked

Under this option:

- FEF-WPK-001B.5 remains blocked indefinitely;
- FEF-FGR-001 remains an evidence-gap record;
- FEF-WPK-001C and FEF-WPK-001D remain blocked or pending behind the missing evidence;
- the historical dependency remains on the critical path;
- no replacement governance baseline is created.

**Advantages**

- imposes no new review effort;
- preserves the strictest possible treatment of the missing evidence;
- introduces no programme redesign.

**Consequences**

- the constitutional consolidation path cannot proceed;
- RGS amendment and later discovery remain delayed;
- the programme retains a dependency that has no identified means of satisfaction;
- programme status remains accurate but operationally static.

### Option B — Authorise Another Evidence Recovery Exercise

Under this option:

- the Founder authorises a further bounded recovery exercise;
- the new search must identify genuinely new sources or access routes;
- FEF-WPK-001B.5 remains blocked while that exercise runs;
- no GF or FD content may be inferred if the additional search also fails.

**Advantages**

- allows investigation of a specifically identified source that was unavailable during WPK-001B.5A;
- may recover original evidence if the Founder can name a new custodian, transcript, export, or service.

**Consequences**

- repeats recovery after the identified and accessible sources have already been exhausted;
- has a low evidence-based likelihood of success unless a new source is identified first;
- consumes programme time without establishing a new governance baseline;
- requires separate Founder authorisation and a clearly bounded search.

This option should not be interpreted as authority to resume searching under the present decision package.

### Option C — Recognise the Historical Event, Retire the Dependency, and Authorise a Fresh Review

Under this option, the Founder would:

1. recognise that the historical Founder Governance Review occurred;
2. recognise that its authoritative evidence is permanently unrecoverable for current programme purposes;
3. preserve FEF-FGR-001 as a historical evidence-gap record;
4. retire the missing historical evidence as a forward programme dependency;
5. authorise a new Founder Constitutional Review to establish a fresh, attributable governance baseline;
6. require the new review’s findings, decisions, rationales, and evidence to be recorded contemporaneously;
7. allow constitutional consolidation to proceed only from the new authoritative record.

**Advantages**

- preserves the historical truth without pretending the missing content is known;
- produces a complete audit trail from loss, through recovery, to replacement authority;
- separates historical evidence failure from future governance creation;
- creates a verifiable source for later constitutional consolidation;
- allows the programme to move forward without weakening provenance requirements.

**Consequences**

- requires new Founder review effort;
- the new review may reach conclusions different from the unrecoverable historical review;
- programme dependencies and statuses would require explicit later updates;
- no downstream work may use the new baseline until the review is completed, recorded, and validated.

## 10. Recommended Option

**Option C is recommended for Founder consideration.**

It provides the strongest audit trail because it preserves all three truths separately:

1. a historical review occurred;
2. its authoritative content cannot be recovered;
3. future governance will derive only from a new attributable review.

This approach avoids both indefinite programme paralysis and unsupported reconstruction. It does not erase FEF-FGR-001, reinterpret the historical review, or convert the recovery report into governance authority.

The recommendation is advisory only. No Option C effect arises unless the Founder expressly selects and authorises it.
