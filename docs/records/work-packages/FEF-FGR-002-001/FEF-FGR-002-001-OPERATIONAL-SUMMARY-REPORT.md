## 3. Authority Structure

```text
Operational Administration
        ↓ prepares and controls
Governance Analysis / Recommendation
        ↓ supplies candidate inputs
Validation Authority
        ↓ confirms integrity and readiness
Founder Decision Authority
        ↓ issues attributable decisions
Constitutional Authority
        ↓ remains outside this operational work
```

No downward or upward arrow transfers authority between layers.

## 4. Independence Model

The model requires separation where practicable between:

- RQ preparation and validation;
- Evidence Pack preparation and validation;
- GF preparation and validation;
- session/FD recording and record validation;
- candidate extraction and candidate validation.

Multiple-role operation is permitted only with disclosure, capacity labelling, conflict assessment, compensating controls, escalation, and preserved audit history.
