# FEF-FGR-002-004 — Founder Readiness Assessment

## 1. Assessment Purpose

Determine whether GF-001 through GF-008 are ready for Founder disposition and whether evidence, maturity, or assurance gaps require treatment first.

This assessment evaluates readiness. It does not select a disposition.

## 2. Readiness Criteria

Each finding was assessed for:

- exact validated text;
- traceability to an admitted RQ and Frozen-pack evidence;
- supporting and contrary evidence;
- assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations;
- affected OQs and dependencies;
- decision question clarity;
- non-independent validation impact;
- likely value of additional evidence.

## 4. Evidence Sufficiency Conclusion

All eight findings have sufficient admitted evidence for the Founder to select a permitted finding disposition within the exact scope of each GF.

Additional evidence is not necessary merely to:

- Accept;
- Accept with Conditions;
- Require Independent Revalidation;
- Reject; or
- Defer a finding.

“Return for Additional Evidence” remains available where the Founder wants evidence beyond the finding’s current bounded scope.

## 5. Finding Maturity Conclusion

All eight findings are sufficiently structured and mature for Founder review because each contains:

- a validated exact finding;
- mapped evidence and RQ;
- contrary evidence;
- uncertainty and limitations;
- affected OQs;
- recommendation separated from the finding;
- a neutral decision brief.

Maturity for disposition is not maturity for constitutional extraction, standard amendment, or implementation.

## 6. Independent Revalidation Assessment

Independent revalidation should be considered because the same Codex agent prepared and validated the findings.

This consideration is most material for:

1. **GF-006**, which directly evaluates the non-independent operating model;
2. **GF-001 and GF-003**, if they will support authority or constitutional decisions;
3. **GF-002, GF-007, and GF-008**, if their review-specific patterns will be generalised;
4. the full set, if later used for Constitutional Candidate assessment.

This assessment does not recommend that revalidation is mandatory. The Founder retains the permitted options of requiring it, accepting with conditions, or applying another disposition.

No finding requires automatic return for more evidence before the Founder may exercise judgement.

## 9. Non-Effects
