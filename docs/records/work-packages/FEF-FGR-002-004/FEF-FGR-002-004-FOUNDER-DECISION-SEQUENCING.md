# FEF-FGR-002-004 — Founder Decision Sequencing

## 1. Purpose

Recommend the order in which future Founder dispositions and any later substantive Founder Decisions should be considered.

This is sequencing advice only. It does not recommend a disposition or draft decision wording.

## 2. Recommended Review and Disposition Order

| Order | Finding | Sequencing Reason |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | GF-006 — Functional Separation and Non-Independent Validation | Establishes the assurance treatment that may condition reliance on every other finding. |
| 2 | GF-001 — Present Authority Source and Boundary | Establishes the current authority baseline for all substantive authority questions. |
| 3 | GF-003 — Current Constitutional Boundary | Separates current Founder-authorised operation from future constitutional authority. |
| 4 | GF-005 — Transitional Authority | Depends on the current source and future boundary; frames continuity before detailed models. |
| 5 | GF-002 — Reserved Founder Authority Core | Establishes which matters remain reserved before delegation, risk, and lifecycle design. |
| 6 | GF-004 — Assignment Is Not Delegation | Depends on the authority source and reserved-authority boundary. |
| 7 | GF-008 — Exception and Risk Authority | Depends on reserved authority and assurance treatment; informs conditional gates. |
| 8 | GF-007 — Lifecycle Transition Authority | Integrates source, boundary, transition, reservation, and risk treatment into later lifecycle design. |

GF-006 is placed first for reliance control, not because it has greater substantive authority.

## 3. Dependency Chains

### 3.1 Assurance Chain

`GF-006 → reliance treatment for GF-001 through GF-008`

### 3.2 Authority and Constitutional Chain

`GF-001 → GF-003 → GF-005`

### 3.3 Reserved and Delegated Authority Chain

`GF-001 + GF-003 → GF-002 → GF-004`

### 3.4 Risk and Lifecycle Chain

`GF-002 + GF-006 → GF-008 → GF-007`

GF-007 also depends on GF-005 because transition and effective treatment interact.

## 4. Prerequisite Decisions

| Later Subject | Prerequisite Founder Treatment |
|---|---|
| Reliance on the finding set | GF-006 assurance limitation and any revalidation condition |
| Complete reserved-authority model | GF-001 current source and GF-003 constitutional boundary |
| Delegation model | GF-002 reserved-authority treatment |
| Transitional governance model | GF-001 and GF-003 treatment |
| Exception and risk model | GF-002 and GF-006 treatment |
| Lifecycle-authority model | GF-001, GF-002, GF-003, GF-005, and GF-008 treatment |
| Constitutional Candidate assessment | Relevant GF disposition plus review-scoped FD where required |

## 5. Opportunities for Coordinated Consideration

The Founder may consider the following findings in the same review window:

- **GF-001 and GF-003:** common authority-source and constitutional-boundary context;
- **GF-002, GF-004, and GF-008:** reserved, delegated, and risk authority cluster;
- **GF-005 and GF-007:** transitional and lifecycle cluster;
- **GF-006 with every cluster:** assurance condition.

Coordinated consideration does not require combined dispositions or combined FDs.

## 6. Decisions That Must Remain Separate

| Separate Subjects | Reason |
|---|---|
| GF disposition versus substantive governance decision | Accepting a finding for decision input is not adopting the governance rule described. |
| Present authority source versus future constitutional hierarchy | GF-001 and GF-003 have different effects and evidence limits. |
| Reserved authority versus delegation | GF-002 identifies reservation; GF-004 addresses transfer and assignment boundaries. |
| Independence treatment versus risk acceptance | GF-006 records assurance; GF-008 addresses who may accept or waive risk. |
| Transitional authority versus universal lifecycle | GF-005 concerns pre-constitutional continuity; GF-007 concerns artefact transitions. |
| Constitutional Candidate designation versus constitutional consolidation | Candidate assessment and later constitutional work have separate authority gates. |

## 7. Sequencing Stop Conditions

Pause a dependent disposition when:

- a prerequisite finding is returned, rejected, or deferred;
- GF-006 requires independent revalidation before reliance;
- new evidence materially changes a source or limitation;
- a proposed disposition would create constitutional or standard effect;
- exact Founder judgement cannot be attributed.

## 8. Non-Effects

The sequence does not draft, number, issue, combine, or approve an FD. The Founder may later alter the order through attributable direction.
