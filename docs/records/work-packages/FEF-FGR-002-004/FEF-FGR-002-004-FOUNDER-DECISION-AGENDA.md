# FEF-FGR-002-004 — Founder Decision Agenda

## 1. Agenda Purpose

Provide a concise agenda for later Founder disposition of GF-001 through GF-008.

No agenda item records a decision.

## 2. Agenda Entry Conditions

Before disposition:

- use the exact GF version 1.0 text;
- preserve the Frozen-pack evidence references;
- disclose the non-independent validation condition;
- distinguish GF disposition from a substantive FD;
- leave OQ, candidate, Constitution, and RGS status unchanged until proper authority acts.

## 4. Agenda Outputs

For each item, later Founder review should produce exactly one attributable treatment:

- Accept;
- Accept with Conditions;
- Return for Additional Evidence;
- Require Independent Revalidation;
- Reject;
- Defer.

Any substantive governance directive beyond finding disposition must be separately identified, scoped, and recorded as a review-scoped FD under FEF-FGR-002-005.

## 5. Founder-Only Decision Fields

No Founder-only fields are completed in this agenda.

The later disposition record must capture exact Founder wording, conditions, rationale or absence of rationale, evidence/GF links, non-effects, and lifecycle treatment.

## 6. Non-Effects

This agenda does not select an option, record an FD, disposition a GF, close an OQ, create a candidate, or authorise downstream work.
