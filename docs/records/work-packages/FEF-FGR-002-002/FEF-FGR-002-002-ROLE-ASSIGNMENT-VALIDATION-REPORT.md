## 4. Independence Assessment

The Codex coding agent holds five operational roles. This is a high-risk combination under FEF-FGR-002-ICR-001 because the same individual prepares and validates initial artefacts.

Compensating controls are:

- capacity labels on each action;
- preparation and validation performed as separate sequential passes;
- validation limited to objective structure, source identity, hashes, counts, links, lifecycle, and boundary checks;
- exact source files remain preserved;
- all evidence and RQ mappings remain inspectable;
- no RQ is answered;
- no governance conclusion, FD, or constitutional candidate is created;
- the limitation is repeated in the pack validation and readiness reports;
- the Founder may require independent revalidation before or at Session 1 entry.

The validation is not represented as independent.

## 5. Founder Authority

Founder authority remains solely with the Founder.

No assignment permits the Codex coding agent to:

- issue or amend an FD;
- accept reserved risk;
- close an Open Question;
- create constitutional effect;
- approve or amend FEF-RGS-000;
- authorise Engineering Discovery.

## 6. Condition

Every pre-session artefact produced under the combined Codex assignments must:

1. identify the preparation capacity;
2. identify the separate Validator capacity;
3. disclose that validation is non-independent;
4. include deterministic integrity and reconciliation evidence;
5. remain open to independent revalidation if directed by the Founder.

## 7. Conclusion

All six assignments are effective and comply with the assignment procedure subject to the recorded non-independent validation condition.
