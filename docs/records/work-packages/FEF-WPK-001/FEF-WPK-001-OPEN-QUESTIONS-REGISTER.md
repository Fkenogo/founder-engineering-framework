## Closure Rule

An open question may be closed only by a cited decision or verified evidence from an authorised source. A drafting preference is not sufficient closure evidence.

The Technical Review amendments introduce proposed answers and terminology but do not close FEF-WPK-001-OQ-002 through OQ-004, OQ-009, OQ-014, or OQ-019 through OQ-023.

## FEF-FAR-001 Impact

FAR-001 confirms that Research Classification and Research Types remain within the research domain and that shared framework-pattern extraction is deferred. It does not resolve the detailed governance choices in FEF-WPK-001-OQ-019, OQ-020, OQ-022, or OQ-023. All 23 questions remain open.
