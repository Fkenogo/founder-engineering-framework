## 7. Founder Review Commencement Dependencies

The Master Programme should prevent review commencement until:

1. the Founder approves or conditionally approves FEF-FGRP-001;
2. Plan conditions are resolved or accepted;
3. the Master Programme reflects the approved operational path;
4. a separate Founder commencement decision is recorded;
5. the review identifier is assigned;
6. roles and controlled repositories are established;
7. empty register baselines are instantiated under the assigned identifier;
8. the 23-question Open Questions baseline is fingerprinted;
9. the first future RQs are prepared and validated;
10. the first future Evidence Pack is assembled, validated, and frozen;
11. entry gates pass.

FEF-WPK-001G satisfies none of the execution dependencies merely by producing specifications and templates.
